*Alejandro Rodriguez Dominguez*

Main category: q-fin.PM

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解市场中持续有符号订单流（即买卖订单的持续不平衡）的形成机制是市场微观结构研究的核心问题。现有文献缺乏统一的机制解释投资组合代表性变化（可能由成本驱动）如何产生这种持续性，以及如何在不同数据集之间进行推断和验证。

Method: 本文采用理论建模与结构化模拟相结合的方式：建立异质切换阈值与机会波动率的投资组合代表性更新模型；运用首次通过更新分析（first-passage renewal analysis）推导订单流协方差衰减的解析形式；通过结构化仿真从实现路径中验证理论预测，并考察权重失配与有限截面造成的推断偏差。

Conclusion: 论文提供了将聚合持续性拟合转换为跨数据集约束的实证协议，使其能够判断基于持续时间的核函数是否适用于独立的执行成本模型，从而为订单流持久性的理论验证与实证测试搭建桥梁。

Abstract: This paper develops a mechanism through which costly changes in the representations used for portfolio choice can contribute to persistent signed order flow. Heterogeneous switching thresholds and opportunity volatility generate heterogeneous residence times, and renewal aggregation maps their execution-weighted tail into the decay of aggregate flow covariance. Under common execution weights, the same tail determines the exponent of representation-spell durations, the order-flow memory exponent, and the horizon at which finite-market scaling must end. First-passage renewal analysis establishes these joint restrictions. Structural simulations recover them from realized paths, quantify the distortion created by mismatched weights, and show how finite cross sections shorten the usable inference horizon. The resulting empirical protocol converts an aggregate persistence fit into cross-dataset restrictions that can determine whether a duration-based kernel is suitable for a separate execution-cost model.

Conclusion: 金融结论在三个条件约束下成立：交叉冲击的识别、容量的归一化、以及行动时间跨度内输入的稳定性。该方法为金融稳定监管中的容量认证提供了统一的推断框架，并优于逐点推断方法在复杂谱根结构下的表现。

Abstract: This paper develops uniform inference and certified capacity decisions for an estimated financial stability boundary. Conditional risk, temporary cross-impact, and effective risk-bearing capacity are jointly estimated from dependent observations. Conventional pointwise inference is reliable at a separated simple spectral root but can fail near semisimple or defective collisions. Projecting a valid joint confidence region for the underlying inputs avoids this local approximation and yields a three-way regime decision with abstention and a one-sided capacity bound. A verified two-dimensional implementation keeps numerical error from creating a resolved sign. Structural simulations recover the predicted tradeoff between coverage and resolution, while observed-risk stresses distinguish statistical abstention from an insufficient computational budget. The financial conclusions remain conditional on the identification of cross-impact, the normalization of capacity, and stability of the inputs over the action horizon.

### [3] [Eliciting ESG Preferences for Reinforcement Learning-Based Portfolio Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02677)
*Giovanni Dispoto,Marcello Restelli,Carmine Ventre*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文提出一个多目标强化学习（MORL）框架，将三个不同ESG机构评级与财务回报同时纳入投资组合优化，并通过高斯过程偏好诱导系统，让投资管理者通过成对比较方式表达其潜在效用偏好。研究使用LLM人格模拟不同地域的投资经理，实证表明地域背景显著影响偏好权重（如欧式人格偏好ESG，德州风格偏好风险调整收益），从而实现了算法交易与人类可持续性偏好的对齐。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有RL投资组合方法通常只针对单一ESG提供商的评级进行优化，忽视了行业内评级方法论的显著分歧；同时，手动为相互冲突的目标设置权重不够直观且难以准确反映人类决策偏好。

Method: 将ESG意识型投资组合优化建模为MORL问题，同时纳入三个不同ESG机构的评级；集成基于高斯过程（Gaussian Processes）的偏好诱导框架，通过基于Sharpe比率和聚合ESG分数的成对比较来推断潜在效用函数；使用LLM人格模拟不同区域背景下（欧洲、德州等）的投资经理用户进行系统评估。

Result: 基于历史市场数据的实证结果表明，地域背景从根本上改变了推导出的偏好权重——例如欧洲人格优先考虑ESG一致性而非财务回报，而德州人格更偏好风险调整后的绩效表现。

Conclusion: 该工作提供了一个高度可适应的框架，成功地将多目标算法交易与现实中多样化的人类可持续发展偏好对齐，弥合了高维算法权衡与人类决策之间的鸿沟。

Abstract: Modern portfolio management increasingly demands a balance between traditional risk-adjusted returns and strict Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) mandates. Current Reinforcement Learning (RL) approaches typically optimize for a single ESG provider, neglecting the significant divergence in rating methodologies across the industry and the unintuitive nature of manually weighting conflicting objectives. This paper addresses these limitations by formulating ESG-aware portfolio optimization as a Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning (MORL) problem that simultaneously incorporates ratings from three distinct ESG agencies. To bridge the gap between high-dimensional algorithmic trade-offs and human decision-making, we integrate a Preference Elicitation framework using Gaussian Processes. This system enables practitioners to infer their latent utility functions through intuitive pairwise comparisons of candidate portfolios based on their Sharpe ratios and aggregate ESG scores. We systematically evaluate our framework by employing Large Language Model (LLM) personas to simulate Portfolio Managers operating under varied regional contexts. Empirical results using historical market data reveal that regional backgrounds fundamentally shift the derived preference weights. For instance, European-based personas tend to prioritize ESG alignment over financial returns, while Texas-based personas favor risk-adjusted performance. This work offers a highly adaptable framework that successfully aligns multi-objective algorithmic trading with diverse, real-world human sustainability preferences.

### [4] [What Would it Cost to End Extreme Poverty?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02013)
*Roshni Sahoo,Joshua Blumenstock,Paul Niehaus,Leo Selker,Stefan Wager*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究了通过直接转移支付减少贫困的问题，将其框架为统计学习问题，同时保留现实项目面临的信息约束。基于34个国家的全国代表性家庭消费调查（覆盖全球76%的贫困人口），估计将贫困率从13%降至1%每年需花费2110亿美元（名义），这是总贫困缺口缩减的4.0倍，但仅为全民基本收入成本的19%。全球外推表明约需全球GDP的0.28%来基本终结极端贫困。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者希望将扶贫的直接转移支付问题形式化为一个统计学习问题，在现实项目的信息约束下严格估计终结极端贫困的真实成本，为政策制定提供可靠依据。

Method: 利用34个国家的全国代表性家庭消费调查数据，将扶贫直接转移设计构建为统计学习问题，并纳入现实世界项目所面临的信息约束（如目标识别不确定性），据此估算各项成本指标。

Conclusion: 在全球信息约束下，通过直接转移支付将极端贫困率降至1%是可行的，其成本远低于全民基本收入，约占全球GDP的0.28%，为实现终结极端贫困提供了量化的政策参考。

Abstract: We study poverty minimization via direct transfers, framing this as a statistical learning problem while retaining the information constraints faced by real-world programs. Extrapolated globally, the results imply a cost of 0.28% of global GDP to (approximately) end extreme poverty.

### [5] [Dutch Books for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02797)
*Isaiah Andrews,Suproteem Sarkar*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文通过基于de Finetti定理的程序评估语言模型概率预测的一致性（coherence）。利用股票收益率数据生成事件，通过线性规划计算最大Dutch-book利润作为不一致性度量，无需结果标签即可评估。研究发现语言模型预测存在显著不一致性，逻辑关系越丰富不一致性越高，无关上下文细节可将不一致性提高一个数量级。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 人们越来越多地使用语言模型来支持人生决策，这些决策常涉及概率预测（如重大生活事件、自然灾害、经济结果的概率）。用户可能隐含地信任这些预测源自连贯的世界模型。然而，语言模型的概率预测是否具备概率论意义上的连贯性（即避免Dutch-book、满足de Finetti一致性公理）尚未得到充分验证，因此需要系统评估其预测的连贯性。

Method: 该方法构建于de Finetti定理之上：从股票收益率数据生成事件，向语言模型询问这些事件的概率预测；然后使用线性规划计算最大的Dutch-book利润——即套利者通过反对模型生成概率所能保证获得的利润——将其作为不一致性的度量。该方法的一个关键优势是无需结果标签，因此可以在结果未观测或尚未解决的情况下评估一致性。

Result: 研究发现语言模型的预测存在显著的不一致性证据。这种不一致性在事件之间存在更丰富的逻辑关系时增加，而无关的上下文细节可以使不一致性提高一个数量级。

Conclusion: 作者最后讨论了替代性训练策略可能如何改善语言模型的概率一致性。

Abstract: People increasingly use language models to support life decisions. Many such decisions involve a probabilistic forecast: How likely is a major life event, a natural disaster, or an economic outcome? Users of language models may implicitly trust that these forecasts fall out of a coherent world model. In this paper, we evaluate the coherence of language model probabilistic forecasts through a procedure that builds on a theorem due to de Finetti. We elicit forecasts from language models across events generated from stock returns data. We then use linear programs to compute the largest Dutch-book profit - the profit an arbitrageur could guarantee by betting against model-generated probabilities - which we use as a measure of incoherence. Our procedure does not require outcome labels, so we can evaluate coherence even in settings where outcomes are not observed or have not yet resolved. We find substantial evidence of incoherence in language model forecasts. Such incoherence increases when there are richer logical relationships between events, and irrelevant contextual details can increase incoherence by an order of magnitude. We conclude by discussing how alternative training strategies may improve probabilistic coherence.

</details>

### [6] [Price manipulation in nonlinear transient impact models: rigidity before memory and complete positivity after memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02447)
*Minhyeok Lee*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文系统分类了瞬时冲击模型的两种组合顺序（非线性力作用于交易速率之前 vs 可测读出作用于冲击状态之后），揭示了不同顺序下无价格操纵准则的根本差异，并推广到奇异核、Prony核及矩阵记忆等情形。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 瞬时冲击模型将非线性函数与记忆核组合，组合顺序决定了无价格操纵准则，但此前对两种顺序缺乏系统分类；早期刚性定理假设核在零点有界，无法处理奇异核，且Gatheral的著名界限δ+γ≥1遗留的开放区域问题需要解决。

Method: 提出零体积"chattering pump"论证配合两个薄基线交易来读出操纵信号，适用于奇异Volterra核；对可测读出作用于核之后的情形，将安全性等价于核的完全正性并给出构造性逆；引入"remote compensating block"方法比较往返安全与全输入安全；用这些机制对两模Prony核、一阶非时齐记忆和稳定全驱动矩阵记忆进行分类。

Result: (1) 当任意瞬时律f在积分前作用于交易速率，任意非零可积Volterra核下，有限分段常数往返的非负成本迫使f仿射，非零卷积核下迫使f线性；(2) 幂律冲击与幂律衰减组合仅在δ=1时无操纵，平方根冲击在所有衰减指数下可操纵，Gatheral开放区域收缩为线δ=1；(3) 可测读出在核之后时，全输入安全等价于完全正性，平方根冲击后接幂律记忆是无操纵的；(4) 往返安全与全输入安全对尾均匀消失的核一致，对永久记忆存在显式存储商差异；(5) 标定指数对全部落在可操纵集合中。

Conclusion: 无摩擦时，非线性组合在记忆核之前必然导致可操纵性（除非仿射/线性），因此凹性必须进入记忆之后而非之前；本文为奇异核提供了统一rigidity理论，填补了Gatheral界限的遗留空白，并建立了从幂律情形到一般记忆结构的分类框架。

Abstract: Transient impact models compose a nonlinearity with a memory kernel, and the order of composition determines the criterion for absence of price manipulation. We classify both orders. If an arbitrary instantaneous law $f$ acts on the trading rate before any nonzero integrable Volterra kernel, nonnegative cost on every finite piecewise-constant round trip forces $f$ to be affine, and linear for every nonzero convolution kernel. In particular, the power law $\mathrm{sgn}(x)|x|^δ$, $δ>0$, combined with power-law decay $t^{-γ}$, $0<γ<1$, admits manipulation if and only if $δ\ne1$: square-root impact is manipulable at every decay exponent, and the region left open by Gatheral's slow-rate two-block bound $δ+γ\ge1$ collapses to the line $δ=1$. Earlier rigidity theorems require a kernel that is bounded at zero; the argument here is a zero-volume chattering pump read out by two thin baseline trades, and it applies to singular kernels. If instead a monotone readout acts on the impact state after the kernel, safety for all inputs and all readouts is equivalent to complete positivity of the kernel, with a constructive converse; in particular, square-root impact after power-law memory is manipulation-free. A remote compensating block shows that round-trip safety and all-input safety coincide for kernels with uniformly vanishing tails and differ, for permanent memory, by an explicit storage quotient. These mechanisms classify every two-mode Prony kernel, first-order time-inhomogeneous memory, and stable fully actuated matrix memory, and they quantify the friction, the two-block phase, and the switching complexity behind the power-law case. Calibrated exponent pairs all lie in the manipulable set: absent friction, concavity has to enter after the memory, not before it.
