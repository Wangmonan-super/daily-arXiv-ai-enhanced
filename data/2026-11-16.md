### [1] [Who Should Be on the Panel? Task-Conditioned Persona Selection for LLM Trading Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18150)
*Boming Yang,Yuquan Wang,Jiaxian Guo,Yusuke Iwasawa,Yutaka Matsuo*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 提出任务条件的角色选择问题，并以FinPanel实例化：由"招聘者"依据股票的方向盲统计草图，为每只股票招募一组具名交易员人设，独立给出日度判断，再由经理结合层级战绩决定交易；在2025年八只美股上优于固定小组、三类已有LLM交易智能体及规则/预测/强化学习基线，且无需热身即保持表现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有多智能体LLM系统先固定角色阵容再设计协调机制，同一阵容复用于所有任务，角色选择的代价被掩盖；金融交易中股票特性各异，适用风格不同，使"谁入组"的选择成本显性化，因此需研究任务条件的人设选择，并将"谁在组内"置于"如何协调"之前。

Method: FinPanel：招聘者基于股票交易方式的方向盲统计草图，为每只股票挑选具名交易员人设组成面板；每位人设以自身风格独立给出日度判断；经理阅读论点及交易员自维护的层级战绩后执行交易；信息不回传给交易员，故所选面板保持不变，并可在零记录下交易新股票。

Result: 在2025年（知识外年份）八只美股上，所选面板优于同规模固定面板及按原论文实现的三种已有LLM交易智能体，也优于规则、预测与强化学习基线；每只股票回撤均浅于买入持有；无热身、完全无记录时性能保持，而记忆增强型智能体收益随热身波动；增加席位或经理对论点的解读均不能替代选对人。

Conclusion: "组内是谁"应是按任务、在协调方式之前做出的设计决策；任务条件的人设选择是可复用的多智能体LLM设计原则。

Abstract: Multi-agent LLM systems are built by fixing a cast of roles and then designing how the cast coordinates. Which agents should make up the group for a given task is left to the designer, and the same cast is reused for every task. Financial trading makes the cost of that choice visible: stocks trade differently, and the trading styles suited to one need not suit another. We study task-conditioned persona selection, composing the group from the characteristics of the task it will face, and instantiate it with FinPanel, an LLM multi-agent trading system in which a recruiter selects a panel of named trader personas for each stock from a direction-blind statistical sketch of how the stock trades, each persona gives an independent daily call in its own style, and a manager commits the trade after reading the panel's arguments and their desk-kept, hierarchical track records. Because nothing is returned to the traders, the panel stays the panel that was selected, and it can trade a new stock from an empty record. On eight US stocks over the out-of-knowledge year 2025, against three prior LLM trading agents re-implemented in the same framework and against rule-based, predictive and reinforcement-learning baselines, the selected panel is ahead of a fixed panel of the same size and of the prior agents run as their papers prescribe, with a shallower drawdown than buy-and-hold on every stock; it keeps its performance with no warm-up and with no record at all, whereas the memory-augmented agents' returns swing with their warm-up; and neither more seats nor the manager's reading of the arguments substitutes for selecting the right ones. Who is in the group is a design decision to be made per task, before how the group coordinates.

### [2] [Mapping NGFS Climate Pathways to GICS Sectors: Application to Portfolio Decarbonization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17942)
*Maxime Jourand,Adel Medjabri*

Main category: q-fin.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出一种基于范围1和2温室气体排放重要性（而非语义相似性）的NGFS-GICS行业映射，并结合行业脱碳方法（SDA）将NGFS情景轨迹降尺度为组合层面的GICS行业路径，经区域敞口调整后应用于两个示例性ETF指数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 组合气候对齐分析受限于资产管理者的行业分类与气候情景行业分类之间的不匹配，现有映射多依赖语义相似性，难以反映真实排放重要性。

Method: 构建以范围1、2排放重要性为依据的NGFS-GICS映射；将其嵌入行业脱碳方法（SDA），把NGFS情景轨迹降尺度为组合层面的GICS行业路径，并按区域敞口进行调整；对两个示例性ETF指数进行实证演示。

Result: 结果显示行业构成与区域轨迹共同塑造组合的融资排放路径；框架具有透明性、资产类别无关性。

Conclusion: 该框架可透明、跨资产类别地用于气候压力测试等内部练习。

Abstract: Portfolio climate alignment is limited by the mismatch between asset managers' sector classifications and those of climate scenarios. This article proposes an NGFS-GICS mapping based on the materiality of Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions rather than on semantic similarity. The mapping feeds a Sectoral Decarbonization Approach that down-scales NGFS trajectories into GICS sector pathways at portfolio level, adjusted for regional exposure. Applied to two illustrative ETF indices, it shows how sectoral composition and regional trajectories shape a portfolio's financed emissions pathway. The framework is transparent, asset-class agnostic and usable for internal exercises such as climate stress tests.

</details>

### [3] [AI and the Two Margins of Work: Why Job Losses Show Up Before Job Creation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17530)
*Mark Esposito,Liu Zhang*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 技术变革通过"在位企业内调整"与"新企业创造新活动"两个边际影响劳动需求，而工资册统计对前者记录及时、对后者记录滞后。作者构建含AI能力与创始人努力的模型，并用1978年后进入队列及至2026年一季度机构就业流动数据检验，提出"净复职比率"以纠正滞后统计对替代效应的多年高估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有研究多以工资册数据度量技术（含AI/自动化）对劳动需求的影响，但工资册对"新增活动"记录严重滞后，导致对替代（displacement）的系统性高估，需要从测量与理论两方面澄清。

Method: 1) 经验事实：使用工资册统计刻画1978年后进入队列的份额演变（1998年占42%，五年后留存约80%），并考察自动化暴露最高行业中在位者收缩与进入者累积的时序；2) 理论：构建模型，其中一项AI能力同时降低既有生产中"可接受产出"的全成本、使更多新项目值得尝试，创始人努力与雇佣工时相分离；区分运营产品"即时再定价/再扩张"与"存在时滞"两种情形；3) 检验：用至2026年一季度的机构层面就业流动，按语言模型暴露度检验进入与在位两个边际的梯度，并与"创始任务暴露"文献中进入上升10–20%的结果对照。

Result: 自动化暴露高行业呈现"在位者先收缩、进入者后累积"的序列，该序列在样本前期精确、末期仅为提示性。若运营产品可即时再扩张，自动化不减少总量工时；若存在时滞，工时先降后随在位者再扩张与新产品进入而回升，长期符号由收入效应决定。至2026年一季度机构就业流动在语言模型暴露上两个边际均无梯度——这既符合观测 adoption 路径下的模型预测，也与"工资册无效应"的观测难以区分。创始任务暴露研究则发现进入率上升10–20%。

Conclusion: 记录新工作滞后的统计会在数年内高估替代效应；作者提出"净复职比率"作为校正指标。当前数据尚不足以区分"模型预测的时滞期"与"真实无效应"，需依赖创始任务暴露等前瞻性度量。

Abstract: Technological change acts on labor demand at two margins: adjustment within established activities and the creation of additional activities, often by new firms. Payroll statistics record the first promptly and the second late. In the computing era, firms that entered in 1978 or later held 42 percent of payroll by 1998 and entering cohorts kept about 80 percent of their payroll after five years; in the industries most exposed to automation, incumbents contracted before entrants accumulated, a sequence that is precise early in the period and suggestive by its end. We develop a model in which one AI capability lowers the full cost of acceptable output in existing production and makes additional projects worth attempting, with founder effort separate from hired hours. If operating products re-scale instantly, automation displaces no aggregate hours; if they take time, hours fall first and recover as incumbents re-expand and new products arrive, with the long-run sign set by income effects. Establishment job flows through the first quarter of 2026 show no gradient on language-model exposure on either margin, which is what the model predicts under the observed adoption path and also what no effect on payroll would look like; studies with founding-task exposure find entry rising 10 to 20 percent. We propose a net reinstatement ratio and show that statistics which record new work late overstate displacement for years.

</details>


### [4] [Menu Design Meets Market Design: Strategic Simplicity vs. Choice Complexity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17984)
*Gian Caspari,Paul Ergün Hild,Michael Hilweg-Waldeck,Manshu Khanna,Vincent Meisner*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 在串行独裁机制的实验室实验中，策略简单并不等于偏好可知：约1/6的选择被逆转，近1/5涉及客观被占优的组合，而二分解选择结构并未改善，反而提高低能力被试的逆转率与过度自信。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有研究强调串行独裁等机制的策略简单性可消除误报偏好的激励，但忽略了另一前提：参与者必须能识别自身偏好。论文旨在区分"激励相容"与"偏好可知"两个问题，检验简化选择结构能否帮助被试识别偏好。

Method: 以真实努力任务组合为标的的串行独裁实验室实验；被试完成15次有金钱激励的选择，标的组合在包含任务数量上存在差异；选择通过两种架构实施：大菜单一次性选择 vs. 一系列二元选择（binary decomposition）；全部选择完成后，被试需意外地将每个已选分配与先前被拒绝的备选进行重新比较。

Result: 约六分之一次选择在事后重新比较中被逆转；近五分之一次选择涉及客观被占优组合（dominated bundle）；简化选择架构无显著帮助；二元分解反而提高低能力被试的逆转率，并进一步放大其对自己选择的信心（过度自信）。

Conclusion: 策略简单性不足以保证偏好识别；选择架构的简化（尤其二元分解）不能消除甚至可能加剧偏好错误与信心失配，提示机制设计与福利分析需同时关注偏好认知成本与信心校准。

Abstract: Strategic simplicity removes the incentive to misreport preferences, but it does not ensure that participants can identify what they prefer. We study this distinction in a laboratory experiment on serial dictatorships, where optimal play only requires choosing one's favorite bundle of real-effort tasks. Participants make fifteen incentivized choices among bundles that vary in how many tasks they combine. They choose either from a large menu or through a series of binary choices. Having made all choices, they unexpectedly reconsider each assignment against a previously rejected alternative. We find that roughly one in six choices is reversed and almost one in five involves an objectively dominated bundle. Simplifying the choice architecture does not help. Binary decomposition even raises reversal rates among low-ability participants and further inflates their confidence about their choices.

</details>
