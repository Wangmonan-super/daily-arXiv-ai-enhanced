*Reza Bradrania,Ya Gao*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 社会信任会放大彩票型股票的MAX效应（高估与低后续收益），且在教育/收入较低地区更强。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 理论逻辑：高社会信任降低投资者对极端收益相关乐观信息的怀疑，提升彩票型股票需求，导致高估与低后续收益。实证上使用1996–2018年美国49个州8,875家公司的样本，以MAX作为彩票型特征代理，按地区信任水平分组检验MAX效应，并按教育、收入水平做横截面调节分析，辅以替代彩票代理变量与多项稳健性检验。

Using a sample of 8,875 firms across 49 US states over the period 1996-2018 and MAX as a proxy for lottery-like characteristics, we show that the MAX effect is concentrated in regions with relatively high trust and is weak or absent in regions with relatively low trust. We further find that this trust conditioning is stronger in regions with lower education or income levels, consistent with social trust playing a relatively more important role when investors have more limited access to private information.
