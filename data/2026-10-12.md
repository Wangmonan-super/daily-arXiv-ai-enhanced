### [1] [MLP-Initialized Reinforcement Learning for Exit Timing in SPX Short-Straddle Portfolios](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13126)
*Jianning Wang,Robert Ślepaczuk,Paweł Sakowski*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 该论文采用两阶段监督学习与强化学习框架，研究SPX空头勒式组合的最优退出时机，结果表明监督式MLP显著优于被动持有及固定规则，而以MLP初始化的Actor-Critic经风险感知奖励微调后表现最佳。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 期权退出决策本质上是稀疏且路径依赖的问题，传统固定时间或盈亏规则难以适应，而纯强化学习从零训练样本效率低、稳定性差，因此需要一种可靠的初始化方式来提升策略微调效果。

Method: 第一阶段利用历史情景生成事后最优持有/退出标签，以当时可获得的状变量训练多层感知机（MLP）；第二阶段以训练好的MLP初始化强化学习Actor，并以风险感知的交易奖励进行微调，最后在2024—2026年样本外区间进行评测。

Result: 样本外评估显示，监督式MLP大幅优于被动持有及代表性固定时间、盈亏退出规则；在七个独立随机种子下，MLP初始化的Actor-Critic平均累计收益37.54%、夏普比率1.705，而监督式MLP为35.01%和1.651；从零训练的Actor-Critic平均收益仅0.18%。

Conclusion: 在稀疏且路径依赖的期权退出问题中，基于事后最优标签的监督学习可为基于奖励的策略微调提供稳定基础，显著提升强化学习的表现与稳定性。

Abstract: This paper studies exit timing for SPX short-straddle portfolios using a two-stage supervised and reinforcement-learning framework. Historical episodes first generate ex-post optimal hold/exit labels for training a multilayer perceptron (MLP) using only contemporaneously available state variables at deployment. The trained MLP then initializes reinforcement-learning actors that are fine-tuned with a risk-aware trading reward. Out-of-sample evaluation over 2024-2026 shows that the supervised MLP substantially outperforms passive holding and representative fixed-time and profit-and-loss-based exit rules. Across seven independent seeds, MLP-initialized Actor-Critic averages 37.54% cumulative return and a Sharpe ratio of 1.705, compared with 35.01% and 1.651 for the supervised MLP. Actor-Critic trained from scratch averages only 0.18% return. These findings show that oracle-guided supervised learning provides a stable foundation for reward-based policy fine-tuning in a sparse and path-dependent option-exit problem.

### [2] [FOMO: FLEX Options and Market Outcomes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13044)
*Shivesh Prakash,Frank Ferstler,Nicholas Pezolano*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: FLEX期权活动可观测地标记机构波动率需求与持仓布局，但并非股票方向性收益的预测因子。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FLEX期权允许参与者自选非标准行权价、到期日、行权方式与结算条款，同时保留中央清算。作者希望检验单一标的FLEX交易是否会在常规挂牌期权市场及标的股票上留下可度量的痕迹。

Method: 构建关联数据集，涵盖FLEX合并记录、每日未平仓合约文件、挂牌期权链、股票收益与公司行动；样本跨越250个交易日（2025年3月—2026年3月），覆盖41个标的与53个事件日。采用匹配对照组双重差分（DiD）及日期固定效应回归进行估计，并进行Benjamini-Hochberg多重检验校正。

Result: (1) 匹配对照DiD显示事件后挂牌期权未平仓合约显著上升（beta=0.3061，p=0.0042，BH校正p=0.0168）；(2) FLEX活动强烈选择进入高隐含波动率（IV）环境，集中于中价标的（日期固定效应ATM IV溢价 beta=0.1235，p=0.0030）；(3) 事件日IV减已实现波动率价差可预测执行压力下的短跨式收益（beta=0.0366，p=0.0100）；股票收益的短期效应（CAR_0差异-0.85%，p=0.0442）在1至5日窗口内不持续。

Conclusion: FLEX活动主要作为机构波动率需求与未平仓头寸布局的可观测标记，而非股票方向性收益的预测信号。

Abstract: FLEX options are customized exchange-traded contracts allowing participants to choose nonstandard strikes, expirations, exercise styles, and settlement terms while retaining central clearing. We investigate whether single-name FLEX activity leaves measurable footprints in regular listed-option markets and underlying equities. Using a linked dataset of FLEX consolidations, daily open-interest files, listed-option chains, equity returns, and corporate actions over 250 trading days (March 2025-March 2026) across 41 underlyings and 53 event dates, we document three primary empirical findings: (1) Matched-control difference-in-differences estimates reveal a robust post-event increase in listed-option open interest (beta = 0.3061, p = 0.0042, Benjamini-Hochberg p = 0.0168). (2) FLEX activity is strongly selected into high implied-volatility (IV) environments, concentrated in mid-price underlyings (date-fixed-effect ATM IV premium beta = 0.1235, p = 0.0030). (3) Event-day IV-minus-realized-volatility spreads predict execution-stressed short-straddle returns (beta = 0.0366, p = 0.0100). Short-lived equity return effects (CAR_0 difference of -0.85%, p = 0.0442) do not persist over 1-to-5-day horizons. Overall, FLEX activity serves primarily as an observable marker of institutional volatility demand and open-interest positioning rather than a directional stock-return predictor.

### [3] [Crisis without Collapse: A Theory of Capitalist Persistence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12477)
*Murat Ova*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该论文将"资本主义终结论"175年预测屡屡落空视为待解释的典型事实，整合适应性系统、机制设计、计算复杂性、行为决策理论、全局博弈与再分配政治经济学，提出包含10个假设、12个形式化结果与1个定理的统一模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自1848年以来，关于资本主义"终局危机"的预测反复出现却从未实现。作者将这一长期预测失败现象作为需要理论建模的典型事实，试图解释为何崩溃预言持续失败但仍不断被提出，并统一多个学科对系统性崩溃、革命窗口与再分配的分析。

Method: 构建整合性形式化模型：结合适应性系统、机制设计、计算复杂性、行为决策理论、全局博弈与再分配政治经济学，通过若干假设推导形式化结果；以冲击类别的崩溃风险几何衰减、价格机制集中替代的"计算或真实输入"双角困境、累积前景理论推导戴维斯J曲线、个性化媒体对全局博弈均衡的影响，以及可支配基尼对市场基尼的调节机制为分析工具。

Result: 模型给出12个形式化结果与1个定理：冲击类别具有几何衰减的崩溃风险，银行类冲击中从系统性失败到全系统存款担保的时滞由1930-33年的816天降至2008年的18天和2023年的2天；集中化替代价格机制至少在计算或真实输入其中之一失败；由累积前景理论推导出戴维斯J曲线，并得出革命窗口内生关闭且长度与享乐适应速度成反比，校准参数下约4-9年，其可感知阈值由参与约束导出而非外生假设；个性化媒体将抗议博弈推向全局博弈的唯一无攻击均衡；再分配是调节旋钮而非固定属性，市场基尼0.505-0.512的国家可维持可支配基尼0.273-0.395。

Conclusion: 论文解释了资本主义终局危机预测长期落空的原因，提出统一的理论框架，并给出7个可检验预测与6个证伪条件，同时指明各竞争学派若要否定该模型必须放弃的具体假设。

Abstract: The terminal crisis of capitalism has been predicted regularly since 1848. It has not occurred. We treat this 175-year forecasting record as a stylized fact in need of a model, and we supply one, unifying results from adaptive systems, mechanism design and computational complexity, behavioral decision theory, global games, and the political economy of redistribution. Ten assumptions deliver twelve formal results and one theorem. Recurring shock classes have geometrically decaying collapse hazards: within the banking class alone, the lag from systemic failure to a system-wide deposit backstop fell from 816 days (1930-33) to 18 days (2008) to 2 days (2023). A centralized replacement of the price mechanism fails on at least one of two horns, computation or truthful input, and we are explicit about which horn survives modern algorithms. We derive Davies's J-curve from cumulative prospect theory together with a new result: the revolutionary window closes endogenously, with length inversely proportional to the speed of hedonic adaptation, roughly four to nine years at calibrated parameters, and with its perceptibility threshold derived from the participation constraint rather than assumed. Personalized media shift protest games, in the model, into the unique no-attack equilibrium of a global game. Redistribution is a dial, not an identity: countries with market Ginis of 0.505-0.512 sustain disposable Ginis from 0.273 to 0.395. We state seven testable predictions and six falsification conditions, and map which assumption each rival school must deny.

### [4] [On the Optimality of Mental Accounting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12587)
*Jarrod Burgh,Emerson Melo,Matthew Shum*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出心理账户的第三种解释：它是消费者对抗卖方市场势力的承诺机制，与既有的自我控制解释并行。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 心理账户通常被归因于自我控制，两阶段预算则被归因于偏好可分离性。作者希望给出一个不依赖偏好结构的新解释，即从卖方市场势力出发理解这些行为。

Method: 构建理论模型：若消费者承诺在某类别支出固定金额，其需求为单位弹性，卖方限制数量无利可图；若消费者锁定固定实物数量，需求完全无弹性，垄断者可加以利用。刻画能消除卖方加价激励的承诺集合，并比较预算刚性与偏好可分离性的作用。

Result: 固定金额预算是限制性最小的承诺；起决定作用的是预算刚性而非偏好可分离性。价格变化时重新优化类别预算的消费者，只有在类别互为替代品时才能免受市场势力影响；而刚性预算在任何情形下都能使其免受影响。

Conclusion: 心理账户可被解读为对抗加价的承诺装置，这一逻辑独立于文献中既有的自我控制动机。

Abstract: Mental accounting is usually explained by self-control, and two-stage budgeting by separable preferences. We propose a third explanation based on sellers' market power. A consumer who commits to spend a fixed number of dollars in a category has unit-elastic demand, so a seller in that category gains nothing by restricting quantity. A consumer who instead targets a fixed physical quantity has perfectly inelastic demand, a case that a monopolist can exploit. We characterize the commitments that remove a seller's incentive to mark up prices, and show that the fixed dollar budget is the least restrictive. We then show that what matters is budget rigidity rather than separability of preferences. A consumer who re-optimizes her category budget when prices change is insulated from market power only if categories are substitutes; a rigid budget insulates her in every case. Mental accounting can therefore be read as a commitment device against markups, a rationale that is separate from the self-control motive in the existing literature.

</details>


### [5] [Linear Demand Systems are Consistent with Discrete Choice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13785)
*Afonso Rodrigues*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文证明聚合线性需求系统可由异质性消费者的离散选择微观基础推导，并可纳入收入效应；提出仅用市场层面数据的结构估计方法，在保留线性需求解析与计算优势的同时达到BLP模型的丰富性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 聚合线性需求系统虽具解析与计算优势，但缺乏微观基础，且难以纳入收入效应；而BLP等离散选择模型虽丰富，却需外生设定市场规模、计算成本高。作者希望弥合二者，兼得线性需求的便利与离散选择的丰富性。

Method: 通过异质性消费者的离散选择构建聚合线性需求的微观基础，并扩展至含收入效应的情形；仅利用市场层面数据对模型进行结构估计，借助聚合项处理角点解，从而恢复消费者偏好分布。

Conclusion: 离散选择的微观基础便于福利分析、加成与成本传导的灵活测度，在广泛应用中兼具BLP的丰富性与线性需求的解析和计算优势。

Abstract: Aggregate linear demand systems can be micro-founded by a population of heterogeneous consumers making discrete choices. They can also be extended to incorporate income effects. I prove these results and show how to structurally estimate this class of models using only market-level data. The distribution of consumer preferences is recovered by disciplining the corner solutions with aggregate terms only. This approach allows for the same richness of Berry, Levinsohn, and Pakes (1995) [BLP] while retaining the analytical and computational advantages of linear demand: it does not require exogenously fixing market size, allows for heterogeneity in choice sets, and results in a flexible substitution matrix which varies endogenously as different consumers enter/exit the market. The discrete-choice microfoundation facilitates welfare analysis and the flexible measurement of markups an cost pass-throughs, among a wide range of other applications.
