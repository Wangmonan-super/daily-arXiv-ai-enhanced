Motivation: 双向拍卖机制推动价格趋向竞争均衡的趋势在实验室实验中被广泛记录，但长期缺乏理论解释，本文旨在填补这一理论空白。

Method: 构建纯交换经济下的动态双向拍卖模型，假定代理人依据其当前持有量与偏好所隐含的无差异价格进行报价，并将瓦尔拉斯均衡刻画为双向拍卖映射的不动点。

Result: 证明瓦尔拉斯均衡与双向拍卖的不动点一致；重复双向拍卖生成有界的配置序列与价格序列，其聚点为带转移的瓦尔拉斯均衡。

Conclusion: 该结果为双向拍卖价格收敛于竞争均衡的实验规律提供了理论依据，表明竞争均衡是双向拍卖动态的稳定极限点（允许转移情形下）。

Abstract: The tendency of the double auction mechanism to drive prices to competitive equilibrium has been well documented in laboratory experiments, but the phenomenon has lacked a theoretical explanation. This paper studies dynamic double auctions in a pure exchange economy where agents bid their indifference prices implied by their current holdings and preferences. We show that Walras equilibria coincide with the fixed points of the double auction and that repeated double auctions generate bounded sequences of allocations and prices whose cluster points are Walras equilibria with transfers.
