### [1] [Legal aid eligibility and court outcomes: a design-based double-machine-learning approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05211)
*Fabio Italo Martinenghi*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文利用澳大利亚新南威尔士州将法律援助与法院结果相关联的新行政数据集，结合双重机器学习（DML）方法，研究拒绝法律援助对法院判决结果的影响。研究发现，未通过经济状况审查而聘请私人律师的申请人，其被监禁的可能性比通过审查并依靠法律援助的人低10个百分点；但若被监禁，他们服刑时间可能更长。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 法律面前人人平等是一项基本人权，为贫困被告提供高质量的法律援助对于执行这一权利至关重要。在所有人都有律师的情况下，作者希望识别拒绝法律援助对法院结果（尤其是监禁判决）的因果影响，以评估法律援助政策的效果。

Method: 作者结合双重机器学习（Double Machine Learning）方法与新构建的行政数据集（将法律援助申请与法院结果联系起来，数据来自澳大利亚新南威尔士州），利用已知输入学习分配函数（assignment function），从而估计拒绝法律援助的因果效应。通过DML方法处理选择偏差和高维协变量，比较未通过经济状况审查（means test）而聘请私人律师的被告与通过审查并获得法律援助的被告之间的结果差异。

Result: 研究发现：未通过经济状况审查并聘请私人律师的申请人，被监禁的可能性比通过审查并依赖法律援助的申请人低10个百分点。考虑到平均监禁时长接近四年，这一差距是显著的。但与此同时，有证据表明如果被监禁，前者在监狱中度过的时间可能更长。

Conclusion: 政府若偏好扩大法律援助的覆盖面而非增加每案分配的时间，可能解释上述模式（即法律援助律师更可能争取无罪/较轻判决从而降低监禁率，但一旦被定罪，私人律师可能在量刑阶段取得不同结果）。这一发现对法律援助资源配置政策具有启示意义。

Abstract: Equality before the law is a human right, and access to high-quality legal aid for indigent defendants is essential to enforce it. In a context where all defendants have access to a lawyer, I study the impact of denying legal aid on court outcomes. I combine double machine learning and a new administrative dataset linking aid to court outcomes in New South Wales, Australia, to learn the assignment function, whose inputs are known. I find that applicants who fail the means test and hire private lawyers are 10 percentage points less likely to be incarcerated than if they passed and relied on legal aid. Given an average incarceration length of nearly four years, this gap is significant. However, I find evidence suggesting that they spend more time in jail if they are incarcerated. A government preference for broad access to aid over allocated time per case could explain this pattern.
  Keywords: Indigent Defense, Crime, Criminal Justice.
  JEL: I30, K14, H44.

### [2] [Large-Market Discipline in Combinatorial Double Auctions: No Assembly, Bundle Selection, and Complementarities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06134)
*Konstantinos E. Zachariadis,Yongxin Yang*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究了在商品以捆绑形式具有价值（如数据、模型权重、微调AI资产）的市场中的双向拍卖。核心制约是"不可组装"（No Assembly）：平台因法律或技术原因无法将不同卖家的组件组合成单一捆绑。作者在这种约束下构建了组合式买方出价双向拍卖，并在显式稳定性与价格影响条件下证明各捆绑子市场继承了单商品双向拍卖的大市场纪律：出价抑制（bid shading）消失，清算价格集中于竞争水平并追踪共同价值（价格发现）。关键激励步骤（捆绑出价不产生超出单商品逻辑的一阶战略扭曲）对两种商品成立，更大商品集上作为维持条件。多智能体强化学习模拟将福利损失分解，表明在有限市场摩擦中"不可组装"而非战略出价抑制是主要约束，两种损失在中度稠密市场中都很小，且随商品互补性增强而下降。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 许多现代市场（数据市场、AI模型资产等）中的商品以捆绑形式才有价值，但平台可能因法律或技术限制无法将不同卖家的组件组装成单一捆绑。这种"不可组装"摩擦给组合式双向拍卖的机制设计带来独特挑战，现有单商品双向拍卖理论无法直接适用，因此需要研究该约束下的市场机制与激励性质。

Method: 作者形式化了在No Assembly约束下的组合式买方出价双向拍卖。在一般情形和两商品情形（由局部价格接受和反馈界导出）下，维持显式的稳定性与价格影响条件进行分析。关键激励步骤（捆绑出价无额外一阶战略扭曲）对两商品情形给出了证明；更大商品集作为维持条件。同时采用多智能体强化学习模拟来分解福利损失。

Result: 证明每个捆绑子市场在大市场条件下继承单商品双向拍卖的纪律：bid shading消失，清算价格集中于竞争水平并追踪共同价值。两商品情形的关键激励步骤得证。强化学习模拟表明No Assembly而非战略shading是有限市场中绑定的摩擦，两种损失在适度稠密市场上均较小，并随商品互补性增强而下降。

Conclusion: 在No Assembly约束下，组合式双向拍卖在足够稠密的市场中仍能保持近似竞争性的价格发现与效率；有限市场的福利损失主要源于结构性摩擦（No Assembly）而非策略性行为，且互补性越强损失越小，理论结果对更大商品集的可扩展性仍需进一步研究。

Abstract: We study double auctions for markets in which goods are valuable in bundles, such as data, model weights, and fine-tuned AI assets. A key friction in such markets is No Assembly: a platform may be unable, for legal or technical reasons, to combine components supplied by different sellers into a single bundle. We formulate a combinatorial buyer's-bid double auction under this constraint. Under explicit stability and price-influence conditions (maintained in general, and for two goods derived from local price-taking and a feedback bound), each bundle submarket inherits the large-market discipline of single-good double auctions: bid shading vanishes, and clearing prices concentrate on competitive levels and track the common value (price discovery). The key incentive step, that bidding on a bundle creates no first-order strategic distortion beyond the single-good logic, is proved for two goods; for larger item sets it remains a maintained condition. Multi-agent reinforcement-learning simulations decompose the welfare loss and indicate that No Assembly, not strategic shading, is the binding finite-market friction, with both losses small in moderately thick markets and declining with complementarity amongst goods.

### [3] [Velocity- and Regime-Aware Detection of Intraday Options Market Manipulation, with Explainable Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05373)
*Alex Chen,Maria Hybinette*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文发现日内市场操纵会留下"泵-崩"动态特征，可通过市场状态的速度而非水平来检测。基于平滑状态速度构建分钟级检测流水线，在印度BANKNIFTY指数期权测试集上10/10召回监管确认的操纵日；该动态特征可迁移至美股，但精度受未标注正常标签限制仅约25%。SHAP归因表明未确认警报与确认日共享归因特征（余弦相似度0.99），精度上限源于不完整的执法标注而非检测器失效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 日内市场操纵难以检测：其足迹短暂、深埋于海量报价中、统计上与正常波动相似。现有检测器高召回意味着大量误报，精度崩塌，产生监管无法行动的警报。作者旨在提出一种更具判别力的动态信号特征来克服这一困境。

Method: 构建分钟级检测流水线，严格按时间分区训练/测试，基于平滑化市场状态速度（期权Delta速度与股票价格速度）。使用SHAP精确归因解释每个警报；通过HMM推断市场制度/状态作为条件协变量；在锁定波幅外测试集上使用自编码器检测，并以泵-反转形状评分（pump-reversal shape score）跨市场评估。

Result: 在锁定的印度BANKNIFTY指数期权测试集上，自编码器恢复10/10个监管识别操纵日。HMM制度条件化产生负面结果：权衡了召回和精度（闭合世界假设下精度仅约25%）。该特征迁移至美股（SEC v. Patel），形状保留但幅度不保留；泵-反转评分对ARQQ和ACY的AUC分别达0.91和0.81，且ARQQ评分峰落在诉状文件记录的分钟窗口内。SHAP归因显示未确认警报与确认日归因相似度0.99。

Conclusion: 跨市场、跨工具类型转移的是"动态签名"本身。精度上限与不完整的执法标签一致而非检测器失效，表明该动态特征可作为通用日内操纵检测的稳健核心信号。HMM制度调节在此设定下无益，反而牺牲精度换召回。

Abstract: Intraday market manipulation is hard to detect because its footprint is brief, buried in millions of quotes, and statistically similar to ordinary volatility. Detectors reach high recall only by flagging so many other days that measured precision collapses, producing alerts no regulator can act on. We show that this manipulation leaves a distinctive dynamic signature: a pump-and-crash pattern visible in the velocity of market state, rather than its level.
  We build a minute-level detection pipeline, strictly partitioned in time, based on smoothed state velocity: option-Delta velocity for index options and price velocity for equities. We explain every alert with SHAP attribution. We hold the test period strictly out-of-sample and fix all thresholds before evaluation. On the locked Indian BANKNIFTY index-options test, the plain autoencoder recovers 10 of 10 regulator-identified manipulation days.
  Conditioning detection on market regimes inferred by a hidden Markov model yields an instructive negative result. The regimes are descriptively distinct, but using them trades recall for precision. Under the closed-world assumption that unlabeled days are normal, precision remains near 25%.
  The same dynamic appears in thinly traded U.S. equities (SEC v. Patel). The shape of the signature survives the transfer; its velocity magnitude does not. A pump-reversal shape score ranks the complaint's alleged manipulation days with AUC 0.91 (ARQQ) and 0.81 (ACY). On the ARQQ worked example, the score peaks inside the complaint's documented minute window.
  Finally, exact SHAP attribution over every alert shows that unconfirmed alerts share the regulator-identified days' attribution profile (cosine similarity 0.99). The precision ceiling is consistent with incomplete enforcement labels rather than detector failure. What transfers across markets and instrument types is the dynamic signature itself.

</details>
