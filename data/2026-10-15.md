### [1] [Creative destruction: Human-AI competition in an experimental market](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18921)
*John Conlon,Peter Schwardmann*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 通过三项研究(N=1,025)的游戏设计实验，发现人类在生产中的角色并不因品味优势或消费者偏好而持续：AI独立设计的游戏优于人类（含专业创意者），人类未预料到该优势，且披露人类设计身份也不提升消费者支付意愿。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着AI能力提升，人类在生产中的角色能否持续存在？论文提出两种假设：一是人类在"品味"上具持久优势（即便AI代理自动化常规任务，人类仍能设计更好产品）；二是消费者内在偏好人类参与生产。研究旨在检验这两大假设是否成立。

Method: 实验设计：参与者分别扮演生产者与消费者。生产者使用AI编码代理设计电脑游戏；消费者购买这些游戏。共三项研究，总样本N=1,025。比较AI独立设计、业余人类、专业创意者设计的游戏质量；考察人类生产者对AI优势的预期；并操纵是否披露游戏为人类设计，测量消费者支付意愿。

Result: 未发现支持任一假设的证据：(1) AI独立设计的游戏表现优于人类生产者（包括业余者与专业创意者）；(2) 人类生产者并未预料到AI的这一优势；(3) 披露游戏由人类设计并未提升消费者的支付意愿。

Conclusion: 人类在生产中的持续角色无法由"品味优势"或"消费者人类偏好"解释。随着AI能力增长，人类在创意生产中的比较优势可能被削弱，且市场（消费者）并不为人类身份支付溢价，提示相关劳动力市场与政策预期需重新审视。

Abstract: We study two hypotheses for how a human role in production could persist despite growing AI capabilities. Humans might have a durable advantage in taste, designing better products even as AI agents automate routine tasks, or consumers might intrinsically prefer that humans have a hand in production. In our experiment, participants act as producers, designing computer games with an AI coding agent, and as consumers who buy them. Across three studies (N = 1,025), we find no evidence for either hypothesis. Games designed by an AI on its own outperform those of human producers, including both amateurs and professional creatives. Human producers do not anticipate the AI's advantage. Revealing whether a game is human-designed does not raise consumers' willingness to pay for the product.

### [2] [Long Term Pediatric Vaccine Tender Scheduling with Quantity Discounts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19045)
*Nicholas Uhorchak,Ruben Proano,Sandra Eksioglu,Burak Eksioglu*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文扩展HCVM框架，以灵活期限和全量数量折扣调度多年疫苗招标，比较UG、SB、MP三类目标，发现采购目标而非需求波动主导成本与调度复杂度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 中低收入国家的儿科疫苗采购需跨异质市场、多利益相关方与不确定需求进行长期协调，但现行招标仍按一系列独立续约安排，缺乏协调调度方法。

Method: 扩展假设协调疫苗市场（HCVM）框架，实现具有灵活招标期限与全量数量折扣的多年招标调度；构建并比较三种目标模型：平衡型协调主体模型（UG）、最小化漏种剂次的社会效益模型（SB）、生产者利润最大化模型（MP）。采用UNICEF十年需求预测，在六种折扣情景下进行30次需求重复仿真。

Result: 采购目标而非需求波动决定成本-招标关系与调度复杂度；数量折扣利于大型生产商，导致市场集中并增加供应链脆弱性；折扣有效性取决于市场成熟度而非价数：五价和六价疫苗已接近价格下限，单价疫苗中仅轮状病毒对折扣有响应，HPV和PCV仍因价格过高而缺乏竞争力；将五价和六价疫苗移出采购池会显著增加漏种剂次。

Conclusion: 研究为规划者提供了关于目标选择、折扣设计和保障联合疫苗可及性的实践指导。

Abstract: Pediatric vaccine procurement for low- and lower-middle-income countries (LICs and LMICs) requires long-term coordination across heterogeneous vaccine markets, competing stakeholder objectives, and uncertain demand, yet tenders are currently scheduled as a series of independent renewals. We extend the Hypothetically Coordinated Vaccine Market (HCVM) framework to schedule multi-year tenders with flexible lengths and all-unit quantity discounts, and compare three objectives: a balanced coordinatingentity model (UG), a social-benefit model that minimizes missed doses (SB), and a producer profit-maximizing model (MP). Using UNICEF ten-year demand forecasts and 30 demand replications across six discount scenarios, we find that the procurement objective, not demand variability, drives cost-to-tender and schedule complexity. Quantity discounts favor large producers, concentrating the market and increasing supply chain vulnerability. Discount effectiveness depends on market maturity rather than valency: pentavalent and hexavalent vaccines are already near their price floor, and among monovalent vaccines only rotavirus responds to discounts, whereas HPV and PCV remain too expensive to compete. Removing pentavalent and hexavalent vaccines from the procurement pool sharply increases missed doses. These results give planners practical guidance on objective selection, discount design, and protecting access to combination vaccines.

### [3] [Which Text Matters: Handling Model Uncertainty in Text-as-Data Regressions, with an Application to Central Bank Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19720)
*Martin Feldkircher,Bernd Prostmaier,Paul Hofmarcher,Pierre Siklos*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 论文提出spike-and-slab广义可加模型（SSGAMs），将文本协变量选择与函数形式（线性/非线性）判定统一纳入模型不确定性框架，并用欧洲央行224场月度记者会（2002年末—2025年中）的45种竞争性文本指标来追踪专业通胀预期，结果显示仅稀疏少数指标有效，常用替代指标及所有非线性效应均无增量信息。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 社会科学中常见的两阶段设计先由文本构造主题份额、理想点、情感得分等协变量，再放入统计模型，但缺乏理论指导：研究者并不清楚该构造或纳入哪些文本协变量。每种方案都包含可争议的测量选择，同一构念的不同操作化可能导致不同结论，单个研究问题因而衍生出一系列互相竞争的回归模型。因此，“哪些文本协变量应进入模型、以何种函数形式进入”本质上是一个模型不确定性问题。

Method: 提出spike-and-slab广义可加模型（SSGAMs）。该模型通过spike-and-slab先验实现变量选择（spike成分收缩至零），同时借助广义可加模型的平滑项判定协变量效应为线性还是非线性，从而联合完成协变量选择与函数形式识别。实证部分以某强势非选举机构（欧洲央行）的沟通为对象，从2002年末至2025年中224场月度记者会中提取45种竞争性文本指标，用以追踪专业通胀预期。

Result: 模型选出的是稀疏的文本指标子集；被广泛使用的替代指标以及所有受检验的非线性效应均未提供增量信息。

Conclusion: SSGAMs为文本协变量的选择与函数形式判定提供了统一的、以模型不确定性为导向的框架，能有效避免因测量选择任意性而导致的结论分歧，并支持构建更简约、更稳健的文本驱动统计模型。

Abstract: Social scientists routinely convert text into covariates like topic shares, ideal points or sentiment scores, and insert them into statistical models of interest. This two-step design is convenient, but it is rarely guided by theory: little tells the analyst which text-based covariates to construct or include. Each approach embodies contestable measurement choices, and competing operationalizations of the same construct can support different conclusions, so a single research question gives rise to a menu of competing regression models. Which text constructed covariates belong in a model, and in what functional form, is thus a problem of model uncertainty. We propose spike-and-slab generalized additive models (SSGAMs), which jointly select covariates and determine whether their effects are linear or nonlinear. We illustrate the approach with the communication of a powerful unelected institution, asking which of 45 competing text indicators from 224 monthly European Central Bank press conferences, spanning late 2002 to mid-2025, track professional inflation expectations. A sparse set is selected; widely used alternatives, and every tested nonlinear effect, carry no incremental information.

</details>
