### [1] [The Gendered Impacts of Perceived Skin Tone: Evidence from African American Siblings in 1870-1940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10606)
*Ran Abramitzky,Jacob Conway,Roy Mill,Luke C. D. Stein*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 利用19世纪末至20世纪初美国人口普查全计数数据，通过兄弟姐妹内部对比，发现非裔美国人的肤色感知差异对女性教育、婚姻及丈夫收入有显著影响，而对男性同期结果影响较小。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有研究已知非裔美国人内部存在按感知肤色的经济结果差异，但难以区分这种差异是来自家庭层面的机会与资源差别，还是来自针对个体的同期歧视。作者希望厘清肤色差距的驱动机制。

Method: 使用19世纪末至20世纪初美国十年一次人口普查的全计数数据；比较普查员标记为"Black"或"Mulatto"的儿童，并将儿童跨普查链接。首先记录教育与收入差距，再识别35,992个同性别兄弟姐妹被赋予不同Black/Mulatto分类的家庭，进行家庭内兄弟姐妹对比。

Result: 家庭内对比显示，被标为Black的姐妹相对被标为Mulatto的姐妹受教育程度更低、结婚可能性更低、丈夫受教育程度更低且收入可能更低；而不同分类的兄弟在教育、婚姻与收入上结果相近。总体人群中肤色较深的非裔男性收入更低，主要源于跨家庭的机会与资源差异。

Conclusion: 针对非裔女性的同期歧视随感知肤色加深而更严重；非裔男性无论感知肤色如何都面临相似的同期歧视，符合"一滴血"种族分类规则。非裔男性总体收入差距则由家庭层面机会与资源差异驱动，可能反映历史或家庭层面的歧视影响。

Abstract: We study differences in economic outcomes by perceived skin tone among African Americans using full-count U.S. decennial census data from the late-19th and early-20th centuries. Comparing children coded as "Black" or "Mulatto" by census enumerators and linking these children across population censuses, we first document large gaps in educational attainment and income among African Americans with darker and lighter perceived skin tones. To disentangle the drivers of these gaps, we identify all 35,992 families in which enumerators assigned same-gender siblings different Black/Mulatto classifications. Relative to sisters coded as Mulatto, sisters coded as Black had lower educational attainment, were less likely to marry, and had less-educated, likely lower-earning husbands. These patterns are consistent with more severe contemporaneous discrimination against African American women with darker perceived skin tones. In contrast, we find similar educational attainment, marital outcomes, and incomes among differently-classified brothers. Men perceived as African Americans of any skin tone faced similar contemporaneous discrimination, consistent with the "one-drop" racial classification rule that grouped together individuals with any known Black ancestry. Lower incomes for African American men perceived as having darker skin tone in the general population were driven by differences in opportunities and resources that varied across families, likely reflecting the impacts of historical or family-level discrimination.

### [2] [Risk Ceilings and Development Deadlines: Pacing AI under Uncertain Safety Productivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11093)
*Li Gan*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究在安全生产力未知时，监管者能否同时承诺风险上限与开发截止期，并给出实现条件与成本。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 监管者希望同时承诺“风险上限”和“开发截止期”两项约束，但当安全技术在研发过程中的产出效率（安全生产力）未知时，二者可能相互冲突，需要厘清何时能同时成立及代价。

Method: 理论建模：将风险上限设为低于最终模型“无保护风险”，推导其需要最小安全知识存量，从而时间与风险双承诺成立须排除弱研究；提出“先学习、再重放开发（replaying development）”的策略，并在安全上保留闲置算力（spare compute）；在一个仅含状态风险、安全产出率恒定、知识完全转移的校准情形下进行数值校准。

Result: 该策略所需生产力仅比必要下限高 3.3%；双承诺的成本由客户服务端承担；固定算力分配的规则实际决定的是日期而非风险。

Conclusion: 风险上限与开发截止期能否同时承诺取决于安全知识的最低存量，先学习再重放开发并预留安全算力接近该下限；监管若只约束算力分配，则只能锁定时间表而不能锁定风险水平。

Abstract: Can a regulator promise both a risk ceiling and a development deadline when safety productivity is unknown? A ceiling below the final model's unprotected hazard requires a minimum stock of safety knowledge, so both promises hold only if weak research can be ruled out. Learning first and then replaying development, with spare compute in safety, comes close to that minimum. In a calibration with only state risk, constant safety yield, and full knowledge transfer, it needs only 3.3 percent more productivity than the necessary bound. Customer services pay for the guarantee. Rules that fix compute allocation fix dates, not risk.

</details>


### [3] [Who Leads and Who Collects:Algorithmic Collusion in Markets of Heterogeneous Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11256)
*Jun Yeong Lee*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究当市场中卖方使用不同定价算法（不同厂商的大模型）而非同一算法时，算法合谋是否仍然出现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有算法合谋证据多来自所有卖方运行同一算法的市场，本文追问异质算法（不同厂商大模型）混合定价时合谋是否持续、如何被混合结构影响。

Method: 采用四家厂商（Claude、Gemini、DeepSeek、GPT）各自最便宜档位的语言模型，在无沟通的四企业logit Bertrand市场中定价，覆盖全部同质、二二混合与完全混合共11种组合，每种20次运行、200期。

Result: 合谋是模型属性：Claude与Gemini市场达垄断租金的72%–79%，DeepSeek市场24%，GPT市场为零（但其价格漂移至垄断水平之上而非趋向竞争）。混合本身不降低合谋：含Gemini（开价最高、收敛最高）的市场比其构成的同质市场更合谋，含Claude（开价较低、跟随降价）的市场更不合谋，仅完全混合市场显著低于同质市场均值。稳定性取决于最不稳定参与者，两家GPT即可阻止任何市场收敛。混合市场内租金按DeepSeek>Claude>Gemini>GPT的传递顺序分配，与锚定排名相反；抬价者获得最少租金，符合价格领导模型预测。

Conclusion: 算法合谋并非同质算法专属；异质大模型定价下合谋程度由模型特性决定，混合结构可加剧或削弱合谋，且租金分配呈现与价格领导预测一致的逆向模式。

Abstract: Evidence that pricing algorithms collude comes from markets in which every seller runs the same algorithm. We ask what happens when they do not. Four language models from four providers, each at its cheapest tier, price in a four-firm logit Bertrand market without communication, in every homogeneous, two-by-two and fully mixed composi- tion (11 cells, 20 runs, 200 periods). Collusion is a property of the model: Claude and Gemini markets reach 72 to 79 percent of the monopoly rent, DeepSeek markets 24 percent, and GPT markets none, though GPT prices drift above the monopoly level rather than toward competition. Mixing does not reduce collusion by itself. Markets containing Gemini, which opens at the highest price and settles highest, are more col- lusive than the homogeneous markets they are built from; markets containing Claude, which opens lower and follows its rivals down, are less so; and only the fully mixed market is significantly less collusive than the average homogeneous one. Stability de- pends on the least stable participant: two GPT firms suffice to keep any market from converging. Inside mixed markets the rent is shared in a transitive order, DeepSeek over Claude over Gemini over GPT, that inverts the anchor ranking. The model that raises the price collects the least of the rent, as the price-leadership model predicts.

</details>


### [4] [The Myth of Restrictive Working-Time Agreements: Firms' Working-Time Adjustment after Exit from Collective Bargaining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12049)
*Vinzenz Pyka,André Rieder*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 基于德国IAB企业面板2008—2024年数据，采用固定效应与事件研究模型，发现退出集体谈判后周工时仅增加约0.3%—0.6%（每周8—13分钟），且无证据显示周末工作等其他工时调整边际被更广泛使用，表明工时约束并非退出集体谈判的主要驱动因素。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 德国雇主普遍声称集体协议限制了其在工时安排上的灵活性，且以往调查证据常将工时约束视为企业退出集体谈判覆盖的主要原因之一。本文旨在用代表性企业面板数据系统检验这一论断。

Method: 使用德国IAB企业面板（IAB Establishment Panel）2008—2024年数据，以退出集体谈判覆盖为处理事件，采用固定效应模型与事件研究（event-study）设计，估计退出后周工时及其他工时调整边际（如周六、周日工作）的变化。

Conclusion: 与以往调查证据相反，本文结果对“工时约束是退出集体谈判覆盖的主要驱动因素之一”这一观点提出质疑。

Abstract: Employers in Germany argue that collective agreements restrict their flexibility concerning working hours. Using data from the IAB Establishment Panel for the years 2008 to 2024, we investigate working-time adjustments after exit from collective bargaining. We find that exit is associated with only a modest increase in weekly working hours of around 0.3 to 0.6 per cent using fixed-effects and event-study models, corresponding to eight to thirteen additional minutes per week. This adjustment occurs shortly after leaving collective bargaining coverage. We further find no evidence that other margins of working-time adjustment, such as Saturday or Sunday work, are used more extensively after exit. In contrast to previous survey evidence, our findings call into question that working-hours constraints are one of the main drivers of exit from collective bargaining coverage.

### [5] [Microfinance Competition in the Presence of Moneylenders: Theory and Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12186)
*Shyamal Chowdhury,Prabal Roy Chowdhury,Joeri Smits*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文构建含事后道德风险的动态模型，研究小额信贷机构（MFI）与放贷人（moneylender）并存及竞争效应，并用孟加拉村级随机实验验证：新增一家MFI使放贷人贷款减少约30–40%，但对MFI及总借贷无显著影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管小额信贷大规模扩张且利率更高，放贷人仍与MFI长期并存，二者共存机制及MFI竞争如何影响这一格局尚缺乏统一解释与因果证据。

Method: 构建含事后道德风险的动态模型：借款人用MFI贷款进行生产，受冲击后可向放贷人借过桥贷款以偿还MFI并保留未来MFI信贷渠道。基于此分析MFI竞争对MFI信贷与放贷人信贷的影响，并利用孟加拉按基线MFI覆盖度分层的村级随机扩张实验进行检验。

Result: 竞争对MFI信贷的影响不确定：既可能削弱还款激励，也可能替代放贷人作为过桥贷款提供者；但竞争明确降低放贷人信贷。实证上，新增一家MFI使两至三年后放贷人贷款数量减少约30–40%，对MFI借贷和总借贷无可检测影响。

Abstract: After decades of microfinance expansion and despite charging higher interest rates, moneylenders continue to exist alongside microfinance institutions (MFIs). We then study how MFI competition shapes the coexistence between MFIs and moneylenders. In a village-level randomized expansion in Bangladesh, stratified by baseline MFI presence, one additional MFI reduces the number of moneylender loans by roughly 30-40% two to three years later, with no detectable effect on MFI or overall borrowing. This suggests that, in markets where MFIs rely primarily on the threat of exclusion to induce repayment and moneylenders provide emergency finance, gains from additional MFI entry may be greater in less saturated areas.

</details>

### [6] [Diffusive Market Impact: A Consistent Microfoundation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11691)
*Julius F. Bonart*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文将"结构性价格扩散率"理论微观基础化：在包含一名交易者与多名套利者的经济中，套利者逐步消除可预测收益，当聚合私人信息趋于完备并可收敛至平稳极限时，交易者冲击律呈现 j=U·w 形式，U 为因果全通滤波器。文章进一步探讨冲击相位的经济含义，并给出双指数交易流下冲击衰减带宽约为 50%–60% 的结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 结构性价格扩散率能够解释市场冲击的多项经验规律（包括平方根定律及其在低交易速率下向线性区制的过渡），但其核心预测——信息中性交易策略产生扩散型冲击状态——此前缺乏微观基础。本文旨在为该预测提供基于套利者信息聚合的微观基础，并厘清冲击相位（impact phase）的经济含义。

Method: 构建一个包含一名交易者与多名套利者的经济体。每名套利者观察到已实现收益，以及基本面收益某一组成部分的私人信号；套利者逐步消除可预测收益。分析聚合私人信息趋于完备、并在适当条件下收敛至平稳极限时的冲击律形式；刻画与交易流生成滤波 L 距离最小的全通滤波 U 的经济含义；并考察双指数 L（模型允许的最简形式）下的冲击衰减特征。

Result: 在信息聚合趋于完备并收敛至平稳极限的条件下，交易者冲击律为 j=U·w，其中 w 为交易计划中的新息（innovation），U 为因果全通滤波，因而冲击收益为白噪声，即便任何单个套利者都无法重构潜在交易策略。使 U 与生成交易流的滤波 L 距离最小者被视为特别有意义的候选：此时交易流在冲击与套利下失真最小，且总能保证正的冲击成本。在双指数 L 生成的交易流下，冲击衰减被限制在 50%–60% 的窄带内，低于部分经验估计，却与另一部分估计相当吻合；更复杂的交易流模型可导致不同的衰减。

Conclusion: 冲击相位的经济含义可由"与交易流生成滤波 L 距离最小的全通滤波 U"来刻画，该选择使交易流失真最小并保证正的冲击成本。双指数交易流给出 50%–60% 的冲击衰减窄带，与部分经验证据一致。在真实市场中，冲击相位的长期尾部可能略有"失真"，从而允许冲击传播子完全松弛，表现为微弱、长期的冲击状态均值回复。

Abstract: Structural price diffusivity explains many empirical regularities of market impact including the ``square-root law'' and its crossover to a linear regime for low trading rates \citep{bonart2026diffusive}. One of its central predictions is that an information-neutral trading strategy generates a diffusive impact state. We microfound this result in an economy with a trader and many arbitrageurs who progressively eliminate predictable returns. Each arbitrageur observes realized returns and a private signal of one component of the fundamental return. As aggregate private information becomes complete and under suitable convergence to a stationary limit, the trader's impact law is of the form $j=U\cdot w$, where $w$ is the innovation in the trading schedule and $U$ is causal all-pass. Impact returns are therefore white, even though no individual arbitrageur can reconstruct the underlying trading strategy. We then investigate the economic meaning of the impact phase. We argue that the $U$ which has minimum distance from the filter $L$ generating the trade flow is an especially interesting candidate: Trade flow is then minimally distorted under impact and arbitrage, and it always guarantees positive impact costs. Under a trader flow generated by a biexponential $L$ (the simplest form allowed in our model) an interesting result emerges: Impact decline is confined to a narrow band of $50\%$--$60\%$, lower than some empirical estimates and quite consistent with others. More complicated flow models can lead to different decays. Finally, we argue that in real markets, the impact phase is probably somewhat ``distorted'' in its long-term tails which allows for a full relaxation of the impact propagator. The effect is weak, long-term mean-reversion of the impact state.

</details>
