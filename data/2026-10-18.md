### [1] [NEST: Stale-Robust Tokenized-Stock Measurement for a Crypto-Treasury LETF Stack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21491)
*Doohwi Cha,Minsuk Sung,Minjae Lee,Hyeonjun Yeo,Hanhee Kim,Seunghan Son,Donghwa Seo,Juyeong Lee*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 提出 NEST 协议，将代币化股票标记与开盘缺口的"共享历史拟合"从"条件测量价值"中分离，并用 PDI 在实物案例中量化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 代币化股票在挂牌市场休市时仍可交易，其标记或在盘外为比特币—加密财库股—杠杆ETF 的分层暴露测量带来帮助；但代币收益与次日开盘缺口共享"前收盘→代币标记"这一段价格变动，拟合中有一部分是机械性的，需加以甄别。

Method: 开发 NEST 陈旧稳健协议：以共享历史恒等式与方差时钟界量化纯时点效应对拟合的贡献；用偏决定指数 PDI（控制公开变量后代币与缺口的平方偏相关）度量剩余信息量，并给出以 PDI 为单位的有限样本临界边界；辅以产品、来源、时间戳与执行四项审计判定标记可采纳性；在 MSTRx、NVDAX、AAPLX、TSLAX 及 Gate 逐笔重建上验证。

Result: MSTRx 的代币单独 R²=0.916，控制比特币、标记时龄与 09:00 前 MSTR 盘前代理后仅解释剩余缺口变异的 15.7%（1% 边界 0.031），更严格时钟变体下为 14.0%–17.0%；Gate 逐笔重建为 25.2%。NVDAX、AAPLX 超过边界，TSLAX 未超过。

Conclusion: 代币标记具有可采纳的条件测量价值，但其角色是对 BTC 单位暴露诊断的"更新"而非"替代"；机械共享历史必须显式剔除，PDI 与边界提供了可复现的判定框架。

Abstract: Tokenized equities trade while listed markets are closed, so their marks could improve off-hours measurement along layered exposures that link Bitcoin, crypto-treasury stocks, and leveraged ETFs. A token return and the next opening gap, however, share the price move from the previous close to the token mark, so part of their apparent fit is mechanical. We develop NEST, a stale-robust protocol that separates this shared-history fit from conditional measurement value. A shared-history identity and a variance-clock bound show how much fit timing alone can generate. The partial determination index (PDI), the squared partial correlation between token and gap given public controls, measures what remains, and a finite-sample frontier expresses the partial-F decision in PDI units. Product, provenance, timestamp, and execution audits decide whether an informative mark is admissible. For MSTRx, a tracker certificate on MicroStrategy (MSTR), a token-only $R^2$ of 0.916 shrinks to a modest increment once Bitcoin, mark age, and a pre-09:00 MSTR premarket proxy are controlled: the token explains 15.7% of the remaining gap variation (1% frontier 0.031), and 14.0% to 17.0% under stricter clock variants, including one in which every token mark precedes 09:00. A trade-level reconstruction on Gate gives 25.2%; NVDAX and AAPLX clear their frontiers, whereas TSLAX does not. An admitted mark updates, rather than replaces, BTC-unit exposure diagnostics for the BTC-MSTR-LETF chain.

### [2] [How Much Screening Buys a Second? Speed, Selection, and the Maker's Spread](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21510)
*Aleksei Adadurov,Eduard Airapetian,Anton Chtepine,Antero Eloranta,Boris Pletenev,Andrei Sebyakin,Arsenii Valitov*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文研究做市商在面对可捕捉过期报价（stale quote）的对手方时所需的最大价差（break-even spread），并将"更快重定价"与"筛选知情流"两种防御置于同一尺度上。给出使慢速做市商与快速无筛选场所等价的精确筛选率，且在跳、原子与无穷方差下均成立；实证显示基准价格创新高度非高斯，12 秒→1 秒节奏可削减 84.5% 预期冲击，故需至少筛选 84.5% 知情流，高于高斯模型隐含的 71.1%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 延迟报价会被知情或快速对手方"捡漏"（picked off），做市商需要在两种防御间权衡：缩短重定价间隔，或对订单流进行知情筛选。现有分析多依赖高斯假设，无法刻画真实高频价格路径的非高斯特征，因此需要一套不依赖分布假设的等价尺度，量化"快"与"筛"的替代关系。

Method: (1) 建立 break-even 条件，推导慢速做市商与快速无筛选场所匹配所需的精确筛选率，即在两种防御间建立可比较的单一标度；(2) 证明仅需价格创新可积（integrable），从而允许跳跃、原子与无穷方差；(3) 将可预测漂移处理为居中（centering）问题，说明其成本在以期望参考价重新居中后消失；(4) 以 2026 年 6–8 月四个 USDC-WETH 池、以 Binance ETH-USDT 为基准进行实证，校准一秒基准创新的经验分布并对比高斯模型。

Result: 经验基准创新显著非高斯：73% 的秒内价格无变化。将节奏从 12 秒缩短至 1 秒可消除 84.5% 的预期冲击，故 12 秒做市商须至少筛选 84.5% 的知情流才能匹配 1 秒场所，显著高于高斯假设下的 71.1%。趋势行情中过期池在执行时被套利：顺势成交的有效价差比逆势成交低 11.1 bps，且四个池符号一致。

Conclusion: "更快重定价"与"筛选知情流"可在同一标度上等价换算，且该等价关系对分布假设稳健（含跳跃、原子、无穷方差）。由于真实微观结构价格创新具有大量零变动与厚尾，高斯模型会系统性低估所需的筛选强度。可预测漂移并非本质成本，通过以期望参考价重新居中即可消除。趋势市中被执行式捡漏导致显著的方向性价差不对称，为做市防御策略提供了可检验的经验依据。

Abstract: We study the spread a liquidity provider needs to break even against counterparties that can exploit a stale quote, and we place two defenses on a single scale: repricing sooner and screening informed flow. A break-even condition pins down the exact screening rate at which a slower maker matches a faster unscreened venue: the selection that buys back the extra staleness. The result requires only an integrable price innovation and therefore holds under jumps, atoms, and infinite variance. Predictable drift is a centering problem: it widens a spot-centered quote, but its cost vanishes once the quote is recentered on the expected reference. In four USDC-WETH pools benchmarked against Binance ETH-USDT from June to August 2026, the one-second benchmark innovation is sharply non-Gaussian: 73% of seconds show no price change. Cutting cadence from twelve seconds to one sheds 84.5% of the expected shock, so a twelve-second maker must screen at least 84.5% of informed flow to match a one-second venue, far above the 71.1% that a Gaussian model implies. In trending markets, the stale pool is picked off at execution: with-trend fills obtain an effective spread 11.1 bps lower than against-trend fills, with the same sign in all four pools.

### [3] [Pricing the Unprovable: Ambiguity, Turnstile Runs, and the Verifiability Discount in Crypto Protocols](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21540)
*Doohwi Cha,Hanhee Kim,Hyeonjun Yeo,Minsuk Sung,Minjae Lee,Seunghan Son,Donghwa Seo,Juyeong Lee*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 论文提出"可验证性折让"(verifiability discount)概念，构建VDISC可复现框架，将已报告损失、暴露价值、公开证据、收益与资金池流向分离记录，并以Orchard事件、Zcash历史案例及40事件安全面板进行检验，发现修复但无法排除历史滥用的披露与最大负收益及屏蔽池净流出同向对应（相关性而非因果）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 隐私保护协议能修复漏洞，却无法确认漏洞此前是否已被利用；即使无人报告被盗且总供给受保护，市场仍无法判定历史状态，由此产生潜在价值折让，而现有研究难以区分"已报告损失"与"未解决的暴露历史"。

Method: 提出turnstile模型解释早期退出如何将潜在缺口转移给剩余持有者；构建VDISC框架，分别记录报告损失、暴露价值、公开证据、收益率与池流量；结合Orchard分阶段披露、重建链上流量、早期Zcash案例及40事件安全面板进行实证。

Result: Orchard事件符合预测方向：修复与供给保证后市场反应平淡，而最大负收益与屏蔽池净流出同"无法排除历史滥用"的承认相对应；在38个有公开损失数字的事件中，市值标准化报告损失与未解决暴露高度重叠（Pearson 0.86，Spearman 0.56），部分因暴露常由损失本身编码，故单凭收益无法区分二者。

Conclusion: 结果仅为方向性而非因果；提出三项设计优先事项：生成关于历史状态的隐私兼容证据、限制单一缺陷所暴露的状态范围、显式披露残余不确定性。

Abstract: Privacy-preserving protocols can repair a vulnerability without establishing whether it was exploited beforehand, even when no theft is reported and aggregate supply is protected. We call the potential value reduction from this gap the verifiability discount. A turnstile model shows how early withdrawal can shift a possible shortfall to remaining holders and why credible evidence can become more valuable as exposure grows. We introduce VDISC, a reproducible framework that records reported loss, exposed value, public evidence, returns, and pool flows separately. It combines Orchard's staged disclosures with reconstructed on-chain flows, earlier Zcash cases, and a 40-event security panel. Orchard follows the predicted direction: market responses were muted after repair and supply assurance, while the largest negative return and aggregate shielded-pool net outflow aligned with the acknowledgment that past abuse could not be ruled out. These patterns are directional, not causal. Across events, market-cap-normalized reported loss and unresolved exposure overlap among the 38 events with a public loss figure (Pearson 0.86, Spearman 0.56), partly because exposure is often coded from the loss itself, so returns alone cannot distinguish reported loss from unresolved history. The findings motivate three candidate design priorities: produce privacy-compatible evidence about past states, limit the state exposed by a single flaw, and disclose residual uncertainty explicitly.

</details>
