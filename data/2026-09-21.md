### [1] [Adapting the Actor Model of Concurrency for High-Frequency Trading: Synchronous Message Delivery (fast_send) and a Tick-to-Book Latency Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21173)
*Vincent Maciejewski*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 论文反驳了actor模型不适合高频交易(HFT)的传统观点,针对同址(co-located)actor场景提出四项扩展并在开源C++20框架kaspar-hft中实现。同步投递fast_send的往返延迟仅数十纳秒,在CME实时行情馈送(ES、NQ、ZN期货)上,框架自身开销不到7微秒解码建簿基线的1%;尾延迟由市场非泊松聚集到达过程主导而非actor机制,并实现了实盘与确定性回测的代码统一。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: actor模型具有状态隔离、无数据竞争、无死锁、单消息顺序推理等优点,但长期被认为不适合高频交易:直觉上actor意味着大量线程、每actor独立邮箱、每次交互需堆分配消息并伴随上下文切换,这些开销与微秒级预算不兼容。论文旨在论证这一否定判断在actor同址部署时并不成立。

Method: 提出并实现kaspar-hft开源C++20框架,含四项HFT适配扩展:(1)fast_send,同步投递机制,发送线程内联执行接收方handler并以值形式返回回复,具备接收方透明性(handler无法分辨同步或异步投递及运行线程);(2)actor group,将多个actor共调度于同一线程并共享邮箱;(3)每actor可选邮箱队列;(4)内存池。分组同步链使N个调度上下文切换降为O(1),线程本地调用链检测在加锁前捕获循环调用。采用微基准测试及CME实时行情馈送(ES、NQ、ZN)实测验证。

Result: 同步往返微基准延迟为数十纳秒。在CME实时市场数据馈送(ES、NQ、ZN期货)上,socket-to-book延迟可分解为约7微秒的解码建簿基线与每消息斜率,框架自身贡献不足基线的1%;尾延迟由市场非泊松、聚集式到达过程决定,而非actor机制。共享队列分组还带来生产/仿真二元性:同一actor代码无需修改即可用于实盘与确定性回测。

Conclusion: 对同址actor而言,actor模型不适合HFT的论断是错误的。通过fast_send、actor group、可选邮箱队列与内存池四项扩展,actor的状态隔离、无数据竞争、无死锁与顺序推理优势得以保留,同时开销被压缩至可忽略水平,并可统一实盘与回测代码路径。

Abstract: The actor model - state isolation, data-race freedom, deadlock resistance, and sequential single-message reasoning - has long been dismissed as unsuitable for high-frequency trading (HFT): actors seem to imply many threads, a mailbox per actor, and a heap-allocated message plus a context switch per interaction, overhead incompatible with a microsecond budget. This paper argues the dismissal is wrong for co-located actors, and supports it both analytically and with a deployed, measured implementation: kaspar-hft, an open-source C++20 framework. Four extensions adapt the model for HFT: fast_send, a synchronous delivery mechanism in which the sending thread runs the receiver's handler inline and returns the reply as a value; actor groups, which co-schedule actors on one thread behind a shared mailbox; per-actor selectable mailbox queues; and a memory pool. fast_send has receiver transparency: the handler cannot tell whether delivery was synchronous or asynchronous, or which thread runs it. A grouped synchronous chain runs on one thread, cutting scheduler context switches from O(N) to O(1), and a thread-local call-chain test catches cyclic invocation before any lock is taken. Microbenchmarks put the synchronous round trip at tens of nanoseconds. On a live CME market-data feed (ES, NQ, ZN futures), socket-to-book latency decomposes into a ~7 microsecond decode-and-book floor plus a per-message slope; the framework's own contribution is under 1% of the floor. The tail is set not by the actor machinery but by the market's non-Poisson, clustered arrival process, characterized in a companion paper. The shared-queue group also yields a production/simulation duality: the same actor code runs unchanged in live trading and deterministic backtest.

### [2] [Bricks or Cash? Externalities of Housing Upgrading in High-density Cities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21478)
*Sumit Agarwal,Ying Deng,Yi Fan,Qi Gao,Jing Li,Lin Ma*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文利用新加坡全国性公共住房"主要升级计划"（Main Upgrading Programme）的分批推出来估计高密度城市的住房外部性。控制非随机的邻里暴露后，升级使受处理建筑在完工时价格上涨11.5%，并使500米内的邻近建筑转售价格上涨约2%，超过该距离后衰减至零。一个带有距离衰减外部性的模型表明，在高密度环境下，溢出效应可为实物供给（in-kind provision）造成的扭曲提供正当性；而在较低密度下这一优势减弱甚至反转。来自200多万居民的行政数据显示，升级不成比例地留住了老年住户，提示年龄特定性设施可能是尚未被充分探讨的外部性渠道。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 住房外部性（housing externalities）是城市经济学的核心议题，但已有研究多集中于低密度城市或少见的局部改造项目，缺乏对高密度城市背景下大规模、系统性住房升级的因果识别。同时，实物供给（in-kind provision）会带来效率扭曲，其是否可被邻里溢出效应所抵消，取决于外部性随距离衰减的具体形态与城市密度。本文借此契机，既量化外部性的大小与空间范围，又评估密度在"溢出收益 vs. 扭曲成本"权衡中的作用，并探索外部性的具体渠道。

Method: 以新加坡全国性公共住房"主要升级计划"的分批（staggered）推出作为准自然实验，采用双重差分/事件研究式识别策略，并控制邻里暴露的非随机性（即处理组与对照组的空间选择偏差）。估计受处理建筑价格与邻近建筑转售价格随距离变化的响应。随后构建一个包含距离衰减外部性的空间均衡模型，推导溢出效应与实物供给扭曲之间的取舍，并在不同密度设定下进行比较。最后使用覆盖200多万居民的行政微观数据，分析升级对不同年龄组住户留居（retention）的异质性影响。

Result: 1）控制非随机邻里暴露后，升级使受处理建筑完工时价格上涨11.5%；2）500米内邻近建筑转售价格上涨约2%，且随距离衰减，超出该范围后趋于零；3）模型显示高密度环境下溢出效应足以正当化实物供给的扭曲，而在较低密度下该优势减弱并反转；4）行政数据显示升级不成比例地留住老年现有住户，表明年龄特定性设施是潜在的外部性渠道。

Conclusion: 本文提供了高密度城市住房外部性的因果证据，表明升级项目存在显著的正向空间溢出，且其收益具有明确的空间衰减结构。理论上，密度是决定实物供给政策是否有效率的关键变量：高密度下溢出收益可抵消扭曲，低密度下则不然。实证上，老年住户的差异化留居提示年龄特定性设施构成被忽视的外部性渠道。研究对城市更新政策的空间 targeting、密度导向的政策设计及住房升级的分配后果具有启示意义。

Abstract: We estimate housing externalities in a high-density city, exploiting the staggered rollout of Singapore's nationwide Main Upgrading Programme for public housing. Controlling for nonrandom neighborhood exposure, we find that upgrading raises treated buildings' prices by 11.5% upon completion and neighboring buildings' resale prices by about 2% within 500 meters, decaying to zero beyond. A model with distance-decaying externalities shows that in dense settings spillovers justify the distortions of in-kind provision; this advantage diminishes and reverses at lower densities. Administrative data on over 2 million residents show that upgrading disproportionately retains older incumbents, suggesting age-specific amenities as an underexplored externality channel.

### [3] [Design and pricing of a transparent parametric-modeled loss CAT bond: application to German windstorm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22052)
*John Ery,Erwan Koch*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 本文提出一种新型巨灾债券触发机制，基于成本随机场将物理危险性、脆弱性函数与暴露度分离，可在参数化与模型化损失之间灵活取值，实现了完全透明且易评估的触发条件，并给出定价方法；以德国历史风暴事件为案例，拟合max-stable随机场并校准脆弱性，评估基差风险与模型表现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 巨灾债券通过从更广泛资本市场获取容量，弥补再保险供给不足；但现有触发机制存在道德风险与基差风险之间的权衡，且传统赔付建模技术不透明、评估复杂，阻碍了投资者进入该市场。加之气候变化使自然灾害频率与强度上升，保险公司和公共部门对透明、易评估的巨灾债券需求增强。

Method: 构建由成本随机场导出的触发机制，将物理危险性、脆弱性函数和暴露度分离，使触发形式可在参数化与模型化损失之间灵活调整；提出相应的定价方法。案例研究中，采用德国历史风暴事件的风速数据，在再保险行业标准分辨率上拟合max-stable随机场，并利用行业损失与暴露度数据将脆弱性分量校准至历史观测。

Result: 该触发机制完全透明且比赔付建模技术更易评估；案例中成功拟合max-stable随机场并完成脆弱性校准；测量了触发机制相关的基差风险，并进行了完整模型评估与数值结果讨论。

Conclusion: 所提触发机制与定价方法可有效缓解道德风险与基差风险的权衡，提升巨灾债券透明度，对保险公司、公共部门及有意进入巨灾债券市场的参与者具有现实意义。

Abstract: Catastrophe (cat) bonds overcome some lack of reinsurance by sourcing capacity from the wider capital markets. We present a new type of cat bond addressing the known trade-off between moral hazard and basis risk. As our main contributions we propose a trigger mechanism which is entirely transparent and simpler to evaluate compared to indemnity modeling techniques, as well as a methodology to price this cat bond. This is relevant for insurers and public authorities in a world where natural disasters are occurring with increasing frequency and severity due to climate change, but also for players willing to enter the cat bond market for whom the lack of transparency of this asset class has been a significant obstacle. Our trigger is derived from a cost random field which separates the physical hazard, a vulnerability function and the exposure. This allows the trigger to take a flexible form between parametric and modeled loss, in case exposure is taken into account. We present a case study based on historical windstorm events impacting Germany. Using wind speed data from historical storms, we fit a max-stable random field on a resolution which is standard in the reinsurance industry. The availability of industry loss and exposure data allows us to calibrate the vulnerability component to historical observations. Besides measuring the basis risk associated with our trigger, we perform a full model assessment and discuss numerical results.

</details>
