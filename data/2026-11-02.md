### [1] [Expected shortfall estimation and capital coverage under overlapping scenarios](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41624)
*Martin Aichele,Igor Cialenco,Damian Jelito,Marcin Pitera*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 重叠取样会使ES系统性低估、削弱资本覆盖，序列相依与厚尾会放大该效应，保守加权只能缓解不能消除，需结合模拟评估与覆盖诊断进行模型验证。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ES通常基于由重叠多日收益构建的短样本P&L情景估计，而所用非参数估计量却建立在独立抽样假设下；作者要研究这种假设错配如何影响估计精度与资本覆盖，以及它与序列相依、厚尾的交互作用。

Method: 在相干风险估计框架下，比较六种基于次序统计量的ES估计量（拟合、偏差、资本覆盖指标）；按FRTB设定采用一年估计窗口与2.5%置信水平的ES；结合模拟与十二个风险因子、九十九个股票组合的市场数据。

Result: 重叠抽样并非仅增加噪声，而是系统性下移ES估计；序列相依同向作用，厚尾放大两者；更保守的加权可缓解但无法消除低估。市场数据上，重叠抽样使估计资本覆盖已实现损失所需的置信水平中位数约翻倍，而ES2.5%/VaR1%比率变化相对较小。

Conclusion: 重叠抽样可能显著低估基于ES的资本，且因银行普遍采用少量标准估计量，该低估可能较为普遍；将模拟式的估计风险评估与基于覆盖的诊断相结合，可作为模型验证与监控中识别该问题的实用方法。

Abstract: Expected shortfall (ES) is routinely estimated from a short sample of profit-and-loss (P&L) scenarios built from overlapping multi-day returns, while the non-parametric estimators applied to such data are designed under independent sampling. We study how this mismatch affects estimation accuracy and capital coverage, and how it interacts with serial dependence and heavy tails in the underlying P&L. Working within the coherent risk estimation framework, we compare six order-statistic-based ES estimators on fit, bias, and capital-coverage metrics. Following the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB), we use a one-year estimation window and ES at the $2.5\%$ confidence level as a benchmark setup. We combine simulations with market data on twelve risk factors and ninety-nine equity portfolios. We show that overlapping sampling does not merely add noise but shifts ES estimates systematically downward, thereby weakening capital coverage. Serial dependence acts in the same direction, and heavy tails amplify both effects. More conservative weighting mitigates the underestimation but does not remove it. On market data, the confidence level at which the estimated capital covers realized losses roughly doubles in median under overlapping sampling for every estimator, while the $\textrm{ES}_{2.5\%}$-to-$\textrm{VaR}_{1\%}$ ratio, which links the FRTB base market risk capital metric to its pre-FRTB predecessor, changes comparatively little. Overlapping sampling can thus substantially understate ES-based capital. Since many banks apply a small set of standard estimators in their FRTB regulatory or economic capital model in a historical simulation approach, this understatement is likely to be common for many institutions, and combining simulation-based assessment of estimation risk with coverage-based diagnostics offers a practical method to detect it in model validation and monitoring.
