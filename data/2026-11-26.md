### [1] [Will AI Replace Juniors? How Task Complexity Reduces Automation Risk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34099)
*Vincent Rost,Fabian Stephany*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该研究通过预注册调查实验，考察任务复杂度如何影响企业用AI工具替代入门级劳动力的意愿。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者希望厘清任务复杂度这一关键任务特征，如何塑造管理者以AI替代入门级员工而非招聘的决策倾向。

Method: 对德国信息经济与制造业1,435家企业管理者开展预注册调查实验。管理者被随机分配考虑两类长期额外产能需求：结构良好、标准化的任务，或需要专业判断、情境知识与独立问题解决的复杂任务。

Result: 相比标准化任务，复杂任务使"使用AI而非招聘入门级员工"的意愿在五点量表上降低0.54分，并使对人工监督重要性的评价提高0.15分。此外，处理前回答显示管理者预期AI应用会同时减少入门级员工和有经验员工的招聘，但对入门级员工的抑制显著更强。

Conclusion: 任务复杂度是抑制AI替代入门级劳动力的重要边界条件，复杂任务下企业对人工监督的需求更高；同时管理者普遍预期AI将更强烈地冲击入门级岗位。

Abstract: How does task complexity shape firms' willingness to substitute entry-level labour with artificial intelligence (AI) tools? We study this question using a pre-registered survey experiment with managers from 1,435 German firms in the information economy and manufacturing industry. Managers are randomly assigned to consider a long lasting need for additional capacity involving either well-structured and standardised tasks, or more complex tasks that require professional judgement, contextual knowledge and independent problem-solving. Relative to standardised tasks, complex tasks reduce willingness to use AI rather than hire an entry-level employee by 0.54 points on a five-point scale and increase the importance attached to human oversight by 0.15 points. Further, pre-treatment responses indicate that managers expect the availability of AI applications to reduce hiring of both entry-level employees and experienced workers, but significantly more strongly for entry-level workers.

### [2] [AI, Automation, and Fiscal Pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34234)
*Spencer Bastani,Georg Graetz,Bernat Pla,Oskar N. Skans*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 论文研究自动化如何影响财政：在资本税率低于劳动税率、公共服务依赖税收融资的设定下，自动化使收入向轻税资本转移，即使税率不变也会恶化预算赤字；EU-27基准情景中，自动化带来12%经济增长的同时赤字上升近1个百分点（占增加值）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有研究多关注自动化对就业、工资与不平等的影响，较少系统刻画其对公共财政的影响。论文聚焦两个制度特征：资本税率低于劳动税率，以及医疗、教育等公共服务由税收而非使用者付费融资，这两点使自动化带来的税基结构变化可能直接冲击财政平衡。

Method: 构建两部门一般均衡模型，在任务层面（task-level）引入自动化，并按欧盟国家数据进行校准。模型中区分市场部门与公共服务部门，刻画自动化速度差异、要素收入份额变化及税收结构（劳动税高、资本税低）。

Result: 自动化将收入重新配置到税率较低的资本上。若公共服务随消费者需求扩张，在税率不变时赤字恶化，即使公共服务部门自动化速度与私人部门相同亦然。在EU-27基准情景（市场部门自动化更快）下，自动化推动经济增长12%的同时，赤字上升约1个百分点的增加值。

Conclusion: 自动化对财政的影响取决于税收结构与公共服务融资方式：在劳动税重、资本税轻且公共服务靠税收融资的经济体中，自动化即便促进增长也可能带来显著财政压力，意味着增长与财政可持续性可能脱钩，需关注税制调整与公共服务融资改革。

Abstract: We study how automation affects public finances in countries that (i) tax labor at a higher rate than capital and (ii) fund services such as health care and education from tax revenues rather than user fees. We embed task-level automation within a two-sector general equilibrium model, which we calibrate to match data from EU countries. Automation shifts income toward more lightly taxed capital. If public services follow consumer demand, the budget deficit worsens at unchanged tax rates, even if automation is as fast in the public-service sector as in the private sector. In our EU-27 benchmark scenario, where automation proceeds faster in the market sector, the deficit rises by nearly one percentage point of value added as the economy grows by twelve percent due to automation.

### [3] [How Large are Monetary Policy Effects? dentifying the Shocks in a VAR also Identifies the Constants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34722)
*Andrew Mountford*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 在SVAR中，识别基本冲击的同期效应矩阵约束同时也决定了结构常数项到简约式常数项的映射；作者主张对结构常数项施加先验并纳入贝叶斯估计，并以Baumeister and Hamilton (2018)的货币政策分析为例说明其重要性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有SVAR识别实践通常只关注同期效应矩阵，却忽视了该矩阵同时决定结构常数项与简约式常数项之间的映射关系。研究者往往对结构常数项存在先验认知，若不将其纳入贝叶斯估计，会损失信息并可能导致估计结果不符合经济直觉。

Method: 以Baumeister and Hamilton (2018)的货币政策SVAR为示例模型，在贝叶斯估计框架中对结构常数项施加先验，并利用同期效应矩阵所隐含的映射关系将其传递至简约式常数项；同时提出结构模型的可视化呈现方式，以展示各竞争性组成部分的贡献。

Result: 引入结构常数项先验后，估计出的货币政策规则更符合直觉，货币政策在解释数据演变中的作用显著增强；可视化显示观测到的通胀序列可被理解为正的需求与商品价格效应、被负的货币政策与风险利差效应所抵消的结果。

Conclusion: SVAR识别不应仅被视为对同期效应矩阵的约束问题，结构常数项的先验信息应纳入贝叶斯估计；这一做法能改善货币政策规则的估计合理性、放大货币政策的解释力，并为结构模型提供更清晰的可视化解读。

Abstract: Restrictions on the contemporaneous effects matrix used to identify fundamental shocks in a structural VAR, also determine the mapping from the structural constant terms to the reduced form constant terms. In some models one will have priors about these structural constant terms and these should therefore be included in a Bayesian estimation procedure. We illustrate the potential significance of this using the analysis of monetary policy by Baumeister and Hamilton (2018) as a motivating example. We show that imposing priors over the structural constant terms can lead to a more intuitive estimated monetary policy rule and a much larger estimated role for monetary policy in describing the evolution of the data. The analysis also allows for a clear visual presentation of the structural model, highlighting its multiple contending elements. For example the observed inflation series is estimated to be the result of positive demand and commodity price effects offset by negative monetary policy and risk spread effects.

### [4] [Modelling and Calibration of Climate-Related Credit Risk Under the Emissions Trading System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34771)
*Ying Liao,Ankush Agarwal*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 构建结构化模型研究碳排放交易体系（ETS）下碳价均衡与企业最优排放及违约风险。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ETS覆盖的排放份额不断上升，部分配额上限将在公司债务到期前降至零，需刻画企业在预期碳价下的能源选择与违约风险。

Method: 建立结构化模型：企业在预期碳价下于多种能源间选择排放；实际碳价使每个部门—区域单元的碳市场出清，含情景相关需求冲击与由适应行为缓解的物理损失；线性化情形下碳价确定、违约概率有显式表达。

Result: 证明均衡碳价存在，刻画配额约束何时生效，给出企业最优排放的反馈形式解；与奖惩机制不同，ETS在配额宽松时无成本、约束生效时使企业恰好达配额；用35家欧盟电力生产商校准，复现2018—2024年EUA均价水平。

Conclusion: 该模型为ETS下碳价形成与企业信用风险提供结构化刻画，并具实证拟合能力。

Abstract: Emission trading systems (ETSs) price a growing share of global emissions, and some of their caps are set to reach zero within the maturity of corporate debt. We develop a structural model in which firms choose their emissions from several energy sources under an anticipated carbon price, while the realized carbon price clears the carbon market of each sector-region cell, with a scenario-dependent demand shock and physical losses mitigated by adaptation. We show that the equilibrium carbon price exists, characterize when the cap binds, and derive the optimal emission of each firm in feedback form. In a linearized case of the model, the carbon price is deterministic, and the default probability is explicit. Unlike a reward-penalty scheme, the ETS imposes no cost while the cap is slack and makes firms meet it exactly once it binds. Calibrated to 35 European power producers under the EU ETS, the model reproduces the average level of the European Union Allowance price over 2018 to 2024.

### [5] [Rank-size analysis of optimal portfolio weights with a reverse-engineering application](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34171)
*Roy Cerqueti,Francesco Cesarone,Alessio Di Paolo,Valerio Ficcadenti*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文用秩-规模分析刻画四类组合模型（GMV、min-CVaR、RP、MDP）最优权重分布形状，结合拟合优度与累积二阶ε随机占优筛选最优函数形式，并以秩-规模参数为特征、随机森林做反向识别生成模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 组合权重结构缺乏系统的函数形式刻画与比较准则，且能否从观测权重结构反推生成模型尚不明确；需要紧凑、可解释的参数化描述组合配置几何。

Method: 对四种组合模型在滚动窗口下排序后的权重，拟合七种秩-规模设定；用标准拟合优度指标加Cumulative Second-order ε-Stochastic Dominance（二阶随机占优的ε参数化推广）比较设定；以标定参数为输入构建随机森林分类器，采用考虑估计窗口重叠的blocked cross-validation；实证应用于主要股票指数。

Result: 该方法给出跨不同市场构成的系统化最优函数形式选择准则；反向识别中，秩-规模参数可区分最小风险型与风险分散型策略，并能识别RP与MDP组合，而GMV与min-CVaR难以区分。

Conclusion: 秩-规模参数为组合配置几何提供了紧凑且信息量丰富的描述，可作为组合模型识别的有效特征。

Abstract: This paper investigates the shape of optimal portfolio weights using rank-size analysis. We consider four portfolio selection models: Global Minimum-Variance (GMV), minimum-CVaR, Risk Parity (RP), and the Most Diversified Portfolio (MDP). For each model, we fit seven rank-size specifications to the sorted portfolio weights generated over rolling windows. To compare the fitted specifications, we combine standard goodness-of-fit measures with Cumulative Second-order $\varepsilon$-Stochastic Dominance, an $\varepsilon$-parameterized generalization of Second-order Stochastic Dominance that quantifies the extent to which the exact dominance conditions are satisfied or violated. Empirically applied to major equity indices, this approach provides a systematic criterion for identifying the optimal functional form across diverse market compositions. Furthermore, we address the inverse problem of identifying the generating model from the observed weight structure. We use the calibrated rank-size parameters as inputs to a Random Forest classifier, evaluated by a blocked cross-validation that accounts for the overlap between estimation windows. The parameters separate minimum-risk from risk-diversification strategies and identify the RP and MDP portfolios, whereas the GMV and minimum-CVaR portfolios are difficult to distinguish. These results indicate that rank-size parameters provide a compact and informative description of portfolio allocation geometries.

</details>
