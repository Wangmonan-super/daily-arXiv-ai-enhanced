### [1] [Stablecoin Runs as Safe-Asset Stress Signals: Episode-Level Evidence for Central Bank Surveillance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26180)
*Ahmad Qadi,Hui Gong,Francesca Medda*

Main category: q-fin.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究稳定币挤兑是否携带美国国债收益率的信息。基于2021–2025年USDT、USDC、BUSD日度数据及五次压力事件，用代币数量变化（而非市值）度量资金流以剔除脱锚导致的"虚假流入"。结果显示：常态下资金流与收益率无稳定关系；三次恐慌性挤兑中，五日内10亿美元赎回对应三个月国库券收益率在十个交易日内下降8.5个基点，而两次监管冲击无此信号。效应跨期限广泛存在，且方向与储备抛售预测相反，指向"逃向质量"。效应主要由TerraUSD崩盘驱动，并由USDC/SVB事件佐证。恐慌期五日内约20–30亿美元赎回是稳定币压力对监管具有信息价值的门槛规模。

Abstract: This paper asks whether stablecoin runs carry information about U.S. Treasury yields. During the three panic-driven runs, a one-billion-dollar five-day redemption is associated with an 8.5 basis point fall in the three-month bill yield within ten trading days, while two regulatory episodes carry no such signal. The response is broad across maturities and has the opposite sign to what reserve liquidation predicts, which points to flight to quality. The effect is led by the TerraUSD collapse and corroborated by the USDC/SVB run. Five-day redemptions of roughly USD 2 to 3 billion during a panic mark the scale at which stablecoin stress becomes informative for surveillance.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Centered Gaussian precision gains are always beneficial. An exact symmetric finite-population equilibrium identifies how information resolution and individual strategic size interact near entry. A portfolio extension gives a matrix criterion for the fixed-roster ranking, which can fail under general cross-impact. Even with isotropic execution, shared activation allows equally accurate signals about target magnitude and composition to have opposite execution values under one unchanged prior. Intraday ETF price data is used to characterize target uncertainty and trading conditions without identifying activation or institutional costs.
