### [1] [Who Gains When the Umpire Is a Machine? Evidence from the Automated Ball-Strike System (ABS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27576)
*Seung Jin Cho,Jun Yeong Lee*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 利用2021–2026年超过100万次投球，本文把韩国棒球组织（KBO）2024年引入自动好球带系统分解为"消除人类裁量"与"施加新区域几何"两部分：前者消除了计数依赖判罚、捕手framing价值与明星打者的微小优势，合计仅每球员赛季数分的租金；后者因机器区域随身高缩放、对所有对阵一视同仁而重新分配好球，但对打席结果的影响精确估计且接近于零。投手迅速重新优化（投得更高，左投追平既有趋势），打者则未见调整。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: KBO是首个以自动系统取代本垒裁判好坏球判罚的顶级联盟，提供了检验"用规则取代人类判断"这一制度变革的稀缺自然实验。作者希望区分两类效应：移除人类裁量权（如计数依赖、捕手framing、明星效应）与引入新的好球带几何（随身高缩放、对所有人一致），从而说明规则替代人类判断的福利归宿取决于几何形状与暴露程度，而非仅取决于"是否去人化"。

Method: 基于2021–2026年逾百万次投球的面板数据，采用改革前后对比设计，将判罚变化分解为"人类裁量移除"与"区域几何替换"两个渠道；用计数条件下判罚概率、捕手framing、打者声誉等变量估计租金规模；用身高与对阵匹配的交互识别新几何的再分配效应，并结合暴露度（多少投球落入变化区域）与打席结果（K、BB、wOBA等）进行精确估计；再考察投手投球位置（高度、左右手差异）与打者行为的动态调整。

Result: 机器判罚消除了计数依赖判罚、捕手framing价值及明星打者的微小优势，合计租金仅每球员赛季数分。机器区域随身高缩放且对所有对阵一致，使好球向高个打者及跨对阵重新分配，但暴露于这些变化的投球数量有限，其对打席结果的影响被精确估计且接近于零。投手迅速重新优化，倾向投得更高，左投追平既有趋势；未发现打者调整的证据。

Conclusion: 以规则替代人类判断的效应并非同质：其影响取决于规则的几何形状（谁能获得再分配收益）与暴露程度（多少行为真正触碰变化边界）。在KBO案例中，去人化带来的租金消散很小，几何变化带来的结果效应也接近于零，主要调整来自投手供给侧而非打者需求侧。

Abstract: In 2024 the Korea Baseball Organization became the first top-tier league to replace the home-plate umpires ball-strike judgment with an automated system. Using over a million pitches from 2021 to 2026, we separate the change into the removal of human discretion and the imposition of a new zone geometry. The machine eliminated count-dependent calls, the value of catcher framing and a small advantage for star batters, rents worth only a few runs per player-season. Because the machines zone scales with height and treats every matchup alike, it redistributed strikes toward tall batters and across matchups, but few pitches are exposed to these changes, and their effects on plate-appearance outcomes are precisely estimated and close to zero. Pitchers re-optimized quickly by throwing higher, with left-handers catching up to an existing trend, whereas we find no evidence that batters adjusted. The results suggest that the incidence of replacing human judgment with a rule depends on its geometry and on exposure.

### [2] [Occupational Uniqueness and Wage Growth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28133)
*R. Vestrelli,A. Fronzetti Colladon*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出"任务结构"这一新维度，追踪任务在职业间的分布与扩散，区分任务数量变化与职业独特性变化，并揭示二者与工资增长的不同关联。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有职业任务变化测度将职业视为自足的任务束，忽略了任务在经济中的跨职业分布，因而无法区分"新增/流失的是职业专属任务还是已广泛扩散的任务"，掩盖了经济含义迥异的过程。

Method: 构建测量职业变化"任务结构"维度的框架，跨职业、跨时间追踪任务；将任务数量变化与"职业独特性"（职业任务束相对其他职业的独特性）变化分离；使用1977–2018年DOT与O*NET统一化数据集（196,563条任务陈述），测度任务扩散度、职业独特性及任务新增与移除的不同维度。

Result: 职业独特性下降伴随更低工资增长；任务新增与移除的工资关联取决于该任务在其他职业中的普及程度——新增高度扩散的任务与更低工资增长相关，移除职业专属任务亦与更低工资增长相关；但在经济总体层面模式相反：新增新任务与工资增长正相关，移除任务与工资增长负相关。

Conclusion: 职业任务数量的相似变化可能反映经济上不同的过程；纳入任务的跨职业分布能更丰富地解释职业变化及其与工资结构的关系。

Abstract: Changes in occupational task content may have different economic implications depending on whether the tasks gained or lost are occupation-specific or already widespread across the economy. Existing measures of occupational task change largely abstract from this distinction, treating occupations as self-contained task bundles rather than accounting for how tasks are distributed across occupations. We develop a framework to measure a distinct dimension of occupational change, one that goes beyond task expertise or task content: task structure. Using this new framework that tracks tasks across occupations and over time, we separate changes in task quantity from changes in occupational uniqueness, defined as the distinctiveness of an occupation's task bundle relative to other occupations. Using a harmonized dataset of 196563 task statements from the Dictionary of Occupational Titles and O*NET spanning 1977-2018, we measure task diffusion, occupational uniqueness, and distinct dimensions of task addition and removal. Occupations that become less unique are also associated with lower wage growth, and the wage associations of task additions and removals depend on how widely the affected tasks are performed elsewhere. Adding highly diffused tasks is associated with lower wage growth, as is removing occupation-specific tasks. At the economy-wide dimension, however, the pattern differs: the addition of new tasks is positively associated with wage growth, whereas the removal of tasks is negatively associated with it. Taken together, these results show that similar changes in the number of tasks performed by an occupation can reflect economically distinct processes. Incorporating the cross-occupational distribution of tasks, therefore, provides a richer account of occupational change and its relationship to the wage structure.

### [3] [Dynamic portfolio selection under range value-at-risk with logarithmic returns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26919)
*Hui Mi,Yuzhe Pan,Zuo Quan Xu*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 在连续时间市场中，以对数收益衡量绩效与风险，研究均值-RVaR组合选择；通过分位数表示、拉格朗日与凸化方法，用RVaR特定变换权重函数的凸包刻画最优终端财富，并给出两类几何情形下的多区域支付结构及VaR/ES退化关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RV aR作为VaR与ES之间的区间型分位数风险度量，兼具两者特点但缺乏连续时间动态组合选择下的解析刻画；需要厘清其与VaR、ES极限情形的联系，以及区间型分位数风险控制如何塑造状态依赖的最优支付。

Method: 构建连续时间均值-RVaR问题，将动态优化约化为静态分位数问题；利用分位数表示、拉格朗日优化与凸化，借助RVaR特定变换权重函数的凸包刻画最优终端财富；在常数投资机会下推导闭式财富过程与复制组合策略，并进行数值分析。

Result: 凸包呈现两种几何构型：情形1下终端财富为VaR型结构（最小RVaR支付呈数字期权式，0<λ<1时为两段增长最优区域夹一常数支付区域）；情形2下由连接内点与端点的支撑线或含两个内接触点的公共支撑线决定，形成更丰富的多区域结构。RVaR解在β↓α与α=0时分别退化为VaR与ES解。数值显示(α,β,λ)对风险资产暴露的影响非线性且一般非单调；α>0时纯最小RVaR优化可能在极差状态赋予零终端财富，因[0,α)极值分位区间被排除。

Conclusion: 本文给出RV aR下连续时间组合选择的解析刻画，揭示区间型分位数风险控制对状态依赖支付形态的影响机制，并建立RV aR与VaR、ES极限情形的统一联系。

Abstract: This paper studies continuous-time portfolio selection under Range Value-at-Risk (RVaR), with both portfolio performance and risk evaluated in terms of logarithmic returns. We formulate a mean--RVaR problem in a continuous-time financial market and reduce the dynamic optimization to a static quantile problem. Using quantile representation, Lagrangian optimization, and convexification, we characterize the optimal terminal wealth through the convex envelope of an RVaR-specific transformed weighting function.
  The RVaR-specific convex envelope exhibits two distinct geometric configurations. Under Scenario~1, the terminal wealth has a VaR-type structure: the minimum-RVaR payoff is digital-option-like, whereas for $0<λ<1$ it consists of two growth-optimal regions separated by a constant-payoff region. Under Scenario~2, the convex envelope is determined either by a supporting line connecting an interior contact point to the endpoint or by a common supporting line with two interior contact points, leading to a richer multi-region payoff structure. We further show that the RVaR solutions reduce to their VaR and Expected Shortfall (ES) counterparts as $β\downarrowα$ and $α=0$, respectively. Under constant investment opportunities, we also derive closed-form wealth processes and replicating portfolio strategies.
  Numerical results show that the effects of $(α,β,λ)$ on risky-asset exposure are nonlinear and generally non-monotone. In particular, when $α>0$, pure minimum-RVaR optimization may assign zero terminal wealth in sufficiently bad states because the extreme quantile region $[0,α)$ is excluded from the RVaR criterion. These results clarify how interval-based quantile risk control shapes state-dependent portfolio payoffs and connects RVaR with its VaR and ES limiting cases.

### [4] [LOB-Impact Benchmark: Emergent Market Impact in Generative Limit Order Book Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27100)
*George Nigmatulin,Valentin Mohl,Sascha Frey,Kang Li,Aramis Marti-Shahandeh,Mihai Cucuringu,Anisoara Calinescu,Jakob Foerster,Stefan Zohren*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文提出一个针对消息级限价订单簿（LOB）生成器的市场冲击基准，通过向5个自回归模型在5只纳斯达克股票的生成流中注入执行型 metaorder，发现冲击在仅以next-token预测训练的神经LOB生成器中自发涌现，指数为0.63–1.21，略高于平方根律的0.5，但未能观察到弛豫期的冲击衰减。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生成式LOB模型若要作为交易智能体的world model，必须真实再现市场冲击；若模拟器错误刻画该机制，智能体会学到在仿真中最优、在真实市场中有害的策略。现有评估指标仅将生成流与历史数据做分布对比，几乎未考察对智能体自身订单的反事实响应。

Method: 构建三阶段基准：(1) 自由生成，考察订单流持续性与价格扩散性；(2) metaorder注入，在生成的消息流中注入执行型metaorder，并用实证冲击文献的估计量给出冲击律的指数与幅度；(3) 执行后窗口，测量弛豫（relaxation）。以物理机制已知的基线模型作校准，并将每次可见注入与仅模拟器的盲对照相比较，以分离模型学到的响应与订单簿标准机制。

Result: 在全部模型与全部股票上，测量冲击随累计metaorder参与度的响应；生成模型中出现涌现性冲击，指数范围为0.63–1.21，略高于传统平方根律的0.5。在所测模型族中未观察到弛豫期的冲击衰减，作者初步归因于模型上下文窗口仍然偏短。

Conclusion: 市场冲击可自发涌现于仅做next-token预测训练的神经LOB生成器中，但冲击衰减（弛豫）尚未复现，可能受限于上下文长度。基准已开源，用于验证未来面向市场数据的生成模型。

Abstract: Realistic market impact is essential for generative limit order book (LOB) models to serve as world models for trading agents. If a simulator misprices this mechanism, agents learn policies that are optimal in the simulator but wrong in the real market. Existing metrics assess generated streams purely through distributional comparison with historical data, leaving counterfactual response to an agent's own orders largely unexplored. Here, we show that market impact emerges naturally in neural LOB generators trained solely for next-token prediction. We inject execution-style metaorders into the generated message flow of five autoregressive models across five NASDAQ stocks and measure the response using estimators from the empirical impact literature, validated on baselines whose physics is known in advance. Our benchmark evaluates any message-level generator in three phases: free generation (reading order-flow persistence against price diffusivity); metaorder injection (yielding the impact law's exponent and amplitude); and a post-execution window (measuring relaxation). In addition, comparing each visible injection to a blind, simulator-only control isolates the model's learned response from standard book mechanics. For every model on every stock, we measure how the impact responds as a function of cumulative metaorder participation. Interestingly, we observe emergent impact in generative models of market data, with exponents in the range of 0.63 to 1.21, slightly above the traditional 0.5 of the square-root law of impact. In the family of models tested, we are so far unable to observe impact decay during relaxation. We tentatively attribute this to the still-short context windows of the models. We release the benchmark as open source to validate future generative models for market data.

### [5] [Market Regime-Based Multi-Asset Portfolio Optimization Using Nested Clustered Optimization for Tactical Asset Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27751)
*Supriadi,Bambang Siswoyo*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 提出一种"状态感知"战术资产配置（TAA）框架，将高斯HMM市场状态检测与嵌套聚类优化（NCO）结合，在协方差估计、层次聚类与权重优化三阶段注入状态信息，并采用状态转移触发的自适应再平衡，在多资产（印尼国债、LQ45、黄金、CNY/IDR、BTC）月度样本上实现下行风险显著降低（最大回撤4.44%）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统NCO及HRP等层次化配置方法为"状态无关"（regime-agnostic），在跨状态切换时协方差结构与聚类关系发生断裂，导致风险控制失效。作者假设将市场状态信息嵌入NCO流程可改善风险调整后收益并抑制尾部风险。

Method: 以仅使用决策时点前信息拟合的高斯隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）识别Bullish/Sideways/Bearish三状态以避免前视偏差；在每个再平衡时点用状态条件协方差矩阵与聚类结构参数化NCO；以状态转移或最优权重显著变化为触发条件的自适应再平衡替代固定日历再平衡；采用滚动前向（walk-forward）样本外回测，基准包括1/N、买入持有、标准NCO、最小方差、HRP及规则型状态策略；评估指标含累计收益、年化Sharpe、Sortino、最大回撤、Calmar、换手率与净成本收益，并以Jobson-Korkie检验做显著性判断。

Result: 实证显示该状态感知TAA框架有效约束尾部风险，最大回撤为4.44%；HMM状态检测在多资产风险管理中具有实用价值。摘要未报告Sharpe、Sortino、Calmar及Jobson-Korkie检验的具体数值与显著性结论。

Conclusion: 将状态信息嵌入NCO的协方差估计、聚类与优化环节并配合自适应再平衡，可在异构多资产组合中改善下行风险控制，验证了HMM驱动的状态感知配置框架的可行性；但结论的证据强度受限于单一市场样本与摘要中指标披露不全。

Abstract: We propose a regime-aware tactical asset allocation (TAA) framework integrating market regime detection with Nested Clustered Optimization (NCO) for multi-asset portfolio construction. The central hypothesis is that incorporating market regime information into the NCO pipeline, specifically at the stages of covariance estimation, hierarchical clustering, and weight optimization, yields portfolios with improved risk-adjusted performance and reduced downside risk relative to regime-agnostic baselines. Market regimes (Bullish, Sideways/Neutral, Bearish) are detected using a Gaussian Hidden Markov Model (HMM) fitted exclusively on information available up to each decision point, thereby preventing look-ahead bias. Regime-conditional covariance matrices and clustering structures parameterize NCO at each rebalancing event. An adaptive rebalancing rule, triggered by regime transitions or material changes in optimal weights, replaces fixed-calendar rebalancing. The framework is evaluated on a heterogeneous monthly multi-asset universe comprising Indonesian government bonds (SBN), the LQ45 equity index, gold (IDR-denominated), foreign exchange (CNY/IDR), and Bitcoin/IDR (BTC). Out-of-sample performance is assessed via rolling walk-forward backtesting. Benchmarks include equal-weight (1/N), buy-and-hold, standard NCO without regime awareness, minimum variance, hierarchical risk parity (HRP), and a rule-based regime strategy. Performance metrics include cumulative return, annualized Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, maximum drawdown, Calmar ratio, turnover, and net-of-cost returns. Statistical significance is assessed using the Jobson-Korkie test. The empirical analysis indicates that the proposed regime-aware TAA framework successfully limits tail risk, achieving a maximum drawdown of 4.44% and demonstrating the utility of HMM-based regime detection in multi-asset risk management.

</details>
