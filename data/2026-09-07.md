### [1] [Creators Have Difficulty Abandoning Ideas They Generated](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05115)
*Jin Kim,George E. Newman*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究创造力过程中的"生成 vs 选择"两个阶段，发现人们在选择自己的想法时比选择他人的想法更容易提交低质量想法（即"创造性禀赋效应"），该效应在三个任务中均稳定存在，不受信息干预影响，但数周后的再次回顾能提高选择性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 创造力研究中已有大量关于想法生成心理机制的成果，但对想法选择的过程及其与生成之间的交互作用理解不足。作者推测"生成"这一行为本身可能扭曲后续的"选择"过程，从而影响最终产出的质量。

Method: 采用激励相容的实验设计：成对参与者互相查看对方的想法，并因提交高质量想法获得奖励。通过比较参与者从自己生成的想法中选择与从他人想法中选择时的表现，检验创造性禀赋效应；同时设置了信息干预（明确告知效应存在）和时间干预（数月后重新审视想法）两组实验条件。

Result: 研究发现参与者在自己生成的想法中选择时，会提交更多低质量想法（创造性禀赋效应成立）。该效应跨越两个领域的三个任务均稳定存在，且不受信息干预的影响，但数月后重新审视自己的想法能显著提高选择性。

Conclusion: 生成行为会系统性地扭曲选择过程的客观性，导致人们高估自己产出的质量。这一发现对个人决策和组织的创新管理具有重要启示，提示延迟评估或重新审视可能是缓解该偏差的有效策略。

Abstract: Creativity researchers often distinguish between two stages of the creative process: generation versus selection. While much is known about the psychology of idea generation (e.g., the factors that lead to a greater number of novel and useful ideas), less is understood about the nature of selection, or how generation and selection interact. Here we investigate how the act of generating ideas may potentially distort the selection process. Using an incentive-compatible paradigm in which pairs of participants reviewed the same ideas and were rewarded for submitting only high-quality ideas, we find that people submit a greater number of lower-quality ideas when selecting among their own ideas than when selecting among another person's ideas (the Creative Endowment Effect). This effect generalizes across three tasks in two domains and is resistant to an informational intervention (i.e., explicitly telling people about the effect). However, having participants revisit their ideas several months later increases their selectivity. The broader implications for individuals and organizations are discussed.

### [2] [Portfolio Diversification and Concentration under Dependence Uncertainty: A Majorization Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04496)
*Peng Liu,Yang Liu*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文研究在依赖结构不确定性下投资组合多元化与集中化之间的张力。在无模型不确定性时，利用majorization序和双随机矩阵刻画多元化程度，证明拟凸性是风险泛函与majorization序弱一致的充要条件；推导VaR、ES、RVaR和标准差等风险度量的最坏情形不等式并求解稳健组合选择问题。研究发现"集中悖论"：当依赖结构完全模糊时，稳健优化往往建议将资本集中投资于单一资产。最后提出加权稳健公式以在参考依赖结构与最坏情形结构之间插值，为FRTB框架下的多元化与稳健性权衡提供理论基础。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代投资组合理论将分散化视为降低风险的首要工具，但在模型不确定性（尤其是依赖结构不确定性）下，这一基石可能不再最优。文章旨在探讨依赖不确定性下组合分散化与集中化的张力，揭示稳健优化可能导致与经典直觉相反的集中投资结论。

Method: 使用majorization序与双随机矩阵框架形式化分散化程度，证明拟凸性（quasi-convexity）是风险泛函与majorization序弱一致（weakly consistent）的充要条件。在此基础上推导最坏情形风险度量不等式，并针对VaR、ES、RVaR和标准差等风险度量求解稳健投资组合选择问题。进一步提出加权稳健公式，在参考依赖结构和最坏情形结构之间进行插值。

Result: 发现"集中悖论"：对于许多广泛使用的风险泛函，当依赖结构完全模糊（fully ambiguous）时，稳健优化往往建议将投资集中于单一资产以对冲最坏情形依赖场景。加权稳健公式在结构上类似于FRTB中有约束/无约束期望损失（ES）的混合公式，为在模型不确定性下平衡分散化与稳健性提供了理论基础。

Conclusion: 在依赖结构完全不确定的情况下，传统分散化的风险降低作用可能失效，集中投资反而成为对冲最坏依赖情形的稳健策略。加权稳健化公式连接了参考依赖结构与最坏情形结构，为在实践中平衡分散化的传统优势与稳健投资的需求提供了理论框架。

Abstract: Modern portfolio theory identifies diversification as the primary tool for risk reduction. However, under model uncertainty, this cornerstone may no longer remain optimal. This paper investigates the tension between portfolio diversification and concentration under dependence uncertainty. In the absence of model uncertainty, we employ the framework of the majorization order and doubly stochastic matrices to formalize the degree of diversification, and prove that quasi-convexity is a necessary and sufficient property for a risk functional to be weakly consistent with the majorization order. We further derive worst-case risk measure inequalities and solve robust portfolio selection problems for a broad class of risk measures, including VaR, ES, Range-VaR (RVaR), and standard deviation (SD). Our results reveal a ''concentration paradox'' for many widely-used risk functionals: when the dependence structure is fully ambiguous, robust optimization often recommends concentrating investment in a single asset to hedge against the worst-case dependence scenario. As an application, we propose a weighted robustness formulation that interpolates between a reference dependence structure and the worst-case structure. The formulation is structurally analogous to the constrained/unconstrained Expected Shortfall blend in the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB) and provides a theoretical foundation for balancing diversification against robustness in the presence of model uncertainty.

### [3] [Quantity, Risk, and Return](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05162)
*Yu An,Yinan Su,Chen Wang*

Main category: q-fin.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出一个将市场交易量信息纳入因子定价框架的新期望股票收益模型——beta乘以数量（BTQ）。该模型认为股票期望收益不仅取决于因子风险暴露（beta），还取决于交易流引起的因子数量波动（q），结构化BTQ模型能可靠地预测样本外月度股票收益，并解决了因子动物园问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的因子定价模型仅考虑beta（因子风险暴露），忽略trading flows带来的数量（quantity）信息。然而，当成熟投资者吸收了具有高因子载荷股票中的噪声交易流时，他们应当要求更高的因子溢价。因此需要将数量信息纳入定价框架，以揭示在未考虑数量信息时被掩盖的风险-收益关联。

Method: 提出beta乘以数量（BTQ）模型，将股票的期望收益视为因子风险暴露（beta）与因子数量波动（q，由交易流引起）的乘积函数。通过条件化横截面风险-收益关系于数量变量，构建结构化BTQ模型进行样本外预测，并利用因子选择机制筛选少数关键因子。

Result: 无条件状态下近似平坦的横截面风险-收益关联强烈依赖于数量变量；结构化BTQ模型能可靠地实现月度股票收益的样本外预测；通过仅选择少量因子有效解决了因子动物园问题。

Conclusion: BTQ模型为股票收益提供了一个有说服力的基于风险的解释，该解释在未纳入数量信息时是模糊的。该模型不仅增强了横截面收益的可预测性，还通过精简因子数量提高了因子定价框架的实用性。

Abstract: We propose a new model of expected stock returns that incorporates quantity information from market trading activities into the factor pricing framework. We posit that the expected return of a stock is determined by not only its factor risk exposures (beta) but also the factor's quantity fluctuations (q) induced by trading flows, and hence term the model beta times quantity (BTQ). The rationale is that sophisticated investors should demand a higher factor premium when they have absorbed noise trading flows of stocks with high loadings to that factor. The BTQ model provides a compelling risk-based explanation for stock returns, which is otherwise obscured without considering the quantity information. The cross-sectional risk-return association, which is nearly flat unconditionally, strongly depends on the quantity variable. The structured BTQ model reliably predicts monthly stock returns out of sample, and addresses the factor zoo problem by selecting a small number of factors.

</details>
