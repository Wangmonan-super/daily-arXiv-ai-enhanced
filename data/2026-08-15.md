### [1] [Simulating Stress Laws under Extremal Dependence: Characterizing What Generative Models Must Preserve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13056)
*Mantu Gupta,Anand Deo*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文研究多元重尾风险因子驱动的系统压力情景生成问题，证明条件压力分布与最可能压力配置均由同一极限尾分布支配，并据此提出SSGEN（自相似生成估计）方法，通过中间超额学习极值依赖，利用Pareto径向分量外推至更稀有水平。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在多个金融损失同时极端发生的区域，压力分析需要同时关注风险因子的条件分布以及产生这些损失的最可能配置。然而，现有方法未能充分利用二者共享的尾部极限结构，导致稀有事件概率及相关压力情景的估计存在偏差或效率不足。

Method: 首先在理论上证明条件压力分布与最可能配置均由同一极限尾分布决定；在此基础上提出SSGEN方法，从中间超额样本中学习极值依赖结构，并利用Pareto径向分量实现向更稀有水平的外推生成。该框架同时支持反向压力优化，其密度函数刻画了最可能的压力配置。

Result: 证明了保留该极限尾测度可恢复稀有事件概率与缩放后的条件压力分布，而错误设定极值依赖会扭曲联合压力概率；建立了当目标事件样本中不存在时，生成条件分布与数据驱动反向压力解的收敛速率。

Conclusion: 本文揭示了压力情景生成中条件分布与最可能配置之间的统一尾部结构，所提出的SSGEN方法可在目标事件缺失的有限样本下有效外推生成压力情景，为多元重尾风险下的压力测试提供了理论保证与实用工具。

Abstract: We study stress-scenario generation for systems driven by multivariate heavy-tailed risk factors. Within regions where several financial losses are simultaneously extreme, stress analysis concerns both the conditional law of the risk factors and the most plausible configurations producing those losses. We show that both are governed by the same limiting tail law. Preserving its measure recovers rare-event probabilities and scaled conditional stress laws, while misspecifying extremal dependence distorts some regular joint-stress probability. Its density governs reverse-stress optimization, whose maximizers identify the most plausible stress configurations. To exploit this common structure in finite samples, we develop SSGEN (Self-Similar Generative Estimation), which learns extremal dependence from intermediate exceedances and extrapolates to rarer levels using a Pareto radial component. Even when the target event is absent from the sample, we establish convergence rates for the generated conditional law, and data-driven reverse-stress solutions.

### [2] [EU-ETS under attack? The impact of carbon price suppression on the decarbonization of the power sector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12363)
*Javier Gonzalez-Ruiz,Carlos Rodriguez-Pardo,Alice Di Bella,Paolo Mastropietro,Jose Pablo Chavez-Avila,Massimo Tavoni*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文以意大利2026年《Decreto Bollette》法案为案例，使用基于多智能体强化学习的MARLEY模型评估电力市场中抑制碳价信号对投资、排放和消费者成本的长期影响。研究发现：部分抑制碳价信号虽带来短期成本下降，但长期总系统成本影响甚微，且被推迟的排放最终由消费者偿还；除最激进的绿色投资支持方案外，多数情景下CO₂排放上升，因为碳价信号削弱削弱了可再生能源和储能的投资激励。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 欧洲各国正在辩论如何在应对地缘政治引发的能源成本上涨的同时推进脱碳和电气化。意大利2026年Decreto Bollette法案提出从批发电市场中移除部分燃气发电厂投标中的碳价等价成分，作者以该政策为案例，旨在系统性评估抑制碳价信号对电力系统长期投资、排放和消费者成本的影响。

Method: 采用MARLEY框架，一种面向长期电力市场评估的多智能体强化学习方法，构建意大利电力系统的简化模型（stylized model）。在多种配置组合下测试该政策，配置变量包括绿色投资支持力度、资源充足性（resource adequacy）和灵活性（flexibility）水平。

Result: 部分抑制碳价信号在短期内降低成本，但对长期总系统成本仅有轻微影响，因为推迟的排放成本最终由消费者承担。在大多数配置中CO₂排放上升，原因是碳价信号受抑削弱了可再生能源和储能投资激励。仅最激进的绿色投资支持配置能避免排放上升，但该方式通过边缘化批发电价信号实现，需承诺混合市场范式，与所提议价格干预本身的逻辑相矛盾。

Conclusion: 抑制碳价信号的短期成本收益具有误导性，长期来看无法带来实质性系统成本改善且导致排放增加；即便通过激进绿色支持政策规避排放后果，也与价格信号干预的设计初衷相悖。作者暗示此类政策应重新审视，需权衡短期价格缓解与长期脱碳目标之间的矛盾。

Abstract: European countries are debating policies to mitigate the increased energy costs caused by renewed geopolitical tensions, while pursuing decarbonization and electrification. A notable example is Italy's 2026 Decreto Bollette package, which proposes to remove the carbon price equivalent from the bids of certain gas-driven power plants to wholesale electricity markets, among other provisions. We use this as a case study to assess the long-term implications of suppressing the carbon price signal in the electricity market for investment, emissions, and consumer costs. We employ a stylized Italian power system using MARLEY, a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework focused on long-term electricity market assessments. In this framework, we test this policy across configurations with varying levels of support for green investment, resource adequacy, and flexibility. Results show that partial suppression of the carbon price signal yields short-term cost reductions but only a minor long-term effect on total system costs, as the deferred emissions are ultimately repaid by consumers. CO$_2$ emissions rise across most configurations since suppressing the price signal erodes incentives for renewable and storage investment. Only the most ambitious configurations for supporting green investment avoid this outcome, but they do so by marginalizing the wholesale price signal itself, thereby requiring a commitment to a hybrid market paradigm that is in contradiction with the rationale of the proposed price intervention.

### [3] [Does life-satisfaction inequality measure societal inequality? A focal-value-rounding critique](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12667)
*C. P. Barrington-Leigh*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究了生命满意度自报数据中的"焦点值舍入"(FVR)偏差，即受访者将0-10量表简化为{0,5,10}子集的行为，探讨其对主观幸福感不平等测量的影响。研究发现FVR会显著膨胀标准差测量（135个国家中133个受影响，中位数偏差0.090分），且对序数不平等指数的影响更为严重；但各国FVR修正的方向近乎一致，因此国家排名基本保持不变，均值与标准差的负相关关系在修正后仍然成立（仅有适度衰减）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生命满意度的自报分散程度已被用作衡量社会不平等的综合指标，均值与标准差之间的负跨国关联被解读为这种不平等本身具有福利相关性。然而批评者指出了响应量表的非线性和有界性问题。本文进一步揭示了一个此前被忽视的严重问题：大量受访者会系统性地将0-10评分量表简化为{0,5,10}子集，这种"焦点值舍入"行为不仅破坏了量表的线性性，甚至破坏了其序数性，从而可能严重污染基于该数据计算的不平等统计量。

Method: 作者首先从理论上推导了FVR对标准差测量可引入偏差的尖锐上界；然后通过构建FVR行为模型，对盖洛普世界民意调查的坎特里尔阶梯数据以及另外三个跨国生活满意度调查数据进行估计和偏差校正。作者同时比较了FVR对基数型统计量（标准差）和序数型不平等指数的影响，并检验了FVR校正对均值-标准差相关性及分布占优可比性的影响。

Result: 在经验典型的FVR比例下，可能的偏差约为跨国分散程度的跨度的一半。FVR使135个国家中的133个国家的测量标准差被膨胀，中位数偏差为0.090点。但校正几乎在各国符号方向一致，因此标准差排名的排序基本保持不变。序数型不平等指数受到FVR的影响比基数型标准差更为严重。均值与标准差的相关性在FVR校正后仍然存在，仅有适度衰减。FVR校正还能改善完整分布的成对占优可比性。

Conclusion: FVR本身并不会推翻先前报告的均值-标准差负相关关系，但它为谨慎解读主观幸福感不平等的标量测量提供了进一步的理由：候选统计量受到不同程度的污染，序数修正并不比基数原始值更好，且不同统计量彼此之间存在分歧。作者建议对相关不平等指标进行FVR校正以改善分布可比性。

Abstract: The dispersion of self-reported life satisfaction has been proposed and used as a comprehensive measure of societal inequality. A negative cross-country association between mean life satisfaction and its standard deviation has been read as evidence that this inequality is itself welfare-relevant, but critics have pointed to the nonlinearity and boundedness of response scales. I describe a further problem: a substantial and predictable share of respondents simplify the 0--10 scale to the subset {0, 5, 10} --- "focal-value rounding" (FVR) --- a behaviour that breaks not only linearity of the scale but even its order. I derive a sharp bound on the bias that FVR can induce in the standard-deviation; at empirically-typical FVR fractions the possible bias is roughly half the cross-country range of dispersion. I estimate and correct for the bias by fitting a model of FVR behavior to the Gallup World Poll Cantril ladder and three other multi-country life evaluations. FVR inflates measured standard deviation in 133 of 135 countries, by a median of 0.090 points. However, because the corrections are nearly uniform in sign across countries, rankings of the standard deviation survive essentially intact. The ordinal inequality indices advocated as the theoretically appropriate alternative suffer even worse from FVR. The correlation between mean and standard deviation survives FVR correction with modest attenuation and some caveats. Thus FVR does not, by itself, overturn previously reported correlations; it does, however, add a further reason for caution in interpreting scalar measures of subjective wellbeing inequality: candidate statistics are differently contaminated, the ordinal repairs no less than the cardinal originals, and they disagree with one another. Lastly, I show that FVR-correction can improve the pairwise dominance-comparability of full distributions.
