### [1] [The Network Adaptive Market Hypothesis: Detection, Attribution, and Network-Mediated Efficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09208)
*Hitesh Kumar Sahu,Avishek Bhandari,Ipsita Parida*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出“网络自适应市场假说”（NAMH），将市场效率重新表述为由长记忆可预测性与跨市场信息传递共同决定的网络依赖均衡，并以G20股指与五类商品期货（2001–2026）实证刻画信息枢纽、危机期网络谱性质与效率分组的解释力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自适应市场假说（AMH）将效率视为随环境演化的动态属性，但缺乏对“跨市场网络结构”这一维度的刻画；同时，文献常默认系统性危机必然通过更强的网络放大效应（更高的谱半径）体现，此假设缺乏检验。

Method: 构建NAMH理论框架；样本为2001–2026年G20股票指数与五种商品期货；基于长记忆可预测性度量与跨市场信息传递构建网络，计算网络谱半径与模块度；采用方差分解连通性（variance-decomposition connectedness）度量溢出，并考察危机期跨簇溢出；以地理、资产类别与效率分层解释网络连通性。

Result: 日本为信息中心枢纽，美国主要为信息广播者；系统性危机期网络谱半径并未升高，危机期谱贡献主要由澳大利亚与美国主导；方差分解连通性与模块度的关联强于与谱半径的关联，危机期连通性上升主要源于跨簇溢出；效率分组对网络连通性的解释力远弱于地理与资产类别；中等效率层级表现出最强的特质性（idiosyncratic）行为。

Conclusion: 市场效率应理解为网络依赖的均衡而非孤立属性；危机脆弱性未必通过谱半径放大体现，而更与模块结构及跨簇溢出相关；地理与资产类别比效率分层更能解释连通性，中等效率层级最偏离网络共性。

Abstract: We propose the Network Adaptive Market Hypothesis (NAMH), a novel extension of the adaptive markets framework that conceptualizes market efficiency as a network-dependent equilibrium emerging from long-memory predictability and cross-market information transmission. Using G20 equity indices and five commodity futures over 2001-2026, we identify Japan as the central information hub, while the United States primarily acts as an information broadcaster. Contrary to conventional expectations, systemic crises do not exhibit higher network spectral radii, challenging the assumption that crisis-related fragility necessarily manifests through increased network amplification. Instead, crisis-related spectral contributions are dominated by Australia and the United States. Importantly, variance-decomposition connectedness is more closely associated with network modularity than with spectral radius, with crisis-period increases driven primarily by cross-cluster spillovers. Notably, efficiency-based market groupings explain network connectedness only marginally compared with geography and asset class, while the middle efficiency tier consistently exhibits the greatest idiosyncratic behaviour.

### [2] [Sanctions, Evasion, and Deterrence: Evidence from Speeding Enforcement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09337)
*Romain Gauriot,Maria Wisniewska*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文利用澳大利亚昆士兰州罚款大幅上调的外生冲击，因果识别固定罚金与累积记分对超速累犯及责任转移的影响：单纯提高罚金对减少再犯作用甚微，而罚金与记分同时加重可使再犯率下降4.6个百分点；记分加重会显著提高"顶包"式罚单转让，体现策略性责任转移。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 制裁设计如何塑造再犯行为是犯罪经济学与交通治理的核心问题，但既有研究多关注制裁严厉程度的总体效应，较少区分固定罚金与累积记分两类工具的差异化作用，也忽视被处罚者可能通过将罚单转让他人以规避制裁的策略性反应。作者希望厘清两类制裁对再犯的因果效应及其对责任转移行为的影响。

Method: 以澳大利亚昆士兰州一次大幅提高交通制裁为自然实验，采用准实验/因果推断设计（利用政策变动的时间与外生性）估计制裁加重对后续超速违法及"提名其他驾驶人"（罚单转让）的因果影响；分别识别仅提高罚金与罚金叠加额外记分两种情形下的效应，并以罚单重新提名率作为责任转移的代理指标。

Result: 记分提高显著增加罚单重新提名，与策略性"甩锅"一致；仅面临更高罚金的违法者，其再犯下降幅度很小；当更高罚金与额外记分同时实施时，再犯率大幅下降4.6个百分点。

Conclusion: 制裁的威慑效果取决于工具组合而非单一严厉程度：金钱制裁单独作用有限，记分等非货币、可累积的制裁才能产生显著威慑；同时须警惕记分加重诱发的责任转移规避行为，政策设计应兼顾威慑有效性与规避渠道的封堵。

Abstract: The design of sanctions plays a central role in shaping reoffending behavior. We study the effects of fixed monetary fines and accumulating penalty points on speeding recidivism and liability transfer. Exploiting a large increase in sanctions in Queensland, Australia, we estimate the causal effects of these sanctions on subsequent offending and nomination of other drivers. Higher demerit points increase fine renomination, consistent with strategic blame shifting. Estimated reductions in reoffending are small among offenders facing higher fines alone. Reoffending falls by a substantial 4.6 percentage points when higher fines are accompanied by additional penalty points.

### [3] [Portfolio-aware scenario transfer of reserve movements: evidence from Lloyd's syndicates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08968)
*Colin Priest*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 基于117个劳合社辛迪加2015-2024年632个辛迪加-年样本，估计"理赔进展/准备金"这一冲击指标在组合规模间的转换方式；规模依赖的尺度参数在样本外表现显著优于单一市场尺度，但转换后的上尾对最小账簿仍低估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 一个组合上开展的理赔进展情景可能无法直接作为另一规模组合的压力情景使用；需要可检验、可复核的情景可比性（而非校准资本分位数或下一年度预测）。

Method: 测量指标为选定较早年承保年度的毛理赔估计变动，除以期初毛理赔未决额加上当年承接的准备金（含其进展年度）；并非期初准备金恶化的纯净度量。用632个辛迪加-年数据估计规模相关的转换尺度，并与单一市场尺度比较样本外表现，另检验平方根尺度。

Result: 指标离散度随准备金规模显著下降；规模尺度对留出辛迪加的预测远优于单一市场尺度。但转换后的上尾低估最小账簿：最小准备金规模四分位中158个留出观测有6个超过转换后99.5分位（期望0.79，来自3个辛迪加）。平方根尺度在99%和99.5%分位上样本外pinball损失描述性更低，但在最大账簿上漏报更多。GBP 5亿组合的转换经验99.5分位为准备金的0.34（95%可信区间0.23-0.52，以拟合模型为条件），而原始市场池为0.67，二者均为Syndicate 5820 2016年的变动。

Conclusion: 贡献在于可检查的情景可比性，而非校准的资本分位数或下一年度预测；小账簿上尾风险需谨慎对待。

Abstract: A claims-development movement on one portfolio may not transfer directly as a stress to another size. We estimate how to transfer such scenarios between portfolio sizes, using 632 syndicate-years from 117 Lloyd's syndicates over 2015-2024. The measured movement is the change in gross claims estimates on selected older underwriting cohorts, divided by opening gross claims outstanding plus reserves taken on in the year whose development it includes; it is not a pure measure of opening-reserve deterioration. Its dispersion falls materially with reserve size, and a size-based scale predicts held-out syndicates far better than a single market scale. But the transferred upper tail understates the smallest books: in the smallest reserve-size quartile, six of 158 held-out observations exceeded the transferred 99.5th percentile, against 0.79 expected, from three syndicates. Square-root scaling has descriptively lower held-out pinball loss at 99% and 99.5%, though more misses among the largest books. For a GBP 500m portfolio the transferred empirical 99.5th percentile is 0.34 of reserves (95% credible interval, conditional on the fitted model, 0.23-0.52), against 0.67 in the raw market pool. Both are Syndicate 5820's 2016 movement. The contribution is inspectable scenario comparability, not a calibrated capital quantile or a next-year forecast.

</details>


### [4] [Joint modelling of financial cost and carbon footprint in motor insurance claims: a BINAR(1) decision framework for circular auto parts adoption](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09213)
*Charles Minier,Stéphane Loisel,Renata Alcoforado*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文提出二元离散时间BINAR(1)框架，联合刻画车险理赔成本与维修碳足迹的动态，并基于新件/再利用件（PIEC）情景分析评估二者权衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 车险理赔管理面临双重目标：控制理赔成本与降低维修决策相关的温室气体排放，但二者在随机建模中鲜有联合刻画。

Method: 采用受BINAR(1)依赖结构启发的二元离散时间模型，允许时间持续性、跨维度相依及正负相关；结合外部排放因子与替换件成本数据，将理赔碳足迹在"新件"与"循环经济再利用件（PIEC）"两条维修路径下量化，并对PIEC接受率设定多情景分析。

Result: 较高PIEC接受率可同时降低预期排放与平均理赔成本；联合收益大小取决于BINAR(1)过程所刻画的相依结构与持续性。

Conclusion: 该框架为保险公司提供透明、可复现的工具，用以评估维修政策杠杆，在保留显式随机风险视角的同时使理赔运营与脱碳目标对齐。

Abstract: Motor insurance claims management increasingly faces a dual objective: controlling claim costs while reducing greenhouse-gas emissions associated with vehicle repair decisions. This paper proposes a bivariate, discrete-time modelling framework that links the dynamics of financial claims and repair-related carbon footprint inspired by a BINAR(1) dependence structure, allowing for temporal persistence, cross-dependence and a positive/negative correlation between the two dimensions. Using externally sourced emission factors and cost information for replacement parts, we operationalise the carbon footprint of claims under two repair pathways: new parts versus circular-economy reused parts (PIEC). We then conduct scenario analyses in which the acceptance rate of reused parts varies, and we quantify the joint implications for expected monetary outlays and CO 2 -equivalent emissions. Results show that higher PIEC acceptance can simultaneously reduce expected emissions and average claim cost, and the magnitude of the joint benefit depends on the dependence structure and persistence captured by the BINAR(1) process. The proposed framework provides a transparent, reproducible tool for insurers to evaluate repair-policy levers and to align claims operations with decarbonisation targets while retaining an explicit stochastic risk perspective.

### [5] [The Logic of Factor Portfolios: Boolean Rules, Interactions, and Trading Costs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09834)
*Ziyao Wang,Svetlozar T. Rachev,Frank J. Fabozzi*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 将投资规则视为信号排名阈值陈述的布尔函数，用Walsh分解把规则收益拆分为单信号暴露与信号间交互暴露，并证明同一组系数结合底层陈述的持续性决定换手率；实证显示交互层级越高越不持续、换手越高，且交互组合在扣除交易成本后仍具超额收益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 投资者通过"买入前十分位""持有高动量低波动""剔除未通过筛选"等决策规则把信号转化为持仓，但这些规则往往是关于信号排名的阈值陈述的布尔函数，其结构（规则包含什么、交易频率多高）缺乏统一的形式化刻画，规则收益如何分解为单信号与交互成分、以及为何多数规则换手高、交互组合交易成本高却仍可能有效，均缺少理论解释。

Method: 1) 将规则表述为阈值陈述的布尔函数，从而同时刻画规则持仓与交易频率；2) 采用Walsh分解把任意规则收益拆解为单信号暴露与信号间交互暴露（如两个筛选的交集与并集具有相反的交互暴露）；3) 用同一组Walsh系数叠加底层陈述的持续性预测换手率；4) 用1973–2024年美国股票数据进行实证检验，比较阈值实现与平滑实现（排名乘积）的持续性差异，并基于1995–2024报价价差成本检验净溢价与样本外表现（Sharpe比率提升、六因子模型检验）。

Result: 预测与实现的持续性相关系数不低于0.999；预测换手率对实现换手率的回归斜率为1.02；同一交互以排名乘积形式比阈值规则形式更持续；交易成本随交互阶数上升而增加、溢价随阶数下降，1995–2024年绝对溢价高于报价价差成本的比例：单信号组合58%、双信号交互24%、三信号交互仅6%；样本外交互组合提升单信号切点组合的Sharpe比率，且在1995–2024年拒绝六因子模型，而基于相同信号的传统排序组合无法拒绝。

Conclusion: 规则即阈值陈述的布尔函数这一视角，配合Walsh分解，能精确预测规则的收益结构与换手行为；高阶交互因持续性差而换手高、阈值化因丢弃信息而加剧交易，交互组合在考虑交易成本后仍具经济与统计显著性，为多信号规则设计提供了可量化的理论框架。

Abstract: Investors turn signals into holdings through decision rules: buy the top decile, hold stocks with high momentum and low volatility, exclude stocks that fail a screen. Many such rules are Boolean functions of threshold statements about signal ranks, and writing them this way shows both what a rule holds and how often it trades. A Walsh decomposition splits the return of any rule into exposures to individual signals and to interactions among signals; an intersection and a union of the same two screens, for example, have opposite interaction exposures. The same coefficients, together with the persistence of the underlying statements, determine turnover. Interactions are less persistent than their components, so rules that load on higher-order interactions trade more, and thresholds discard information, so at equal risk threshold implementations of interactions trade more than smooth ones. In U.S. stocks from 1973 to 2024, these predictions hold closely: predicted and realized persistence have correlations of at least 0.999, predicted turnover matches realized turnover with a slope of 1.02, and the same interactions are more persistent as products of ranks than as threshold rules. Trading costs rise with order, and because premia fall with order, 58% of single-signal portfolios have absolute premia above their quoted-spread costs over 1995-2024, against 24% of two-signal and 6% of three-signal interaction portfolios. Out of sample, interaction portfolios raise the Sharpe ratio of single-signal tangency portfolios, and over 1995-2024 they reject a six-factor model that conventional sorts on the same signals do not reject.

</details>
