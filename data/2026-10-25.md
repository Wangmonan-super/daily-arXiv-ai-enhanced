### [1] [Intraday Forecasting Ability and Liquidity Provision of Institutional Traders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31141)
*Xinlei Hao,Jiajun Guo,Jussi Keppo,Xianhua Peng*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 利用高频交易数据，论文发现机构自营交易者具有持续的预测能力，且预测能力与流动性提供均显著提升日内匹配头寸的盈亏。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 厘清机构自营交易者日内盈利的来源：究竟来自"预测能力"还是"流动性提供"，二者对已实现盈亏的相对贡献缺乏基于匹配头寸的直接证据。

Method: 使用单一交易所的高频数据；构造基于熟练交易者成交流的假设性"跟随流"策略检验预测能力的持续性；将买入与卖出成交匹配为头寸，把已实现P&L回归到期限匹配的预测能力代理变量及两笔成交的流动性角色上；以匹配头寸内被动成交次数（0/1/2）度量流动性提供强度。

Result: 跟随流策略在不同交易频率下年化Sharpe比率为16–63；预测能力代理变量每提高一个标准差，每个匹配头寸P&L提高1.02–1.19货币单位；相比无被动成交，1次被动成交对应P&L高1.28–1.31单位，2次较1次再高3.79–3.82单位。

Conclusion: 机构自营交易者的日内盈利同时源于持续的预测能力与流动性提供，且流动性提供（被动成交次数）带来的边际财务收益尤为显著。

Abstract: This study uses a high-frequency dataset from a stock exchange to examine how forecasting ability and liquidity provision relate to institutional traders' proprietary intraday profitability. We discover persistent forecasting abilities among traders. Specifically, we demonstrate that hypothetical flow-following strategies based on the execution flows of these skillful traders yield annualized Sharpe ratios ranging from 16 to 63 across various trading frequencies. We match buy and sell executions into positions, linking realized profit and loss (P&L) to horizon-matched forecasting proxies and the liquidity roles of both executions. Our findings indicate a direct correlation between improved forecasting ability and profitability, where a one-standard-deviation increase in the forecasting ability proxy is associated with 1.02 -- 1.19 currency units higher P&L per matched position. Additionally, we measure liquidity-provision intensity within a matched position by its number of passive executions: zero, one, or two. In these regressions, P&L is 1.28 -- 1.31 currency units higher for one passive execution than for none, and a further 3.79 -- 3.82 higher for two than for one, underscoring the significant financial benefits of liquidity provision.

### [2] [Multiple Stopping and Exercise Boundaries for American Put Options](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30637)
*Katsunori Ano*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 研究有限期限、最多可行使m次且相邻行权须间隔耐火期δ的美式看跌期权；证明最优行权由单一、按剩余权利数排序的行权边界刻画，并给出一系列解析刻画与验证定理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典美式看跌期权仅允许一次行权，而带有多次行权权利且行权之间存在强制等待期（耐火期）的合约在实际中并不少见，其最优行权结构与边界刻画尚缺乏系统分析，特别是有限期限下未用权利会过期这一情形。

Method: 在风险中性Black–Scholes框架下，按剩余权利数构造值函数并进行归纳分析；证明首个边界击中时间的最优性及边界按权利数的单调排序；证明各截止期条带内行权边界的局部Lipschitz连续性；在远离截止期处导出光滑拟合、拟变分不等式与自由边界形式、提前行权溢价表示、递归Volterra型边界方程和验证定理；并将递归形式推广至独立随机终止时间。

Result: 得到以剩余权利数参数化的一族单一、有序行权边界；验证首个边界击中时间最优；获得边界的局部Lipschitz正则性；建立QVI/自由边界刻画、提前行权溢价公式、递归Volterra方程与验证定理；并给出可引入外生随机终止的扩展递归形式。

Conclusion: 多次行权、带耐火期的美式看跌期权具有清晰的单边界结构与良定的自由边界刻画，递归Volterra方程可作为数值求解边界的基础，框架可自然扩展至含外生随机终止的合约。

Abstract: This article studies a finite-horizon American put option with up to $m$ exercise rights under the risk-neutral Black--Scholes model, where consecutive exercises must be separated by a prescribed refractory period $δ>0$ and unused rights may expire. For each number of remaining rights, we show that optimal exercise is characterized by a single exercise boundary and that these boundaries are ordered by the number of remaining rights. We prove optimality of the first boundary-hitting time and establish local Lipschitz continuity of the exercise boundaries within each open deadline strip. Away from the deadlines, we derive smooth fit, the associated quasi-variational inequality and free-boundary formulation, an early-exercise-premium representation, recursive Volterra-type boundary equations, and a verification theorem. We also extend the recursive formulation to an independent random termination time, thereby allowing for exogenous termination of the option contract.

### [3] [Directionally Correct Views in Portfolio Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31074)
*Ojas Kumar,Sreejit Dey,Sarvesh Ravichandran Iyer,Sandeep Juneja*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 借鉴Black-Litterman框架引入专家观点，证明少量方向正确的信息即可显著改善Markowitz型组合配置，且纳入方向正确专家后简单Markowitz型模型与端到端模型表现相当。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 组合优化常用历史数据估计Markowitz型或机器学习模型参数，但需要一种方法使可得数据与投资期限内的预期条件相对齐；受Black-Litterman框架启发，作者探讨融入专家观点能否带来更优的配置。

Method: 在简单Markowitz设定下进行理论分析，说明少量方向正确的信息对决策的改善；并讨论借助熵（entropy）思想将专家观点融入端到端组合模型。最后通过实证比较含专家的简单Markowitz型模型与端到端模型。

Result: 理论结果表明，即便极小量的方向正确信息也能实质性改善投资决策；实证结果显示，在纳入方向正确的专家观点后，较简单的Markowitz型模型与端到端模型表现相当。

Conclusion: 专家观点的方向性正确性比模型复杂度更关键；BL式观点融合在简单模型中已能取得与端到端学习模型相当的效果，提示实际应用不必过度依赖复杂模型。

Abstract: Portfolio optimization techniques often use historical data to estimate parameters for Markowitz-type or ML models. Effective allocation requires a way to align the available data with conditions expected over the investment horizon. Inspired by the Black-Litterman framework, we ask whether incorporating expert views leads to better portfolio allocations. In a simple Markowitz setting, we show that even a small amount of directionally correct information can materially improve investment decisions. We also discuss how expert views can be incorporated in end-to-end portfolio models using entropy-based ideas. Somewhat surprisingly, our empirical results indicate that simpler Markowitz-type model perform as well as an end-to-end model, once directionally correct experts are incorporated.

### [4] [Austerity, Gender, and Multidimensional Deprivation Among Older Europeans: Evidence from SHARE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30527)
*Nita Handastya*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 财政整固（紧缩）总体上会加剧老年女性的多维剥夺，但性别差距的分布既不均匀（集中于南欧与东欧），也不通过收入不安全感渠道传导，且结论对聚类层级敏感。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检验财政整固是否为老年女性带来比老年男性更深的多维剥夺，并识别其作用渠道，弥合"紧缩性别成本"文献在老年群体与多维福利测度上的空白。

Method: 使用SHARE六轮、11个欧洲国家的数据，构建涵盖功能性健康、经济不安全感与社会排斥的多维剥夺指数；以周期性调整初级余额（CAPB）为国家-轮次层面的解释变量，采用双向固定效应模型估计，并对比个体层面与国家层面聚类推断。

Result: 平均而言整固提高剥夺水平，但平均性别差距为零；该零值掩盖了区域异质性——南欧与东欧性别差距显著为正，大陆/北欧不显著。经济不安全感渠道方向与文献预期相反，男性不安全感上升更陡，且是唯一在国家层面聚类下交互项仍显著的维度；社会排斥的性别效应更大、集中于东欧与南欧，但仅在个体聚类下显著。

Conclusion: 老年欧洲人紧缩的性别化代价既不跨福利体制统一，也并非经由收入渠道传导；社会渠道的证据尚不充分，需更强的国家层面推断与更细的机制识别。

Abstract: This paper studies whether fiscal consolidation is associated with deeper multidimensional deprivation among older women than among older men, and through which channel. Using six waves of SHARE across eleven European countries, it builds a multidimensional deprivation index spanning functional health, economic insecurity, and social exclusion, and relates it to the cyclically adjusted primary balance (CAPB) at the country-wave level in a two-way fixed-effects model. Consolidation raises deprivation on average with a null average gender gap, which is later found to conceal regional heterogeneity where the gender gap is positive and significant in Southern and Eastern Europe, and absent in Continental/Nordic Europe. This regional pattern holds under individual clustering but does not survive clustering at the country level. Further, the economic insecurity channel the literature would predict runs in the opposite direction, with men's measured insecurity rising more steeply than women's; this is the only dimension whose interaction survives country-level inference. Social exclusion exhibits a larger gender effect, concentrated in the Eastern and Southern Europe, but it is significant only under individual clustering. The gendered cost of austerity among older Europeans is therefore neither uniform across welfare regimes nor routed through income, though the evidence for its social channel is inconclusive.

### [5] [The Delegation Divide: An Open Index of AI-Agent Delegability across Occupations and Countries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30752)
*Cristian Espinal Maya,Santiago Jiménez Londoño*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 论文构建"Agent Delegability Index (ADI)"，用两个语言模型按预注册的委托层级与障碍量表对O*NET 30.2全部18,796条任务语句评分，映射到433个ISCO-08单元组并按146国ILOSTAT就业加权，首次给出全球口径的AI代理任务可委托性度量及"委托鸿沟"证据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有暴露度指标（如Eloundou等、Felten等）主要衡量对生成式AI的"暴露"，且多聚焦美国，无法刻画任务能否真正交给AI代理执行、以何种层级委托、受何障碍限制，也缺乏全球可比视角。

Method: 构建ADI：两模型在冻结、预注册的委托层级与障碍量规上对18,796条O*NET 30.2任务语句打分；任务映射至433个ISCO-08单元组，按ILOSTAT 146国就业加权；与Eloundou、Felten指标做职业层面相关性检验，并以300条任务的盲评人类评分、带网络搜索的代理式评审面板做一致性验证（kappa）。

Result: 约35.2%任务时间可至少部分委托、仅2.5%可完全委托（2026）；可委托就业占比随收入上升，低收入22.5%→高收入48.7%，每对数单位人均GDP斜率7.23个百分点，差异呈组合性、以体力障碍为主；与Eloundou r=0.93、Felten r=0.89，超过预注册0.80下界；完全委托峰值对应人类约11.8分钟任务；101国中可委托性与观测AI使用相关r=0.86，中等收入国家差距并不更大（p=0.27），预注册的"采纳缺口"未获支持；模型间kappa=0.77，人类盲评一致性kappa=0.85，代理面板kappa=0.87。

Conclusion: ADI在暴露度之上补充了委托层级与障碍维度，提供全球口径度量而非新的职业排序；"委托鸿沟"主要源于职业结构而非采纳差距，完全委托仍属少数。作者开源指数、图谱、提示词与MCP服务器。

Abstract: 35.2% of task time in world employment is at least partly delegable to AI agents in 2026, and only 2.5% can be handed over in full. Existing measures score exposure to generative AI, mostly for the United States. We build the Agent Delegability Index (ADI): two language models rate all 18,796 O*NET 30.2 task statements on a frozen, preregistered rubric of delegation levels and barriers; tasks are mapped to 433 ISCO-08 unit groups and weighted by ILOSTAT employment in 146 countries. Four findings. First, delegable employment rises with income, from 22.5% in low-income to 48.7% in high-income countries, a slope of 7.23 percentage points per log point of GDP per capita. This delegation divide is compositional: the physical barrier dominates the jobs poorer countries employ most. Second, at the occupation level ADI tracks exposure closely (r = 0.93 with Eloundou et al. and 0.89 with Felten et al.), above the preregistered bound of 0.80; ADI adds delegation levels and barriers on top of exposure, for the world, not a new ranking of occupations. Third, full delegation is rare and peaks for tasks that take a human about twelve minutes (11.8 minutes). Fourth, the preregistered adoption gap is not supported: across 101 countries, delegability and observed AI usage correlate at r = 0.86, and the gap between them is not wider in middle-income countries (p = 0.27). Model agreement is kappa = 0.77; a blind human rating of 300 tasks agrees with the model consensus at kappa = 0.85, and an agentic panel with web search at kappa = 0.87. We release the index, the atlas, the prompts and an MCP server at https://github.com/Cespial/delegation-divide.

### [6] [The Reaction of Financial Markets to the Releases of DeepSeek R1 and Kimi K3: A Critical Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30886)
*Marco Taboga*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 对比 DeepSeek R1(2025.1)与 Kimi K3(2026.7)发布的市场反应，论证开放权重模型是在 AI 价值链上重新分配租金而非消灭租金，在位者受互补资产保护。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 两个模型均被呈现为接近能力前沿的中国竞争者可对比，但传递的经济信息不同（R1=开放权重+超低成本，K3=前沿定价）。作者希望纠正"开放模型=行业利润归零"的简单解读，并为投资者与政策制定者提供更准确的风险评估框架。

Method: 描述性事件窗口分析（两次发布前后的股价反应）+ 模型能力与成本对比 + 后续产业发展证据 + 开源软件历史扩散的类比推演。

Result: R1 引发半导体与数据中心相关企业大幅下跌（市场下修算力基础设施预期需求）；K3 反应更集中于中国竞争性 AI 实验室，行业整体冲击有限。

Conclusion: 开放权重模型重新分配而非消除 AI 价值链租金；在位企业依凭分发渠道、产品捆绑、转换成本、专有数据、推理服务容量与合约信任等互补资产保持防护。评估风险应兼顾模型能力与互补资产的所有权及持久性。

Abstract: This paper compares financial-market reactions to the releases of DeepSeek R1 in January 2025 and Kimi K3 in July 2026. Both AI models were presented as credible Chinese competitors near the capability frontier, but their releases conveyed different economic information. DeepSeek R1 combined open weights with unusually low reported training and inference costs, prompting investors to reassess expected demand for computing infrastructure and producing large stock-price decreases among semiconductor and data-centre-related firms. Kimi K3 instead entered at broadly frontier prices and generated a market reaction concentrated more heavily among rival Chinese AI laboratories. Drawing on descriptive event-window evidence, model capability and cost comparisons, subsequent industry developments, and the historical diffusion of open-source software, the paper argues that open-weight models redistribute rents across the AI value chain rather than eliminating them. Incumbents remain protected by complementary assets including distribution, product bundling, switching costs, proprietary data, serving capacity, and contractual trust. The analysis suggests that investors and policy makers should assess risks looking not only at model capability, but also at the ownership and durability of complementary assets across the AI value chain.
