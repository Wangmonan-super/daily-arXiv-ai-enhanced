### [1] [Pricing the DeFi Tail: Do Protocols or Depositors Price Operational Risk?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00911)
*Nils Bundi*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文研究DeFi协议中存款人面临的运营风险。与银行不同，DeFi协议无需持有资本缓冲来覆盖运营风险，该风险直接落在存款人身上。作者用巴塞尔损失分布方法量化风险尾部，发现借贷(Lending)尾部隐含的VaR_99.9资本缓冲占TVL的18%，且大多数头部借贷协议缓冲不足。市场虽对无缓冲协议要求更高收益率（中位数差125个基点），但溢价远低于充分定价的尾部风险。作者建议采取信息披露而非资本强制要求来保护存款人。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DeFi协议与银行类似，使存款人暴露于运营风险（自2020年以来1075起事件累计损失94.5亿美元），但与银行不同，DeFi协议无需持有资本覆盖此类风险。协议可自愿维持缓冲，若无缓冲则风险完全落在缺乏信息与定价能力的零售存款人身上。因此作者希望量化这一尾部风险并检验市场定价是否充分。

Method: 作者采用巴塞尔损失分布法（按部门划分），应用于新构建的运营风险事件数据集来量化尾部风险；通过拟合各核心部门的尾部指数检验风险边际；并比较有/无资本缓冲的借贷平台收益率溢价差异，评估市场纪律的有效性。

Result: 四个核心部门的尾部不重于Moscadelli银行业区间[0.85,1.39]；而Bridge、Derivatives和residual Other部门呈现网络犯罪级别的尾部（hat\xi约1.6），点估计超过无穷均值边界。Lending部门的尾部意味着VaR_99.9资本缓冲占TVL的18%，但前十家借贷平台中仅四家持有缓冲，平均仅覆盖其5%。无缓冲的平台收益率溢价中位数高于有缓冲平台125个基点，但该溢价远不足以充分定价尾部风险。

Conclusion: 市场虽有方向正确的辨识能力（无缓冲平台支付更高溢价），但定价远不充分，未定价的尾部风险不成比例地落在缺乏信息和技能的零售存款人身上。由于这些产品不受银行监管，作者建议以信息披露而非资本强制为主：协议及其前端服务提供商应公布标准化的损失数据、现有资本缓冲及尾部覆盖率。

Abstract: Similar to banks, DeFi protocols expose depositors to operational risk (USD 9.45 billion across 1,075 events since 2020). Unlike banks, they are not required to hold capital against it. A protocol may maintain a buffer voluntarily. Absent one, the risk falls on the depositor, who should then demand a risk premium in the supply yield. I quantify the underlying tail on one benchmark, a per-sector Basel loss-distribution approach fitted to a new operational risk event dataset, and test both margins against it. Tails in the four core sectors are no heavier than the Moscadelli banking band $[0.85, 1.39]$. Bridge, Derivatives, and the residual Other sector exhibit cyber-loss-level tails ($\hatξ\approx 1.6$), with point estimates past the infinite-mean boundary. The Lending tail implies a $\mathrm{VaR}_{99.9}$ capital buffer of 18% of TVL and of the ten largest Lending venues, the four holding a buffer cover on average 5% of it. Under market discipline, depositors should demand a higher yield in compensation where a venue does not maintain a buffer. I find that venues without a buffer pay a higher premium than those with (a 125-bps gap in medians): evidence the market discriminates in the right direction. However, the premium falls far short of an adequately priced tail. This unpriced tail falls disproportionately on the retail depositor, who sees only the posted rate but lacks the information and skills to price it. Because these products are not bank-regulated, I recommend disclosure over capital mandates: protocols, and any service providers that front access to it, should publish standardized losses, existing capital buffers and tail coverage.

### [2] [Illiquidity at Risk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00943)
*Demetrio Lacava,Paolo Santucci de Magistris*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文提出一个新的尾部风险指标 Illiquidity-at-Risk (IlliQaR)，用于量化极端流动性枯竭的规模。基于实现型Amihud指标（高频数据计算的已实现波动率与交易量之比），评估了线性和非线性计量模型的预测能力，特别关注不连续跳跃成分的影响。对S&P 500指数和25只美国大盘股的实证分析表明：纳入跳跃项能显著改善流动性预测，且在系统性压力时期，连续模型会系统性低估流动性蒸发的严重程度。研究发现个股IlliQaR违规事件往往在S&P 500流动性压力期间聚集，表明流动性枯竭不仅是个股局部问题，更是系统性风险，大盘指数可作为个股极端流动性枯竭的领先指标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 市场效率从根本上依赖于稳定的流动性，因此预测流动性动态对投资者和监管者都至关重要。现有文献缺乏专门用于量化极端流动性枯竭（dry-ups）尾部风险的指标，且尚未系统评估跳跃成分在极端流动性预测中的作用。

Method: 构建基于实现型Amihud指标的IlliQaR尾部风险度量，比较多种线性和非线性计量经济模型（含/不含跳跃成分）的极端流动性预测能力，并利用S&P 500及25只大盘股的实证数据进行验证。

Result: 纳入跳跃成分对实现精确的概率覆盖率和更好的IlliQaR预测至关重要，尤其系统性压力时期连续模型会低估流动性蒸发严重性。个股IlliQaR违规在S&P 500流动性压力期间聚集，指数可作为个股极端流动性枯竭的领先指标。

Conclusion: Illiquidity-at-Risk不仅是局部风险，更是系统性风险。跳跃是极端流动性预测不可或缺的组成部分，S&P 500指数在个股流动性极端枯竭中具有先行指示作用。

Abstract: Market efficiency relies fundamentally on stable liquidity. Consequently, forecasting liquidity dynamics is a priority for both investors and regulators. We introduce a new tail-risk metric, Illiquidity-at-Risk (IlliQaR), designed to quantify the magnitude of extreme liquidity dry-ups. Relying upon the realized Amihud (a precise illiquidity measurement derived from high-frequency data as the ratio of realized volatility to trading volume) we assess the predictive power of various linear and non-linear econometric models, with a specific focus on the impact of discontinuous jump components. Accounting for these jumps is essential for achieving accurate probability coverage and better IlliQaR predictions during periods of systemic stress, where standard continuous models systematically underestimate the severity of liquidity evaporation. Our empirical analysis, encompassing the S&P 500 index and a cross-section of 25 large U.S. equities, demonstrates that incorporating jumps significantly improves forecasts of illiquidity. Our results suggest that individual stock IlliQaR violations often cluster during periods of S&P 500 liquidity stress. This indicates that Illiquidity at Risk is not just a localized concern but a systemic one, where the main index acts as a leading indicator for extreme dry-ups in individual stock liquidity.

### [3] [AI and the Economy: An Economic Examination of Production, Distribution, Firms, Labor, and Welfare](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01263)
*Ali Zeytoon-Nejad*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文从经济学视角全面审视人工智能（AI），将其定位为一种多重属性的经济现象——既是资本、合成劳动力、通用目的技术，也是经济基础设施。文章分析了AI通过自动化、增强、优化、预测和创新等多渠道驱动生产率增长，并系统探讨了其对劳动力市场、竞争结构、市场集中度、创业生态及社会福利的影响，最后提出政策建议以平衡AI的收益与潜在风险。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者旨在填补从经济学理论框架系统解读AI的空白。AI不仅是一项技术革新，更已演变为深刻影响生产、就业、市场结构和长期增长的经济现象，需要超越单纯的技术分析视角，从多维度理解其经济影响机制，为学术研究和公共政策制定提供理论依据。

Method: 本文采用规范性经济分析（essay式理论论述），通过多学科交叉视角综合阐述AI的经济属性。具体而言，作者构建了四重性质的分析框架（资本、合成劳动力、通用目的技术、经济基础设施），并沿生产率增长渠道、劳动力市场影响、竞争与市场结构、福利效应及政策启示等维度展开系统论证。

Result: 分析表明：AI是生产率增长的多渠道引擎，通过自动化、增强、优化、预测和创新推动产出增加与成本降低；AI同时通过劳动力替代、劳动力互补和创造性破坏重塑就业格局；AI影响竞争动态和市场集中度，兼具促进与抑制竞争的双向效应；AI对各经济主体（消费者、生产者、工人、政府及社会整体）产生广泛的福利影响。

Conclusion: 作者认为，应最大化AI的潜在收益（如加速经济增长、激发创业活力、提升福利水平），同时通过审慎的政策设计缓解其不利后果（如劳动力替代带来的结构性失业、市场集中度上升的风险等），实现AI赋能经济的良性发展路径。

Abstract: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly transforming economic systems by altering production processes, labor markets, and the structure of firms and industries. AI is not merely a technological innovation. It is fundamentally a major economic phenomenon and a new wave of innovation with important implications for productivity, employment, market structure, public policy, long-run economic growth, and collective welfare. This essay examines the economics of AI by analyzing the multiple channels through which AI influences economic activity and societal well-being. It argues that AI should be understood simultaneously as a form of capital, a form of synthetic labor, a general-purpose technology, as well as an economic infrastructure. The analysis shows that AI functions as a multi-channel engine of productivity growth through automation, augmentation, optimization, prediction, and innovation. It is argued that AI has the potential to increase output, reduce costs, stimulate entrepreneurship, and accelerate economic growth, while also reshaping labor markets through labor substitution, labor complementarity, and creative destruction. The essay further examines AI's effects on competition, market concentration, and entrepreneurship. Finally, it explores the welfare implications of AI for consumers, producers, workers, governments, and society as a whole, and concludes by outlining policy considerations aimed at maximizing the benefits of AI while mitigating its potential adverse consequences.
