### [1] [Constrained defined-contribution pension management: A reparameterized neural network approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06495)
*Yueman Feng,Wenyuan Li*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 研究两因子市场下带一般凸交易约束的DC养老金管理，含长寿风险保险，用人工市场+对偶+重参数化神经网络求上下界，并实证约束显著改变配置。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现实养老金账户交易常受禁止卖空、禁止借贷、组合比例等限制，而传统文献多假设无约束，导致解在实际中可能产生误导性指导。

Method: 二因子金融市场（现金、两只名义债券、通胀挂钩债券、股票指数）加定期寿险；约束集为一般非空闭凸集；构造调整风险资产漂移项的无约束人工市场满足原约束；经对偶变换得到Lagrangian对偶问题，用重参数化神经网络计算原值函数的上下界；Kalman滤波以美国数据校准。

Result: 所提方法对偶间隙小于基准方法；约束与无约束显式解对比显示，交易约束显著改变财富积累、组合策略与保险需求。

Conclusion: 无约束解可能为实际养老金管理提供误导性指导，约束建模是必要的。

Abstract: This paper studies constrained defined contribution (DC) pension management under a two-factor financial market model. The pension participant is allowed to invest in cash, two nominal bonds, an inflation-linked bond, and a stock index. Moreover, they can also purchase term life insurance to manage pre-retirement death risk. Unlike traditional literature, we consider a pension member restricted to a general non-empty, closed, and convex trading constraint set that includes no short-selling, no borrowing, and portfolio-mix constraints as special cases. Such restrictions reflect common limits on pension-account trading. To solve the problem, we construct a family of unconstrained artificial markets, in which the original trading constraints are satisfied by adjusting the drift terms of the risky assets. Using a duality transformation, we derive the Lagrangian dual problem and propose a reparameterized neural network method to compute lower and upper bounds on the primal value function. We calibrate our model with U.S. data using a Kalman filter method, and find that our method produces a smaller duality gap than the benchmark methods. We also compare the constrained results with the unconstrained explicit solution and find that trading constraints substantially change wealth accumulation, portfolio strategies, and insurance demand. This highlights that unconstrained solutions may provide misleading guidance for pension management in practice.

### [2] [Decision Models as Scalable Economic Surrogates that Capture Human Variation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05553)
*Maxime Saxena,Fabio Caccioli,R. Maria del Rio-Chanona*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文首次评估决策模型（DMs）作为经济学中人类代理的可行性：在三个经济场景下对比10个DM与8个LLM，发现前沿DM比LLM更能再现人类反应的异质性，且在速度与成本上具显著优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM作为经济模拟与调查中的人类代理已被广泛使用，但大规模使用时成本高昂，且对人类行为多样性的刻画不足。决策模型（DMs）响应快、输出固定选项上的概率分布，具备成为低成本人类代理的潜力，但尚缺乏系统评估。

Method: 将10个决策模型与8个LLM进行对比，在三个经济场景中考察其作为人类代理的表现：(1) 关于消费的家庭支出调查；(2) 若干经典经济学博弈；(3) 一项劳动力市场实地实验。评估指标包括对人类反应变异的再现程度以及对众数人类反应的捕捉能力，并测量推理速度与成本。

Result: 在三个场景中，在通用决策基准上得分最高的前沿DM均比LLM更贴近地再现人类反应的变异。在支出调查中，DM捕捉人类众数反应的能力与LLM相当；在经济学博弈中则弱于LLM。前沿DM的速度约为最快LLM的十倍，成本可低至百分之一以上（超过百倍便宜）。

Conclusion: 决策模型在刻画人类行为异质性方面优于LLM，并使大规模经济模拟更具可行性；但其对众数反应的拟合在博弈类任务中仍有不足，需进一步改进。

Abstract: Decision Models (DMs), a new class of AI models, give fast responses and return a probability distribution over a fixed set of options. These properties make them candidate human surrogates in economic simulations and surveys, a role increasingly filled by Large Language Models (LLMs), which are costly at scale and under-represent the variation in human behaviour. We present a first evaluation of DMs as human surrogates in economics, comparing 10 DMs with 8 LLMs in three economic settings: a household spending survey on consumption, several classic economic games and a labour-market field experiment. Frontier DMs, those that score highest on general decision-making benchmarks, reproduce the variation in human responses more closely than LLMs in all three settings. They capture the modal human response as well as LLMs in the spending survey, but less well in the economic games. Frontier DMs are also roughly ten times faster than the fastest LLM and can be over a hundred times cheaper. Together, these results suggest that DMs capture the heterogeneity of human behaviour better than LLMs and make large-scale economic simulations more feasible.

### [3] [Economic Policy Uncertainty Index for Poland: Data, Construction, and Validation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05640)
*Alina M. Landowska,Robert A. Kłopotek,He Zhang*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文依照Baker-Bloom-Davis报纸法构建了波兰2000年1月至2026年6月的月度经济政策不确定性（EPU）指数，并用BBD附录A的链式拼接法解决报纸进入时点不同导致的指数水平跳变问题；结果显示该指数受国内事件主导，与美/全球EPU仅弱相关。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 波兰缺乏长样本、连续且跨期可比的月度EPU指数。难点在于六份报纸的可得起始日期不一致，若简单对可用报纸做扩张面板平均，每次新报纸进入都会因样本构成变化造成指数跳变，混淆"真实不确定性变动"与"统计口径变动"。

Method: 采用BBD报纸文本法：选用Rzeczpospolita、Parkiet、Gazeta Wyborcza、Dziennik Gazeta Prawna、Gazeta Polska Codziennie、Puls Biznesu六份深度历史报纸，按文章总量缩放，以2000–2024年（300个月）固定基期做单位标准差标准化，各期对可得报纸取均值，并使基期均值归为100。为解决报纸分批进入问题，使用BBD附录A后向链式拼接：以六报齐全的"锚定面板"为基准向前回溯，在每个重叠窗口内用乘性尺度因子匹配离散度、用乘性比率匹配水平，从而保证指数在各进入点处水平连续。

Result: 指数在COVID-19封锁、2022年能源危机和2023年政府更替期间显著上升，最大反应出现在2016年英国脱欧公投之后。月度变化层面，波兰EPU与美国EPU相关系数为0.26、与全球EPU为0.25，相关度温和。

Conclusion: 波兰EPU指数主要捕捉本国经济政策不确定性，由国内政策动向驱动，而非单纯的国际溢出；国际冲击仍相关，但主要通过引发对波兰自身政策决策的不确定性而传导。拼接处理使指数在长样本上跨期可比，为后续波兰宏观—政策不确定性实证研究提供基础数据。

Abstract: We construct a continuous monthly Economic Policy Uncertainty (EPU) index for Poland spanning January 2000 to June 2026, following the newspaper-based Baker-Bloom-Davis (BBD) methodology. The index uses six deep-history Polish newspapers (Rzeczpospolita, Parkiet, Gazeta Wyborcza, Dziennik Gazeta Prawna, Gazeta Polska Codziennie, and Puls Biznesu), scaled by article volume, standardized to unit standard deviation over a fixed 2000-2024 baseline (300 months), averaged across available papers each period, and normalized so the baseline mean equals 100.
  Because newspapers enter the panel at different dates, a naive expanding-panel average can jump at each entry point from a change in composition rather than real uncertainty. We correct for this using the BBD Appendix A splicing procedure: a backward chain-splice from the richest six-paper anchor panel that matches dispersion via a multiplicative scale factor and matches level via a multiplicative ratio over each overlap window, yielding an index that is level-continuous across all entry points.
  The index rises sharply during the COVID-19 lockdown, the 2022 energy crisis, and the 2023 change of government, with its largest reaction following the 2016 Brexit referendum -- an external event with major implications for Poland's economic-policy environment. In month-on-month changes, Poland's EPU is moderately correlated with the US (0.26) and global (0.25) EPU indices. Together with the event-based evidence, these modest correlations support our central finding: EPU Index PL captures economic policy uncertainty that is predominantly domestic, driven mainly by developments in Poland's own economic policy rather than merely international spillovers, though international shocks remain relevant insofar as they create uncertainty about Poland's own policy decisions.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Abstract: The WTO moratorium on customs duties on electronic transmissions lapsed in March 2026, allowing governments to impose tariffs on cross-border digital products, alongside other instruments such as digital services taxes (DSTs). Here we combine bilateral trade in 31 digital product categories in 2016-2024 with DST rates and tariffs on physical analogues, estimate gravity models and simulate alternative fiscal regimes. We find that digital trade is lower in destinations with a DST, but within destinations it declines only temporarily after adoption. In conditional scenarios for 2024 based on the event study estimate, adding tariff equivalents to DSTs raises revenue from USD 2.7 to 6.9 billion and lowers trade by about 1.4%, and replacing DSTs with tariff equivalents raises USD 4.3 billion. <details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
