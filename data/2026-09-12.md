### [1] [Deep Learning of Robust Market Making under Regime-Switching Order Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11614)
*Felipe Moret,Fabrizio Lillo*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 论文提出基于C51分布型DQN的深度强化学习做市商（RLMM），在零智能限价订单簿中校准与测试。平稳流下RLMM全面优于GLFT；引入贝叶斯在线变点滤波与队列调整报价暴露失衡两个辅助信号后，可在非平稳方向性失衡中恢复盈利，场景-bandit再加权进一步提升鲁棒性。

Motivation: 经典随机控制做市策略（Avellaneda-Stoikov、GLFT）虽有闭式报价，但假设订单流平稳，与真实微观结构下的状态切换（如元订单算法执行导致的制度变化）不符，在非平稳场景下会产生负盈亏。因此需要一种能适应状态切换的做市方法。

Method: 构建深度强化学习做市商RLMM，采用Rainbow风格的分位分布型DQN（C51），在零智能限价订单簿中进行校准与测试；用平稳与非平稳压力场景对比评估；并将状态扩展为加入两个辅助信号：面向方向性流偏差的贝叶斯在线变点滤波器，以及队列调整后的报价暴露失衡。最后引入场景-bandit步骤对低收益状态场景重新加权。

Result: 平稳情形下，RLMM在全部观测到的风险—收益前沿上均优于GLFT；RLMM对流量不对称的鲁棒性强于GLFT，但作为平稳训练的任一策略，在持续方向性失衡下仍因库存饱和而承受大幅回撤；加入两个辅助信号后盈利能力得以恢复；场景-bandit再加权步骤在随机持续性及方向相关压力下进一步提升表现。

Conclusion: 深度强化学习做市在平稳环境下可超越经典闭式解，且通过融入状态切换检测（变点滤波）与微观结构状态特征（队列调整暴露失衡），并结合场景-bandit再加权，能够缓解非平稳订单流导致的库存饱和亏损，恢复并提升做市性能。

Abstract: Classical market-making strategies based on stochastic control, such as the Avellaneda-Stoikov and the Guéant-Lehalle-Fernandez-Tapia (GLFT) extension, provide closed-form quoting rules, but rest on assumptions that break down at realistic microstructure timescales. One of them is that order flow is stationary, while empirical evidence points to the existence of regimes, possibly associated with algorithmic execution of metaorders. In this case, existing methods provide negative PnL. In this paper, we develop a deep reinforcement-learning market maker (RLMM) - a Rainbow-style distributional DQN (C51) which is calibrated and tested in a zero-intelligence limit order book. We find that, in the stationary setting, RLMM outperforms GLFT across the entire observed risk-return frontier. The RLMM is more robust to flow asymmetry than GLFT, but, like any stationarily trained strategy, it still suffers large drawdowns from inventory saturation under persistent directional imbalance. Augmenting the state of RLMM with two auxiliary signals - a Bayesian online change-point filter over the directional flow bias and a queue-adjusted quote-exposure imbalance -restores profitability. A final scenario-bandit step that reweights low-return regime scenarios further improves performance under random-persistence and correlated-direction stress.
