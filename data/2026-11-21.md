*Jose Blanchet,Yang Liu,Tiantian Mao,Ruodu Wang*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文揭示公共风险配置中公平与模糊规避之间的根本冲突，并证明公平增加等价于总体模糊性增加。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 公共规划者既追求资源分配的公平（对称风险暴露下的公平直觉上可取），又关注缓解风险、不确定性与模糊性，二者可能相互冲突，但缺乏理论刻画。

Method: 在具有个体损失间相依不确定性的对称总体损失框架下研究，并将结论特化到Keeney(1980)经典模型；借助α-maxmin偏好框架对公平与模糊规避进行定量刻画，并推广到一般对称聚合函数类。

Result: 证明公平度增加等价于总体模糊性增加；给出两个准则成立的模糊与风险态度条件：公平对应模糊或风险偏好，模糊规避对应模糊或风险厌恶；在一般对称聚合中模糊态度是偏好首要决定因素，聚合结构与风险态度亦起作用；数值实验验证理论并探讨公平—模糊权衡。

Conclusion: 公平与模糊规避在公共风险配置中存在本质冲突，模糊态度是核心决定因素，规划者需在公平与模糊规避之间进行显式权衡。

Abstract: Equitable resource allocations in the context of symmetric risk exposures are intuitively desirable and sometimes even expected in public planning. However, public planners generally are also concerned with mitigating risk and, more generally, uncertainty and ambiguity. We study a fundamental conflict between equity and ambiguity avoidance in public risk allocation problems. Our main results are framed and established in the setting of general symmetric aggregate losses equipped with dependence uncertainty among individual losses. Specializing to the classical model of Keeney (1980), we prove that an increase in equity is equivalent to an increase in aggregate ambiguity. We then provide a quantitative characterization of equity and ambiguity avoidance through the $α$-maxmin preference framework, establishing conditions on ambiguity and risk attitudes under which each criterion holds. Specifically, equity corresponds to ambiguity or risk proneness, while ambiguity avoidance corresponds to ambiguity or risk aversion. The analysis extends to general classes of symmetric aggregation functions, where we show that ambiguity attitude is the primary determinant of preference, though aggregation structure and risk attitude also play important roles. Numerical illustrations validate our theoretical findings and explore the equity-ambiguity tradeoff.
