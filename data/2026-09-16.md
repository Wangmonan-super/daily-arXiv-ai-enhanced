### [1] [From Public Evidence to Contractual Outcome: First and Stable Decidability on Kalshi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16642)
*Maksym Nechepurenko*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文提出预测市场合约中"首次可判定性"与"稳定可判定性"两个时钟概念，并报告了回顾性识别测试、冻结盲试点、全量历史恢复及前瞻性基础设施验证的结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 公开证据可能在场所记录首次裁定前就已足以结算预测市场合约，但其边界取决于适用规则版本、确切发布对象、来源层级、更正历史及未完成合约条件。为精确刻画这一时点边界，需要区分"同时性可判定"与"经最终化稳定后的可判定"。

Method: 定义两个Kalshi时钟：首次可判定性（first decidability），即规则-证据映射中最早的同时性单例；稳定可判定性（stable decidability），即该单例在最终化过程中保持不变的最早历史时点。开展完整回顾性识别测试、冻结盲试点、覆盖152,694个普通ticker与11,530个精确事件身份的全量历史恢复及6,540个只读官方请求，并执行前瞻性基础设施验证。

Result: 回顾性识别测试给出狭窄可行性结果；冻结盲试点中25个身份与盲法检查全部通过，25个身份均恢复现行规则文本，但未恢复任何精确或有界的历史规则版本及官方来源发布对象；全量历史恢复中历史合格事件与ticker均为零。前瞻性基础设施验证覆盖3个来源计划、25个市场，完整性重验证781,266个生命周期帧，22个闭合下界重连回执，无未解决重连间隙、无到期未发官方发布，价格层未启用。

Conclusion: 该研究属于可观测性结果，并不主张没有市场可判定或公开证据从未存在。前瞻样本仅在入组关闭时由前瞻冻结身份与结果前字段构建，其冻结目标规模依据注册的完整、缩减、探索或无支持处置机制性选取。本文不报告任何合约可判定性时钟、人工裁决、价格或跨场所结果。

Abstract: Public evidence can become sufficient to settle a prediction-market contract before the venue records its first determination, but the relevant boundary depends on the applicable rule version, exact release object, source hierarchy, correction history, and unfinished contract conditions. This paper defines two Kalshi clocks: first decidability, the earliest contemporaneous singleton in the rule-evidence mapping, and stable decidability, the retrospective earliest time after which the same singleton remains unchanged through finalization. A completed retrospective identification test establishes a narrow feasibility result. In a frozen blind pilot, all 25 identities and blinding checks passed and current rule text was recovered for all 25; no exact or bounded historical rule version and no exact or bounded official source-release object was recovered. The full historical recovery covered 152,694 ordinary tickers, 11,530 exact event identities, and 6,540 read-only official requests, with zero historically eligible events and zero historically eligible tickers. This is an observability result, not a claim that no market was decidable or that public evidence never existed. A completed prospective infrastructure shakedown established observation capability for three source programmes across 25 markets, with integrity revalidation of 781,266 lifecycle frames, 22 closed lower-bounded reconnect receipts, no unresolved reconnect gap, no due-but-missed official release, and an inactive price layer. Production evidence enrollment is active. The prospective sample is constructed only at enrollment close from prospectively frozen identities and pre-outcome fields; its frozen target size is selected mechanically under the registered full, reduced, exploratory, or no-go support disposition. No contractual-decidability clock, human-adjudication, price, or cross-venue result is reported here.

### [2] [Global Poverty Beyond the Official Line: A bounded estimate of material insufficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16203)
*Giancarlo Crocetti*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 报告不否认世界银行 \$3.00/日 极端贫困线数据的准确性，但指出该门槛仅为"生存底线"设计；若按各国自身发展水平对应的标准衡量，贫困人口规模为数倍之多，最保守估计也超 10 亿。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单一极端贫困线（\$3.00/日，2021 PPP）被广泛引用并带来"安慰性"结论，使人倾向于接受一个令人安心的数字后不再深究；报告旨在揭示这种安慰在多大程度上是测量方式选择的产物，并说明不同贫困口径所回答的是不同问题。

Method: 以官方数据为基准不作质疑，转而分析其"捕捉到什么、遗漏了什么"：按地理、年龄、性别进行分解；在货币贫困之外补充多维指标（粮食安全、在职贫困、社会保护、相对贫困）；并考察调查覆盖率的偏误方向。

Result: 按 \$3.00/日 衡量约 8.47 亿人（占全球 10.4%，2024 年）处于贫困；按各国发展水平相应收入标准，约 37 亿人低于世行 \$8.30/日 中高收入参考线，约 11 亿人处于多维贫困，约 23 亿人粮食不安全。数据质量偏误呈单向性：贫困最严重地区的调查覆盖最弱，故官方数字更可能低估而非高估贫困。

Conclusion: 贫困规模在很大程度上取决于测量口径，\$3.00 线只是生存底线而非基本生活保障的描述；多种口径下的结果并非相互矛盾，而是对不同问题的不同回答，且官方数据系统性偏向低估剥夺程度。

Abstract: Numbers this large invite a defensive reflex: reach for the reassuring figure and move on. By the most widely cited measure, the World Bank's extreme-poverty line of \$3.00 per day (2021 PPP), approximately 847 million people, or 10.4% of the world's population, lived in poverty in 2024. That figure is accurate as measured. It is also, by design, a floor: a threshold built to mark bare survival in the poorest economies, not to describe what it takes for a family anywhere to live with basic security.
  The central argument of this report is that the reassurance offered by that single line is largely an artifact of how we chose to measure. Held to the \$3.00 floor, roughly 847 million people are poor; held to the standards of their own societies, the number is several times larger, and even the most cautious, honest count exceeds a billion. Measured against income standards appropriate to each country's level of development, some 3.7 billion people fall below the World Bank's \$8.30/day upper-middle-income reference line, roughly 1.1 billion are multidimensionally poor, and around 2.3 billion are food insecure. These are not competing errors but answers to different questions.
  This report does not dispute the official data. It takes those figures as accurate and asks what they do and do not capture. It disaggregates by geography, age, and gender and supplements the monetary count with multidimensional measures of food security, working poverty, social protection, and relative poverty. It also documents a data-quality problem that runs in one direction: survey coverage is weaker in regions with the worst poverty, so official figures are far likelier to understate deprivation than to overstate it.

### [3] [An Integrative Multidimensional Conceptualization of Telework Behavior: A Systematic Review and Grounded Theory Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17409)
*Sahar Babaei,Saeed Nosratabadi,Thabit Atobishi,Sahar Abu Bakir*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本研究通过PRISMA系统综述114篇综述文献，运用建构主义扎根理论，构建了远程办公行为的多维整合框架，涵盖六类行为维度、五类前因、六类结果及三个情境调节变量，并提出14条可检验命题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 远程办公快速扩张，亟需理解员工在远程办公情境下的行为表现；既有研究缺乏对远程办公行为多维本质的整合性概念框架。

Method: 遵循PRISMA开展系统文献综述，识别114篇综述文章，采用建构主义扎根理论进行分析与编码整合。

Result: 识别出六类行为维度（绩效、沟通、环境、任务、政策、福祉）、五类前因（个体因素、工作特征、组织规范、技术因素、工作环境）、六类结果（工作满意度、生产力、离职、健康与福祉、工作—生活平衡、社会孤立）及三个情境调节变量（远程办公形式、远程办公偏好、文化与国家情境），并提出14条关联命题。

Conclusion: 该框架将远程办公行为确立为远程工作条件转化为员工与组织结果的核心构念，界定了可据此开发测量工具的内容域，并为组织在政策、培训与干预方面的着力点提供指引。

Abstract: Telework has expanded rapidly, and understanding the behaviors employees enact under it has become correspondingly important. This study develops an integrative conceptual framework specifying the multidimensional nature of telework behavior. A systematic literature review following PRISMA identified 114 review articles, which were analyzed using constructivist grounded theory. Six behavioral dimensions were identified, covering performance, communication, environmental, task, policy, and well-being conduct. Antecedents were grouped into individual factors, job characteristics, organizational norms, technological factors, and work environment factors. Outcomes were grouped into job satisfaction, productivity, turnover, health and well-being, work-life balance, and social isolation. Three contextual moderators were identified, namely telework modality, telework preference, and cultural and national context. Fourteen propositions link these categories and are advanced as testable claims rather than established findings, since they are derived from published review evidence and have not been empirically tested. The framework proposes telework behavior as the construct through which the conditions of remote work are translated into employee and organizational outcomes, and it specifies a content domain from which measures of the construct can be developed. It also indicates where organizations can direct policy, training, and intervention.

</details>
