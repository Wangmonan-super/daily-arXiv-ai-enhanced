### [1] [The Elasticity of Substitution between Native and Immigrant Labor: A Meta-Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26062)
*Klara Kantova,Tomas Havranek,Zuzana Irsova,Jiri Schwarz*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文首次对本地与移民劳动之间的替代弹性进行综合元分析，纳入41项研究的1091个估计，发现存在选择性报告，校正后弹性由约13升至约22，最佳实践估计约为17。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有文献对本地与移民劳动的替代弹性估计差异较大，且可能存在发表偏倚与选择性报告，导致对移民相对工资压力的判断失真，因此需要系统整合与校正。

Method: 基于41项研究的1091个估计进行元分析；使用元回归与选择方法校正选择性报告；采用贝叶斯与频率派模型平均识别异质性来源。

Result: 校正后隐含弹性从约13提高到约22，意味着移民带来的相对工资压力比未校正结果低约40%；异质性主要由地理尺度、数据粒度以及样本是否限于低经验工人驱动，对数均值工资与均值对数工资的选择作用次要；基线区域设定下最佳实践弹性约为17。

Conclusion: 移民与本地劳动的替代弹性被系统性低估，校正后仍显著高于未校正均值，但低于简单偏倚校正结果，说明数据粒度与区域设定对弹性估计至关重要。

Abstract: This paper presents the first comprehensive meta-analysis of the elasticity of substitution between native and immigrant labor, drawing on 1,091 estimates from 41 studies. We find strong evidence of selective reporting: less precise estimates are systematically associated with lower reported elasticities. Correcting for this bias using meta-regression and selection methods raises the implied elasticity from about 13 to about 22, implying about 40% less relative-wage pressure from immigration than uncorrected results suggest. Bayesian and frequentist model averaging show that heterogeneity is driven mainly by geographic scale, data granularity, and whether the sample is restricted to low-experience workers, while the choice between log mean wages and mean log wages plays a secondary role. Our best-practice estimates, which net out publication bias and prioritize the most granular data, imply an elasticity of about 17 in our baseline regional specification, lower than implied by a simple bias correction but substantially higher than the uncorrected mean.

### [2] [Trust, Rule of Law, and the Size Premium: Evidence from a Meta-Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26212)
*Jiri Schwarz,Tomas Havranek,Zuzana Irsova,Jiri Novak*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该文通过105项研究、31个国家的1,613个规模溢价估计进行元回归与贝叶斯模型平均，发现法治与广义信任可部分解释规模溢价估计的异质性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 规模溢价（小公司平均收益高于大公司）的估计在不同研究、国家、时期与设计间差异巨大，作者希望检验制度环境（法治与广义信任）能否解释这种异质性。由于小公司信息更不透明、更依赖外部融资，执法与信息环境对其影响应大于大公司。

Method: 元分析：样本为105项研究、31个国家的1,613个已报告的规模斜率估计。元回归控制研究设计、模型设定、精度、发表背景以及市场与宏观金融条件；采用贝叶斯模型平均（BMA）处理控制变量集合的不确定性。

Result: 更稳健的关联来自法治，且方向与直觉相反：更强的法治对应更负的已报告规模斜率，即更大的传统规模溢价。信任的关联是有条件的、估计精度较低：在法治薄弱处，更高的广义信任与更不负的斜率（更弱的溢价）相关，且该关联随法治增强而消失。

Conclusion: 正式与非正式制度有助于组织该文献中的部分分歧；但分析针对的是已报告估计的变异，并不识别因果效应。

Abstract: Reported estimates of the size premium, the tendency of smaller firms to earn higher average returns than larger firms, vary widely across studies, countries, periods, and designs. We examine whether generalized trust and rule of law help account for that heterogeneity. Small firms are more opaque and more dependent on outside finance, so the enforcement and information environment should matter more for them than for large firms. We study 1,613 reported size-slope estimates from 105 studies and 31 countries. The meta-regressions control for study design, specification, precision, publication context, and market and macro-financial conditions; Bayesian model averaging assesses uncertainty over the control set. The more stable association is with rule of law, and it runs against the intuitive expectation that better legal institutions shrink the premium: stronger rule of law is associated with more negative reported size slopes, hence larger conventional size premia. The trust association is conditional and less precisely estimated: where rule of law is weak, higher generalized trust is linked to less negative reported slopes (and thus a weaker premium), and this link fades as rule of law strengthens. Formal and informal institutions thus help organize part of the disagreement in this literature, although the analysis concerns variation in reported estimates and does not identify causal effects.

### [3] [The network advantage: benefits of interregional scientific knowledge spillovers on AI patenting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26604)
*Saverio Barabuffi,Jacopo Cricchio,Alberto Di Minin,Guido Pialli*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 跨区域AI合作论文产生的知识溢出能够提升区域AI专利产出，但收益几乎完全集中于民营企业主导研发的地区，从而加剧区域分化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有研究多将知识溢出视为经由地理邻近扩散的非意图外部性，而刻意的跨区域网络则主要通过共同发明人关系考察，忽视了合作论文所承载的科学层面。本文据此追问：跨区域AI合作论文的溢出是否促进区域AI专利，以及区域研发的所有权构成是否影响其吸收能力。

Method: 采用网络计量方法，覆盖2006—2020年中国26个省级区域，构建跨区域AI相关合作论文网络，估计其对区域AI专利的影响，并考察地区研发所有权结构（民营企业主导程度）的调节作用。

Result: 跨区域合作论文的溢出显著促进区域AI专利产出；但该回报几乎全部集中在民营企业主导研发的地区，在国有企业主导的地区并不明显，进而强化了区域间分化。

Conclusion: 科学层面的跨区域合作网络是AI知识溢出的重要渠道，但其经济回报具有所有权条件性，吸收能力取决于区域研发由谁主导，因而合作网络在缺乏相应制度与主体条件时可能扩大而非缩小区域差距。

Abstract: Knowledge spillovers have largely been studied as unintended externalities diffusing through geographic proximity, while deliberate interregional networks have been examined mostly through co-inventor ties rather than the scientific layer carried by co-authored publications. This study asks whether spillovers from interregional AI-related co-publications increase regional AI patenting, and whether the ownership composition of regional R&D shapes the capacity to capitalize on them. Using a network-econometric approach across 26 Chinese provincial-level regions from 2006 to 2020, we find that co-publication spillovers foster AI patenting, but the returns concentrate almost entirely in regions where privately owned enterprises dominate R&D, reinforcing regional divergence.

</details>
