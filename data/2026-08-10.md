### [1] [Beyond Co-Movement: Locality by Exposures Enables a Joint Factor-Graph Framework for Portfolio Diversification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06618)
*Sara Chehab,Giorgos Iacovides,Parisa Yazdanparast,Danilo Mandic*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文提出MINGLE框架，通过ADMM优化联合学习潜在因子表示及其诱导的图拓扑，将因子域和图表征域相互正则化，使资产配置直接作用于底层市场结构而非观测的联动性或有限样本伪影。实验表明该框架生成的暴露相似性图更贴合经济板块，构建的投资组合在多个波动率区间与交易成本水平下持续优于基于相关系数的组合。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有投资组合构建方法存在互补性缺陷：标准因子模型忽略特质冲击的影响，而近年基于图的方法则忽略驱动系统性收益的潜在数据结构。两者结合有望让资产配置直接作用于底层市场结构，而非观测的联动性或有限样本伪影。

Method: MINGLE（Mutually-INformed Graph-Locality and Exposures）框架通过统一的ADMM优化，联合学习潜在因子表示及其诱导的图拓扑。其核心创新在于重新定义图局部性——不再基于观测的联动性，而是基于系统性因子暴露轮廓来构建图结构，实现因子域与图域的相互正则化。

Result: 暴露相似性图比传统基于相关性的图更能对齐既有的经济板块划分。基于该表征构建的投资组合在各类波动率区间和交易成本水平下持续优于基于相关性的组合。配对统计检验证实收益来源于图域与因子域的成功调和。

Conclusion: 因子域与图域的相互正则化能够在市场表征中同时捕捉系统性与特质性信息，带来更贴近真实经济结构的图拓扑和更稳健的投资组合表现，为量化投资组合构建提供了新的方法论方向。

Abstract: Current portfolio construction methods are either agnostic to the effects of idiosyncratic shocks (standard factor models) or to the latent data structure driving systematic returns (recent graph-based approaches). This presents an opportunity to combine the complementary market aspects captured by the factor and graph domains, allowing asset allocations to operate directly on the underlying market structure, rather than on its observed co-movement or its finite-sample artefacts. In this work, we introduce the Mutually-INformed Graph-Locality and Exposures framework (MINGLE), which mutually regularises the factor and graph domains by redefining graph locality through systematic factor exposure profiles, rather than via observed co-movements. This is formalised through a unified Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) framework that jointly learns a latent factor representation and its induced graph topology directly from market returns. The resulting exposure-similarity graph aligns more closely with established economic sectors than conventional correlation-based graphs. Portfolios constructed from this representation are shown to consistently outperform their correlation-based counterparts across a range of volatility regimes and transaction cost levels. For rigour, paired statistical testing confirms that these gains stem from the reconciliation of the graph and factor domains.

### [2] [Two Types of Tertiarization: Household Demand, Production Networks, and the Rise of Services](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06584)
*Li Gan*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 利用76个经济体1995-2022年的标准化投入产出表，将服务增加值归因到最终国内需求类别，测量服务经济的"家庭导向性"（household orientation）。研究发现服务占比上升与家庭导向性呈负相关，仅约四成的服务化时期是家庭主导的；在65个服务占比上升的经济体中，77%变得更加非家庭导向。一个基准模型和两类型扩展模型解释了这一反转，精确的Leontief核算与Shapley分解显示生产中位数73%的服务占比增长归因于生产系统渠道，而家庭导向性的下降主要归因于总体最终需求结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的"服务占比"（service share）仅仅记录服务活动的总量，无法揭示其背后的需求来源和驱动机制。作者希望区分服务增长究竟是源于家庭消费还是源于生产网络、投资、出口、政府等非家庭需求，从而更深刻地理解经济转型（tertiarization）的性质与动力。

Method: 使用76个经济体1995-2022年标准化投入产出表，将服务增加值归因到最终国内需求类别（家庭、生产网络、投资、出口、政府）；构建"家庭导向性"指标衡量水平与增长；采用精确的Leontief核算与Shapley分解，将服务占比增长分解为生产系统渠道与最终需求结构渠道；建立单类型基准模型和两类型扩展模型以推导反转条件。

Result: 服务占比与家庭导向性呈负相关，仅约四成服务化时期为家庭主导。65个服务占比上升的经济体中，77%（在2个百分点实质性变化阈值下为75%）变得更不家庭导向。但家庭支持的产出的服务密集度随收入增加而上升。Shapley分解显示中位数73%的服服占比增长归因于生产系统渠道，在80%实质性上升的经济体中该渠道贡献最大；家庭导向性下降则主要归因于总最终需求结构的变动。

Conclusion: 服务占比只能反映服务活动存在多少，无法说明是何种经济转型驱动了它。家庭导向性与服务占比之间的负相关以及生产系统渠道的主导地位，意味着服务化在很大程度上并非由家庭消费驱动，而由生产网络、投资、出口、政府等非家庭需求推动。这要求研究者在分析服务经济转型时超越单一占比指标，关注需求结构与生产系统的具体机制。

Abstract: I measure whether the rise of services is ultimately supported by household consumption or by non-household demand through production networks, investment, exports, and government. Using harmonized input-output tables for 76 economies over 1995-2022, I attribute service value added to its ultimate domestic final-demand category and measure the household orientation of each service economy in both levels and growth. The conventional service share does not reveal the type: it is negatively correlated with household orientation, and only about four in ten tertiarization episodes are majority-household. Among the 65 economies whose service share rose over 1995-2018, 77 percent became less household-oriented; the share is 75 percent under a two-percentage-point material-change threshold. Yet household-supported production becomes more service-intensive with income because households' aggregate position falls faster. A one-type benchmark has no purchaser margin; a two-type extension gives the condition for this reversal. An exact Leontief accounting and Shapley decomposition allocate a median 73 percent of service-share growth to production-system channels, which are the largest contribution in 80 percent of economies with materially rising shares. The decline in household orientation is instead allocated mainly to aggregate final-demand structure. The service share records how much service activity exists, not which economic transformation produced it.

### [3] [Tabular Foundation Models and the Unity of Economic Behaviour](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06842)
*Victor H. Aguiar*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文利用冻结的表格基础模型（foundation model）在统一的choice experiment中预测决策者跨多个行为领域（风险、时间、损失、估值与社会选择）的选择，并结合随机效用模型提取一个跨领域通用的效用函数与随机成分。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经济学中不同领域（风险、时间、损失、估值、社会选择）通常使用不同的行为模型，缺乏一个统一框架来解释同一决策者在这些领域的系统性差异与共同规律。

Method: 设计统一的选择实验，让同一批决策者面对所有行为领域；隐藏某一领域的决策选择，利用冻结的表格基础模型从该决策者其他领域的选择及其他参与者的带标签选择中恢复；随后估计一个基于该模型学习表征的随机效用模型。

Result: 基础模型在预测上优于训练样本中位数，且当可见选择在决策者之间被打乱时优势消失；随机效用模型保留了基础模型大部分预测误差降低，能预测未参与效用估计的领域，并复现行为测度在人群中的联动关系。

Conclusion: 模型将三个对象分离：学习的共同选择域、该域上的一个系统性效用函数，以及生成观测菜单上随机选择的一个随机成分，从而在统一框架下解释跨领域行为。

Abstract: Economics uses different behavioural models for risk, time, losses, valuation, and social choice. I study a unified choice experiment in which the same decision makers face all these domains. I hide a decision maker's choices in one domain and ask a frozen tabular foundation model to recover them from that decision maker's choices elsewhere and labelled choices by other participants. The foundation model improves on the training-sample median, and the gain disappears when visible choices are shuffled across decision makers. I then estimate one random-utility model over the foundation model's learned representation. This structural model applies the same utility function in every domain, retains most of the foundation model's reduction in prediction error, predicts domains excluded from utility estimation, and reproduces how behavioural measures co-move across people. The resulting model separates three objects: a learned common choice domain, one systematic utility function on that domain, and one random component that generates stochastic choice on observed menus.

### [4] [Reading Copom's Tone: A Weighted LLM Framework for Hawkish-Dovish Sentiment, Forward Guidance, and Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07251)
*Gabriel de Macedo Santos*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出一个用于衡量巴西货币政策委员会（Copom）声明语调的自然语言处理框架，受Itaú的iSent分类器启发，扩展了三个方向：LLM识别鹰派/鸽派短语并赋予0-1强度权重、结合句子计数与信号强度的文档指数（-1至1）、以及衡量前瞻指引方向、指引明确性、不确定性水平及变化的全文档层。样本包含80份声明、1,498个分类句子（2016年8月至2026年8月），其中33.3%鹰派、18.0%鸽派、42.1%中性、6.5%无关。文章主要贡献是方法论层面的：一个透明、可审计、将修辞语调与政策指引及不确定性分离的系统。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有央行沟通语调分析方法往往将语调视为单一维度，未能区分修辞性语调与政策指引、不确定性等结构性信息。作者希望构建一个透明、可审计、可增量扩展的框架，系统性地分离并量化Copom声明中的不同信息维度，以支持更精细的沟通分析。

Method: 框架在iSent的句子级四分类（鹰派、鸽派、中性、无关）基础上扩展三个方向：(1) 使用LLM识别短小鹰派/鸽派表达并分配0-1强度权重；(2) 文档指数结合句子计数与文档级平均信号强度，产生-1到1的有界分数；(3) 增加全文档层，分别衡量前瞻指引方向、指引明确性、不确定性水平及不确定性变化四个维度。

Result: 样本包含80份声明、1,498个分类句子（2016年8月31日至2026年8月5日）。句子分布：33.3%鹰派、18.0%鸽派、42.1%中性、6.5%无关。平均文档分数为+0.107，最鹰派读数+0.570（2021年8月）。最新声明（2026年8月5日）得分+0.232，含8句鹰派、2句鸽派、9句中性；其结构层显示指引方向模糊但部分明确，不确定性为中枢水平且高于上次会议。语调与指引方向分数的同期Pearson相关系数为0.719。

Conclusion: 该系统的核心贡献是方法论性的：提供了一个透明、增量式、可审计的框架，将修辞语调与政策指引和不确定性分离。研究明确声明这些是描述性输出而非经验证的Selic决策或DI收益预测模型，因此不宣称预测能力，而致力于提供结构化的沟通分析工具。

Abstract: This paper documents an applied natural-language-processing framework for measuring the tone of Brazilian Monetary Policy Committee (Copom) statements. The project is explicitly inspired by iSent, Itaú's Central Bank sentiment classifier, particularly its sentence-level division of official communication into hawkish, dovish, neutral, and out-of-context classes. The implementation extends that idea in three directions. First, an LLM identifies short hawkish and dovish expressions and assigns each a 0-to-1 intensity weight. Second, the document index combines sentence counts with document-specific average signal intensities, producing a bounded score from -1 to 1. Third, a separate full-document layer measures forward-guidance direction, guidance explicitness, uncertainty level, and change in uncertainty. The empirical sample is restricted to communications dated August 2016 or later and contains 80 statements and 1,498 classified sentences from August 31, 2016 through August 5, 2026. Across this sample, 33.3% of sentences are hawkish, 18.0% dovish, 42.1% neutral, and 6.5% out of context. The average document score is +0.107, while the most hawkish reading is +0.570 in August 2021. The latest statement, dated August 5, 2026, scores +0.232, with eight hawkish, two dovish, and nine neutral sentences. Its structural overlay is more nuanced: guidance is directionally ambiguous but partly explicit, while uncertainty is classified as central and higher than at the prior meeting. Tone and the guidance-direction score have a contemporaneous Pearson correlation of 0.719. These are descriptive outputs, not a validated forecast of Selic decisions or DI returns. The main contribution is therefore methodological: a transparent, incremental, auditable system that separates rhetorical tone from policy guidance and uncertainty.

</details>
