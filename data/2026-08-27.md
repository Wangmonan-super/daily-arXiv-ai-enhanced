### [1] [Forecasting Weather-Driven Price Dynamics Across Sri Lankan Tea Market Catalogues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24894)
*Hesandi Mallawarachchi,Senilka Madurapperumage,Nadil Kulathunge,Thilokya Angeesa,Nethsith Gunaweera,Sandeepa Weerasekara,Patalee Narasinghe,Nisansa de Silva,Sandareka Wickramanayake*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本研究构建了2023年底至2026年间105份科伦坡茶叶拍卖周报的结构化数据集，结合区域天气数据，运用Granger因果分析和四种树模型（Random Forest、XGBoost、LightGBM、Gradient Boosting）分析斯里兰卡四大茶叶目录（High Grown、Low Grown、Off-Grade、Dust）的价格影响因素。结果表明市场动态是主要驱动因素，但天气条件也有显著影响：Low Grown对降水和日照时长敏感（p<0.05，1-3周滞后），Off-Grade和Dust对温度变化有显著响应。按目录分组建模优于统一建模，LightGBM在四种目录中三种表现最佳。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 科伦坡茶叶拍卖在全球茶叶定价中至关重要，但本地天气条件与不同茶叶目录价格行为之间的关系尚未被深入探索，缺乏针对性的结构化实证研究。

Method: 从105份周度经纪人报告中提取信息构建新型结构化数据集（覆盖2023年底至2026年），并与区域天气数据整合；应用Granger因果分析识别因果关系，结合四种基于树的机器学习模型（Random Forest、XGBoost、LightGBM、Gradient Boosting）进行预测建模，并比较统一建模与按目录分组建模的效果。

Result: 市场动态是价格的主要驱动因素，但天气条件亦有显著影响。Low Grown茶对降水和日照时长表现出强敏感性（p<0.05，1-3周滞后区间）；Off-Grade和Dust目录对温度变化有显著响应。按目录分组建模优于统一建模，LightGBM在四种目录中的三种上表现最优。

Conclusion: 茶叶价格预测应同时考虑局部天气模式和目录层面的差异，本研究提供了一个更精准且实用的定价框架，对茶叶产业具有重要实践意义。

Abstract: The Colombo Tea Auction (CTA) plays a vital role in determining global tea prices, yet the relationship between local weather conditions and price behavior across different tea catalogues has not been thoroughly explored. In this study, we develop a novel, structured dataset by extracting information from 105 weekly broker reports spanning late 2023 to 2026, and combined with region-specific weather data. Our analysis focuses on four main tea catalogues of Sri Lankan tea: High Grown, Low Grown, Off-Grade, and Dust. To better understand the factors influencing tea prices, we apply Granger causality analysis alongside tree-based machine learning models: Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Gradient Boosting. Our results show that while market dynamics are primary drivers, weather conditions also have significant effects. Notably, Low Grown tea shows strong sensitivity to precipitation and sunshine duration (p<0.05) across 1-3-week lags. Off-Grade and Dust catalogues also exhibit significant responses to temperature variations. Catalogue-specific modelling outperformed unified approaches, with LightGBM emerging as the superior model for three out of four catalogues. Overall, this study highlights the importance of considering both localized weather patterns and catalogue-level differences when forecasting tea prices, offering a more precise and practical framework for the tea industry.

### [2] [Normative boundaries of AI in scientific work: Evidence from PhD researchers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25678)
*Francesco Angelini,Johan Lyrvall*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本研究通过对Nature 2025年研究生调查中3785名STEM及医学健康科学博士生的数据分析，运用潜在类别分析识别出四种对AI使用的态度模式：主导的"分工劳动"模式（接受文献类任务中的AI，抵制写作、数据分析和实验设计等智力贡献相关任务）、"维持现状"模式（普遍不适）、"万能"模式（普遍接受）和"犹豫不决"模式（高度不确定）。研究发现态度并非围绕简单的接受-拒绝二分，而是围绕任务特定边界组织，提示AI治理、博士培训、披露和研究评价需要采取任务特定的方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 人工智能在科研工作中日益普及，但研究人员可能在评估其使用时在不同研究任务间存在不统一的标准。本研究旨在考察博士生对AI在不同研究任务中使用的具体态度，以理解这些态度如何组织，从而为AI治理、培训和评价提供依据。

Method: 基于Nature Graduate Survey 2025中3785名STEM及医学健康科学博士生的自选国际样本，测量受访者对AI在撰写研究论文、收集和分析数据、设计实验、追踪科学文献和总结文献等任务中的舒适度，采用潜在类别分析（LCA）识别不同的态度画像。

Result: 识别出四种不同的态度画像："分工劳动"模式（主导性，广泛接受文献相关任务的AI但抵制写作、数据分析和实验设计）、"维持现状"模式（普遍不适）、"万能"模式（普遍舒适）和"犹豫不决"模式（大量不确定性）。态度组织围绕任务特定边界而非简单的接受-拒绝对立，涉及委托、作者身份和责任的考量。

Conclusion: 由于调查测量的是舒适度而非合法性，这些画像应被理解为具有规范维度的态度配置。研究强调AI治理、博士培训、披露和研究评价需要任务特定的方法，而非一刀切的政策。

Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly embedded in scientific work, but researchers may not evaluate its use uniformly across research tasks. This study examines task-specific attitudes towards AI among an international, self-selected sample of 3,785 PhD students in STEM and medical and health sciences who participated in Nature's Graduate Survey 2025. We analyse respondents' comfort with using AI for writing a research article, collecting and analysing data, designing experiments, tracking scientific literature, and summarising it. Latent class analysis identifies four distinct attitudinal profiles. The dominant profile reflects a "division of labour," in which AI is widely accepted for literature-related tasks but resisted in activities closely associated with intellectual contribution, such as writing, data analysis, and experimental design. A "status quo" profile is broadly uncomfortable across tasks, an "all-purpose" profile is broadly comfortable, and an "undecided" profile expresses substantial uncertainty. These patterns suggest that attitudes towards AI in research are organised less around a simple acceptance-rejection divide than around task-specific boundaries, likely concerning delegation, authorship, and responsibility. Because the survey measures comfort rather than legitimacy, the profiles are best interpreted as attitudinal configurations with a normative dimension. The findings highlight the importance of task-specific approaches to AI governance, doctoral training, disclosure, and research evaluation.

### [3] [The Dynamic Trade-Off of Dual-Class Shares](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25972)
*Hyunseob Kim,Doron Levit,Roni Michaely*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文基于52年美国双重股权公司的新数据和双重差分设计，动态分析双重股权结构的治理权衡：估值在双重股权资本重组后上升但随时间下降，而创新产出持续增加，且这些效应集中在企业特定投资较高的行业。统一股权结构的结果与之对应，成熟双重股权公司的投资对机会敏感度更低、投票溢价随成熟度上升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 双重股权结构将控制权分配给创始人（其企业特定投资驱动公司价值），但将控制权与所有权分离，从而提高代理成本。已有研究多为静态分析，本文试图从动态视角分析这一权衡，使用更长时间跨度的新数据来揭示时间维度上的效应差异。

Method: 使用覆盖52年的美国双重股权公司新数据，采用差分中的差分（difference-in-differences）设计，比较双重股权资本重组前后的估值和创新产出变化，并在行业层面区分企业特定投资强度，同时考察股票统一（unifications）的对应情形。

Result: 估值在双重股权资本重组后上升但随时间推移下降；创新产出呈持续增长；这两类效应集中在企业特定投资较高的行业。成熟双重股权公司的投资对机会的敏感度较低，且投票溢价随公司成熟度增加而上升。

Conclusion: 研究结果支持动态处理效应（dynamic treatment effects），表明双重股权结构的收益和成本随时间演变，并为政策制定提供新的启示。

Abstract: Dual-class shares allocate control to founders whose firm-specific investments drive firm value but separate control from ownership, raising agency costs. We analyze this trade-off dynamically. Using new data on US dual-class firms spanning 52 years and difference-in-differences designs, we show that valuations rise following dual-class recapitalizations but decline over time, whereas innovative output increases persistently. These effects are concentrated in industries with greater firm-specific investments. We find corresponding results for stock unifications. Investment by mature dual-class firms is less sensitive to opportunities and voting premia increase with maturity. Our results support dynamic treatment effects and yield new policy implications.

</details>
