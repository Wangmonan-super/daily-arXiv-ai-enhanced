Along the cycle, rising OUD paradoxically raises the employment rate, as falling productivity requires more labor per unit of output; across steady states, however, higher OUD prevalence is associated with a lower employment rate, consistent with evidence that opioid use disorder reduces labor force participation. Moreover, our numerical experiments show that OUD sensitivity to income widens fluctuations, while productivity sensitivity to OUD counterintuitively compresses them through a self-correcting feedback that speeds the recovery of the wage share. Policies that weaken the link between income and OUD, such as stronger social safety nets, stabilize the resulting dynamics.

### [13] [On Bonart's interpretation of the Square-Root Impact Law](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10053)
*J. -P. Bouchaud,I. Mastromatteo,B. Toth*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文重新审视并简化 Bonart 关于平方根冲击律（SRIL）的微观起源论证，指出其"市场对参与者元订单流进行白化"这一关键前提面临理论反例与实证困难，并通过体积守恒论证揭示扩散性路径必须假设的隐含步骤。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SRIL（I = Yσ√(Q/V)）同时包含形状（冲击正比于交易量的平方根）与幅度（Y=O(1) 且与参与率 φ 无关）两个事实，单一机制需同时解释二者。Bonart 提出优雅方案：若已实现与反事实价格均为扩散过程，则信息中性冲击须有白噪声增量，SRIL 自然成立。本文旨在重审该论证，明确其成立的关键前提与局限。

Method: 1) 重新表述并简化 Bonart 的论证，强调其核心要素：市场白化的是每个参与者元订单的平稳流，而非孤立单一元订单。2) 以 Lillo–Mike–Farmer（LMF）模型检验其前提：虽满足扩散假设，但孤立元订单表现为超平方根冲击 φ^{(1-γ)/2}Q^{(1+γ)/2}，因孤立订单统计不可见而被机械定价。3) 构造显式多主体传播子（multi-agent propagator）实现 Bonart 白化，检验其再现 SRIL 的条件。4) 运用体积守恒论证定位扩散性路径的隐含假设。

Result: 1) LMF 模型满足 Bonart 前提却给出超平方根冲击，构成理论反例。2) 所构造的多主体传播子仅当市场能将交易准确归因于发起者时才精确再现 SRIL；而对匿名交易的正确再归因是极艰巨的任务，要求市场如不可思议般高效的信号处理机器运行。3) 流假设给出可证伪预测：时间上充分分离的孤立元订单不应服从 SRIL，但该预测似与已知实证数据不符。4) 体积守恒论证：扩散性只固定冲击的方差（加性、平稳、与年龄无关的量），而 SRIL 是关于平均冲击的定律，后者随年龄以 1/√age 微弱衰减，因而需要一个时间原点。

Conclusion: Bonart 的扩散性白化方案虽然优雅，但其必需前提——市场对元订单流的白化及对交易的正确归因——在理论上被 LMF 模型反例削弱，在实证上其孤立元订单预测与数据不符，且正确归因匿名交易所需的市场效率高得不切实际。此外，扩散性论证只能约束冲击方差而非均值，而 SRIL 作为均值定律需要一个时间原点，这一隐含步骤被扩散性路径所掩盖。体积守恒论证则独立地选出平方根律。

Abstract: The square-root impact law (SRIL), $I = Yσ\sqrt{Q/V}$, bundles two facts that a single mechanism must explain at once: a shape (impact proportional to square-root of traded volume $Q$) and an amplitude ($Y=O(1)$, independent of the participation rate $\varphi$). Bonart has recently proposed an elegant solution: if realized and counterfactual prices are both diffusive, information-neutral impact must have white increments and the SRIL follows without the wart. We reformulate and simplify his argument, and foreground the ingredient he himself regards as essential --- that the market whitens the stationary stream of each participant's metaorders, not any isolated one. The Lillo--Mike--Farmer model satisfies his premises, yet gives an isolated metaorder the super-square-root impact $\varphi^{(1-γ)/2}Q^{(1+γ)/2}$, $γ\in(0,1)$, because single metaorders (i.e. not part of a stream) are statistically invisible and thus priced mechanically. We then construct an explicit multi-agent propagator that realizes Bonart's whitening, and show it reproduces the clean SRIL precisely when the market can attribute trades to their issuer. But correct re-attribution of anonymous trades is a formidable task, which requires markets to behave as implausibly efficient signal processing machines. The stream hypothesis makes a sharp, falsifiable prediction --- isolated metaorders, well separated in time, should not obey the SRIL --- that does not seem agree with empirical data known to us. Independently, a volume-conservation argument singles out the square-root law and lays bare the step the diffusivity route must assume: diffusivity fixes the variance of impact --- an additive, stationary, age-blind quantity --- whereas the SRIL is a law for the mean impact, which marginally decays as $1/\sqrt{\text{age}}$ and therefore demands an origin of time.
