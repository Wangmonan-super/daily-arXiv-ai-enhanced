### [1] [A Rough Instantaneous Squared VIX Model for Term Structure and Option Pricing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07120)
*Tianfeng Yang,Wen Su,Blanka Horvath*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 本文在马尔可夫框架中引入分数阶记忆，构建粗糙瞬时平方VIX（RISVIX）模型，推导VIX期限结构的显式表示与VIX期权的半解析定价公式，并通过两步校准在2018–2025年数据上证实其优于马尔可夫基准。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: VIX期限结构提供跨期限的前瞻性不确定性度量，VIX期权可用于对冲波动率风险；传统马尔可夫框架难以刻画波动率的长记忆/粗糙特征，本文旨在以可处理的直接VIX衍生品模型捕捉分数阶记忆带来的改进。

Method: 在常规马尔可夫框架中引入分数阶记忆，构造RISVIX模型；推导由Mittag–Leffler衰减刻画的VIX期限结构显式表示，并基于仿射Fourier–Laplace变换得到VIX期权的半闭式定价公式；采用两步校准：先由VIX期限结构指数估计记忆参数、均值回复及日期特定初始状态，再将剩余分布参数校准至VIX期权价格。

Result: 使用2018年1月至2025年6月的日度VIX期限结构指数与VIX期权报价，RISVIX在拟合VIX期限结构上优于马尔可夫基准，在高波动期及斜率、曲率维度上改进更大；样本外期权定价MSE与MAE分别降低13.06%和6.33%，在短期限和高VIX状态下收益更明显；与近期文献的其他基准相比亦取得不同程度改进。

Conclusion: 分数阶记忆带来的收益在波动率抬升时尤为显著，且可在可处理的VIX衍生品直接模型内实现，兼顾期限结构拟合与期权定价表现。

Abstract: The VIX term structure provides forward-looking measures of market uncertainty across horizons, while VIX options allow investors to hedge volatility risk. This paper proposes a rough instantaneous squared VIX (RISVIX) model by introducing fractional memory into the conventional Markovian framework. We derive an explicit representation of the VIX term structure governed by Mittag--Leffler decay and a semi-closed-form pricing formula for VIX options based on an affine Fourier--Laplace transform. These results support a two-step calibration procedure that estimates memory, mean reversion, and date-specific initial states from VIX term-structure indices before calibrating the remaining distributional parameters to VIX option prices. Using daily VIX term-structure indices and VIX option quotes from January 2018 to June 2025, we find that RISVIX improves upon the Markovian benchmark in fitting the VIX term structure, with larger gains during elevated-volatility periods and for its slope and curvature. RISVIX also improves out-of-sample option pricing, reducing MSE and MAE by 13.06\% and 6.33\%, respectively, with larger gains at shorter maturities and in high-VIX states. These findings suggest that the gains from fractional memory are particularly pronounced when volatility is elevated and can be obtained within a tractable direct model of VIX derivatives. Comparisons with additional benchmarks from the recent literature also show improvements to varying degrees in both VIX term-structure fitting and option pricing.

### [2] [Information-Theoretic Model for the Growth Optimal Portfolio of Stocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07457)
*Eckhard Platen,Kevin Fergusson*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 该文以信息论原理与经验证据为基础，构建并建模股票增长最优组合（基准）的代理变量；其活动时间的导数刻画交易过度反应，服从快速移动的准平稳标量扩散；据此预测基准对数收益服从自由度略小于4的Student t分布并做实证检验，进而实现以基准为计价单位的基准中性定价与对冲。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统资产定价与对冲模型（如几何布朗运动、高斯收益假设）难以刻画真实的厚尾收益与交易中过度反应的行为特征，缺乏一个既有理论依据又能经验验证的统一框架；作者希望用信息论（增长最优组合、活动时间）替代传统概率假设，为定价与对冲提供更贴近现实的基础。

Method: 1) 依据信息论原理并结合经验证据，构造股票增长最优组合的代理（基准）；2) 对基准的活动时间求导，用以刻画交易行为的过度反应，并假设该导数为快速移动的准平稳标量扩散过程；3) 在该结构下推导基准对数收益的分布形式，理论上预测为自由度略小于4的Student t分布；4) 用实证数据检验该分布预测；5) 以基准为计价单位（numeraire），导出基准中性的定价与对冲公式。

Result: 理论推导得到基准对数收益服从自由度略小于4的Student t分布，实证检验结果与该预测相符；活动时间导数的准平稳扩散假设为过度反应提供了可操作的建模工具；以基准为计价单位可得到基准中性的定价与对冲方案。

Conclusion: 该研究将信息论、行为层面的过度反应与厚尾分布统一在一个可检验、可操作的框架内；Student t（自由度略小于4）的厚尾特征意味着方差存在但四阶矩发散，对风险度量与对冲策略设计具有直接含义；基准中性计价为实务中的定价与对冲提供了新视角。

Abstract: Drawing on information-theoretic principles and empirical evidence, a proxy for the growth-optimal portfolio of stocks (the benchmark) is constructed and modeled. The derivative of its activity time captures overreactions in trading behavior and follows a fast-moving, quasi-stationary scalar diffusion. The benchmark's log-returns are predicted to follow a Student t distribution with degrees of freedom slightly less than four, and this prediction is tested empirically. With the benchmark as numeraire, the derived model enables benchmark-neutral pricing and hedging.

### [3] [Economies of scale and the equity-efficiency trade-off: Evidence from a piped water network in rural Bangladesh](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08031)
*Md. Ahasan Habib,Anna Tompsett*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文通过在孟加拉国农村开展激励相容的支付意愿实验并利用项目成本数据，发现以实现成本有效性最大化为目标的最优接入定价为零：规模经济显著压倒用户付费收入，从而消除公平与效率的权衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 政策制定者在为公共基础设施定价时常预设公平—效率权衡：提高价格可增加收入、抵消成本，但会降低贫困人口的接入率。作者指出，在存在规模经济（基础设施网络中很常见）时，这一权衡会被根本性改变，因此需要实证检验最优定价问题。

Method: 在孟加拉国农村，为农户提供接入新建管网的机会以激励其真实显示偏好，从而测度家庭管道饮用水的支付意愿；同时利用项目成本数据估计规模经济。将二者结合，求解使"每个已接入家庭的成本"最小化的价格。

Result: 使成本有效性最大化的价格为零；规模经济效应强烈压倒用户付费所获得的收入。作者进一步指出，实现零价格最优需满足两个条件：显著的规模经济，以及需求在接近零价格处对价格高度敏感（发展中国家已有充分证据）。

Conclusion: 由于零价格本身最大化公平，规模经济可以消除公平—效率权衡。该结论对基础设施定价政策具有直接含义，但也依赖于规模经济强度与近零价格处需求弹性这两个前提条件。

Abstract: Policymakers who set prices for public infrastructure often anticipate an equity-efficiency trade-off: while higher prices could increase revenues from fee-paying users, helping to offset costs, they may reduce take-up among the poor. But this trade-off is fundamentally altered in the presence of economies of scale, which are common in infrastructure networks. We elicit willingness to pay for a household-level piped drinking water connection in rural Bangladesh, where truthful revelation of preferences is incentivized by the opportunity to connect to a new pipeline network that we construct, and we use project cost data to measure economies of scale. Combining the two, we show that the cost-effectiveness-maximizing price---that which minimizes the cost per connected household---is zero. Economies of scale strongly dominate revenues raised by user fees. More generally, the two conditions needed for a zero price to maximize cost-effectiveness are important economies of scale and high sensitivity of demand to prices near zero, which is well documented across developing countries. Since a zero price naturally maximizes equity, we conclude that economies of scale can eliminate the equity-efficiency trade-off.

</details>


### [4] [Cross-Border Ownership and Input Access: Evidence from the Lithium Supply Chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08430)
*Kei Irizawa*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文构建包含跨境所有权的锂供应链模型，量化"剥离中资股权"与"禁止对华矿石出口"两类政策的效应：剥离股权增加现货矿石供给但锂产量几乎不变；出口禁令因他国冶炼产能无法替代中国产能而降低全球锂供给。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 关键矿产（锂）供应安全引发政策关切，但跨境所有权如何影响供应链（矿石定价、现货销售、竞争程度）缺乏量化分析，政策制定者需要评估剥离股权、出口限制等工具的实际效果。

Method: 利用全球矿山与冶炼厂数据估计结构模型，其中所有权机制通过三条渠道发挥作用：降低冶炼厂有效矿石成本、促使矿山限制现货销售、软化下游竞争；在此基础上进行两类反事实政策模拟——剥离海外生产商中的中资股权、禁止矿石出口至中国。

Result: 剥离股权使现货矿石供给增加，但锂总产量基本不变；维持贸易则保留中国获取矿石的渠道；出口禁令因其他地区冶炼产能无法完全替代中国产量而减少全球锂供给。所有权转移并未将矿石锁定用于本国生产。

Conclusion: 所有权一体化主要通过成本与竞争渠道而非实物预留影响供应链；限制中国获取矿石在现有产能下会造成显著供给成本，相关政策需权衡供应安全目标与全球供给效率。

Abstract: How does cross-border ownership shape the lithium supply chain? Using global data on mines and refineries, I estimate a model in which ownership can lower refiners' effective ore costs, encourage mines to restrict spot sales, and soften competition. To study these effects, I examine policies motivated by national security concerns about reliable access to critical mineral inputs: divestiture of Chinese stakes in overseas producers and a ban on ore exports to China. Divestiture increases spot ore supply but leaves lithium output nearly unchanged, while continued trade preserves Chinese access to ore. The export ban reduces global lithium supply because refining capacity elsewhere cannot fully replace lost Chinese production. Ownership transfers do not reserve ore for domestic production; restricting Chinese access imposes a substantial supply cost at existing capacities.

### [5] [Credibility Clustering for Experience Rating: An Automated Approach to Risk Group Allocation in Group Life Insurance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07110)
*Thom van Rijn,Frank Weber,Michael Merz*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文将多维Bühlmann-Straub可信度模型与k-means聚类结合（"可信度聚类"），用可信度权重自动化团体寿险的风险分组，优于两种k-means基准。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经验费率通过按历史理赔经验调整保费来缓解逆向选择，其关键步骤是把被保险人集体划分为同质风险组。团体寿险中该划分传统上依赖人工决策和任意阈值，易导致低效与结果不一致，因此需要数据驱动的自动化方法。

Method: 提出"可信度聚类"方法：将多维Bühlmann-Straub可信度模型与k-means聚类相结合，在聚类中引入可信度权重，以反映各经验数据统计显著性的差异，使风险分类既符合精算原理又具实践可操作性。

Result: 基于某欧洲大型保险公司真实团体寿险数据的实证结果显示，该框架在可行性、时间稳定性及统计可靠性刻画三方面均优于所比较的两种k-means基准变体。

Conclusion: 可信度加权的聚类能替代人工阈值划分，为经验费率下的风险组分配提供精算合理、稳定可实施的数据驱动方案。

Abstract: In insurance, experience rating plays a crucial role in mitigating adverse selection by adjusting premiums according to historical claims experience. A key step in this process is the classification of insured collectives into homogeneous risk groups. Within group life insurance, this classification traditionally relies on manual decisions and arbitrary thresholds, which may lead to inefficiencies and inconsistent outcomes. This paper proposes a data-driven methodology for automating risk group allocation based on a combination of the multidimensional Bühlmann-Straub model and k-means clustering -- referred to as credibility clustering. By incorporating credibility weights, the method accounts for the varying statistical significance of experience data and ensuresthat risk classifications are both actuarially sound and practically implementable. Empirical results based on real-world group life insurance data from a major European insurer demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms the two k-means benchmark variants considered in the empirical application in terms of viability, temporal stability and accounting for statistical reliability.

</details>
