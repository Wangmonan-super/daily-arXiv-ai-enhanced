### [1] [FOMO: FLEX Options and Market Outcomes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13044)
*Shivesh Prakash,Frank Ferstler,Nicholas Pezolano*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: FLEX期权活动主要通过挂单量与波动率需求在常规期权市场留下可观测痕迹，而非方向性股票收益的预测指标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FLEX期权是允许定制行权价、到期日、行权方式与结算条款但仍保留中央清算的定制化场内合约。作者关注单名FLEX交易是否会在常规挂牌期权市场及标的股票中留下可测量的影响痕迹。

Method: 使用涵盖250个交易日（2025年3月至2026年3月）、41个标的与53个事件日的链接数据集，包括FLEX合并记录、每日未平仓合约文件、挂牌期权链、股票收益与公司行为数据；采用匹配控制的双重差分、日期固定效应回归，并配合Benjamini-Hochberg多重检验校正。

Short-lived equity return effects (CAR_0 difference of -0.85%, p = 0.0442) do not persist over 1-to-5-day horizons. Overall, FLEX activity serves primarily as an observable marker of institutional volatility demand and open-interest positioning rather than a directional stock-return predictor.

### [5] [On the Optimality of Mental Accounting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12587)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有文献主要用自我控制来解释心理账户，用可分偏好解释两阶段预算。作者希望提供一个独立于自我控制的替代性解释，即消费者通过承诺固定支出预算来抵御卖方的市场势力与加价行为。

We then show that what matters is budget rigidity rather than separability of preferences. A consumer who re-optimizes her category budget when prices change is insulated from market power only if categories are substitutes; a rigid budget insulates her in every case. Mental accounting can therefore be read as a commitment device against markups, a rationale that is separate from the self-control motive in the existing literature.
