### [1] [Discretion at the Margin: Threshold Manipulation and Electoral Bans in Colombian Public Procurement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28965)
*Cristian Espinal Maya,Santiago Jiménez Londoño*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文利用哥伦比亚按实体预算档设定、随最低工资年度调整的采购价值门槛，识别出估计值在门槛下的显著聚集（bunching），并量化其代价：门槛下流程竞争更低、折扣更小、中标后更常扩容；选举前直接采购禁令亦引发时序层面的规则规避。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 采购法以价值门槛换取程序裁量权，门槛以下的采购可采用更快、竞争更少的程序，因而买方有动机压低估计值以规避竞争性程序。既有bunching估计常被"整数偏好"混淆，且缺乏对规避行为的成本度量。

Method: 哥伦比亚提供"干净"识别环境：门槛按法律规定分五个预算档、随最低工资每年1月变动，而通常干扰估计的整数金额保持不变。使用SECOP I与II全量采购记录（2011–2026年，SECOP II共8,569,218个流程），以各实体-年度自身门槛作为参照点，检验估计值聚集；并通过无价值上限流程、门槛随最低工资移动、实体仅在自身档位出现尖峰等检验排除整数效应。进一步比较门槛上下流程的投标人数、折扣率与合同变更，并利用总统选举前四个月直接采购禁令作为时序维度的第二变异来源。

Result: 小额采购门槛下出现49,631个超额流程（相当于门槛下最后5%区间观测质量的86%），招标门槛下为3,157个；尖峰随门槛移动而非随整数移动，无上限程序中的聚集小一个数量级。民选地方政府（市长、州长）在小额采购门槛处的聚集强于任命制的国家机构，即使按预算档、采购对象与年份重加权后依然如此。门槛下聚集流程的投标人数约为门槛上的一半，折扣更小，且更常在中标后扩容。选举前直接采购禁令期间直接采购下降95%，禁令开始前两周上升209%，四个选举周期均如此，并部分转向门槛下程序。智利固定UTM门槛呈同样模式，2024年取消的门槛此后无聚集。

Conclusion: 价值门槛会诱发系统性的估计值操纵，且该操纵以降低竞争、减少财政节约和事后合同扩容为代价；民选问责结构与规则时序约束均影响规避强度。门槛设计（尤其固定名义门槛）需权衡程序简化与策略性行为。

Abstract: Public procurement laws trade rules against discretion with value thresholds: below them, buyers use faster and less competitive procedures. Colombia offers an unusually clean setting to measure how much buyers manipulate estimated values to stay below the line and what it costs. Thresholds are entity-specific (five budget bands set by law) and move every January with the minimum wage, while the round numbers that usually confound bunching estimates stay fixed. Using the universe of Colombian procurement records (SECOP I and II, 2011-2026; 8,569,218 SECOP II processes) and each entity-year's own threshold, we find sharp bunching of estimated values just below both legal cutoffs: 49,631 excess processes below the small-purchase threshold (86% of the mass observed in the last five percent below it) and 3,157 below the tender threshold. The spike tracks the threshold, not the round numbers: it moves with the minimum wage, appears only at each entity's own band, and is an order of magnitude smaller for procedures that carry no value cap. Territorial governments, run by elected mayors and governors, bunch more than appointed national agencies at the small-purchase threshold, even after reweighting for budget band, object and year. Processes bunched below the tender threshold attract half as many bidders as those just above it, obtain smaller discounts, and are more often enlarged after award. A second source of variation, the four-month ban on direct contracting before presidential elections, shows that buyers respond to rules on the timing margin: direct contracting falls by 95% during the ban and rises by 209% in the two weeks before it, in each of four electoral cycles, with a partial shift toward below-threshold procedures. Chile's fixed UTM thresholds display the same pattern, and a cutoff abolished in 2024 shows no bunching afterwards.

### [2] [Motivation and Contribution in Open Source Software: A Meta-Analytic Investigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29199)
*Parisa Omidmand,Saeed Nosratabadi*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该元分析（41项研究、178个相关系数、29个独立样本、N=9,632）区分贡献努力与持续意愿，发现八类前因均与意愿正相关，且与意愿的关联普遍强于与努力的关联；内在动机并不比外在动机更能预测努力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 开源及开放协作平台的参与动机研究已逾二十年，但结论相互冲突，且常将"实际贡献的工作量"与"继续贡献的意愿"视为可互换的构念，导致理论积累受限。作者基于自我决定理论与动机挤出理论，系统澄清前因对两类结果变量的差异化作用。

Method: 采用元分析：纳入41项研究；主分析汇总29个独立样本中8个前因变量与"贡献努力"和"持续意愿"的178个相关系数（N=9,632）；比较各前因与两类结果的相关强度差异，并按是否存在声誉系统进行情境化调节分析，同时检验金钱奖励对内在享受的挤出效应。

Result: 7个前因与贡献努力正相关，全部8个前因与持续意愿正相关；每个前因与意愿的关联至少与对努力的关联一样强。与常见假设相反，内在动机对努力的预测并不强于外在动机，但其"意愿—努力"差距更大。声誉主要在有声誉系统的平台上与努力相关；未发现金钱奖励挤出（crowd out）享受的证据。

Conclusion: 贡献努力与持续意愿是需区分的结果变量，动机研究应明确结果构念；平台可通过声誉系统等设计将贡献者的意愿转化为实际投入，且无需过度担忧货币奖励削弱内在动机。研究为平台治理与激励机制设计提供了元分析证据。

Abstract: Research on why people contribute to open source software (OSS) and related open collaboration platforms spans more than two decades, but its findings conflict, and studies often treat the work people contribute and their intention to keep contributing as interchangeable. Drawing on self-determination theory and motivation crowding theory, we meta-analyze 41 studies. The main analysis pools 178 correlations of eight antecedents with contribution effort and continuance intention from 29 independent samples (N = 9,632). Seven antecedents relate positively to effort, all eight relate positively to intention, and each relates at least as strongly to intention as to effort. Contrary to a common assumption, intrinsic motivation is not more strongly related to effort than extrinsic motivation, but its gap between intention and effort is larger. Reputation relates to effort mainly on platforms with a reputation system, and we find no sign that monetary rewards crowd out enjoyment. The findings inform how platforms can turn contributors' intentions into work.

</details>


### [3] [Climate-Driven Supply-Demand Matching Reshapes Global SolarEV Decarbonization Potential](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29344)
*Yuchen Liu,Stefan Pfenninger-Lee,Tosiyuki Nakaegawa,Takuro Kobashi*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该研究在全球5000个网格单元上模拟屋顶光伏与电动汽车电池的耦合系统，发现PV-EV系统最高可满足84%的建筑与交通年用电需求，且仅用容量因子筛选会低估21.9%的全球前1000人口大城市的潜力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 屋顶光伏受发电与需求不匹配（二者均受局地气候影响）制约；城市电动汽车规模化部署在屋顶光伏附近形成分布式储能，可将光伏电力在时间上转移。因此需要评估PV-EV耦合的城市脱碳潜力，并检验常用筛选指标的偏差。

Method: 基于逐小时气象数据和气候驱动的电力需求，在全球5000个网格单元模拟屋顶光伏与电动汽车作为电池的集成系统，评估其满足建筑与出行用电需求的自给能力；并与单一容量因子筛选方法进行对比。

Result: PV-EV系统最高可供给84%的建筑与交通年用电需求；自给率在白天需求与发电匹配、季节波动较小的地区最高；仅用光伏容量因子筛选会低估全球前1000人口城市中的21.9%；显著潜力集中于众多低纬度城市。

Conclusion: 评估PV-EV潜力需考虑光伏发电、电力需求与EV储能的时间耦合，而非仅用发电侧指标；低纬度城市具备较大PV-EV潜力，为更清洁的城市能源系统与碳中和提供路径。

Abstract: Rooftop photovoltaics (PV) can support rapid urban decarbonization, but their contribution is constrained by the imbalance between PV generation and demand, both shaped by local climate. Growing urban electric-vehicle (EV) deployment creates distributed storage near rooftop PV, shifting PV electricity across time. Here we simulate rooftop PV integrated with EVs as batteries across 5,000 grid cells globally, using hourly meteorology and climate-induced demand. We find that PV-EV systems can supply up to 84% of annual building and mobility electricity demand. Self-sufficiency is highest where daytime demand aligns with generation and seasonal variability remains limited. Using PV capacity factor alone to screen PV-EV potential undervalues 21.9% of the world's 1,000 most populous cities because generation-side metrics overlook the temporal coupling of PV generation, electricity demand and EV storage. Substantial PV-EV potential is concentrated in many low-latitude cities, offering a pathway to cleaner urban energy systems while accelerating progress towards carbon neutrality.

### [4] [Understanding the Inner Workings of a State-Space Foundation Model for Limit Order Books](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28753)
*Valentin Mohl,Alexandre Bismuth,Aramis Marti-Shahandeh,George Nigmatulin,Kang Li,Stefan Zohren,Jakob Foerster,Mihai Cucuringu,Anisoara Calinescu*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 该文用探针实验证明，自回归训练的 Mamba-3 状态空间模型在限价订单簿消息流上学习到了可解码、可跨股票泛化、且可因果操控的市场微观结构内部表征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自回归基础模型在生成 LOB 消息流以模拟市场方面已达先进水平，但其内部机理不明，导致研究方向难以聚焦、实际交易部署存在不确定性。作者因此追问：这些模型究竟学到了什么市场微观结构知识。

Method: 在八只美国大盘股的 token 化 LOB 消息流上自回归训练一个 8.2M 参数的 Mamba-3 状态空间模型；采用表征探针（probing）解码订单流失衡等微观结构指标，并配合对照与基线；进行跨股票迁移测试；进一步通过沿特征轴扰动隐藏状态检验表征的因果可控性。

Result: 订单流失衡（短周期价格变动核心驱动因素）可从模型内部状态以 R²≈0.59 解码，显著高于对照与基线；该表征可迁移至未见股票，说明学到的是通用表征而非个股特异模式；在成交量与波动率等其他指标上结论同样成立；沿特征轴推动状态会使模型对下一消息的信念按该特征方向单调变化（剂量效应）。

Conclusion: 序列模型在 LOB 消息流上形成了市场及其动态的内部表征，具备可解码性、跨资产泛化性与因果可控性，证据指向"世界模型"的涌现，为窄化研究方向与评估实际部署提供了机理层面的依据。

Abstract: Autoregressive foundation models offer state-of-the-art capabilities to simulate the market by generating the message flow of limit order books (LOBs). Yet, without understanding their inner workings, research directions are hard to narrow and practical use in real-world trading environments remains uncertain. This study therefore asks what these models actually learn about market microstructure. We find that, similarly to games where sequence models build an internal representation of the board, an 8.2M-parameter Mamba-3 state-space model trained autoregressively on the tokenized message stream of eight large-cap US equities learns an internal representation of the market and its dynamics. In particular, order-flow imbalance, a central driver of short-horizon price moves, is decodable at $R^2 \approx 0.59$, far above controls and baselines. This representation extends to unseen stocks, suggesting that these models learn general representations rather than ticker-specific patterns. The model also generalizes across metrics beyond order-flow imbalance: results hold across traded volume and volatility. Additionally, the representation is causally steerable: pushing the state along a feature's axis shifts the model's next-message beliefs as that feature implies, monotonically in dose, consistent with the emergence of a world model.

</details>

### [5] [Distributions or Quantiles? A Convex Variational Theory of Worst-Case Risk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29652)
*Jianjun Gao,Jonathan Li,Zhen Wang,Yang Zhang*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 该论文以谱风险测度为底层类别，建立了统一的凸变分理论来求解最坏情况风险，覆盖更丰富的模糊集并给出闭式/半闭式解与投影算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 最坏情况风险评价在金融与运筹中至关重要，但既有解析结果零散且大多局限于特定结构的模糊集，缺乏统一分析框架。

Method: 基于分位数或分布两种表示（选取能保持全模糊集凸性的表示）建立表示原理，推导最优性条件以识别最坏情况分布及其风险值；并据此构造基于投影、在固定网格下收敛的算法。

Result: 复现经典中心矩界，并对带尾部压力的Wasserstein模糊集、双侧ES带、非对称部分矩、几何矩界及指数/高斯参考KL邻域给出闭式或半闭式解；在高维组合应用中建立高斯参考下均值锚定联合KL模糊的精确投影恒等式，使标准化最坏情况ES系数经一次标定后适用于所有组合，鲁棒组合选择化为精确二阶锥规划。

Conclusion: 谱风险测度下的凸变分理论统一并扩展了最坏情况风险分析，兼具解析可解性与算法可计算性，并在鲁棒组合优化中实现高效精确建模。

Abstract: Worst-case risk evaluation is central to finance and operations research, yet existing analytical results remain fragmented and largely confined to ambiguity sets with specific structures. Using spectral risk measures as the underlying class, we develop a convex variational theory that unifies these analyses and extends them to richer ambiguity models. The theory rests on a representation principle: use the quantile or distribution formulation that preserves convexity of the full ambiguity set. In both representations, the resulting optimality conditions provide a systematic route to identify the worst-case law and determine its risk value. This approach recovers classical central-moment bounds and yields closed-form or semi-closed-form solutions for practically motivated models involving Wasserstein ambiguity with tail stress, two-sided expected shortfall (ES) bands, asymmetric partial moments, geometric-moment bounds, and exponential- or Gaussian-reference Kullback--Leibler (KL) neighborhoods. Beyond these analytical results, the same theory yields projection-based algorithms with fixed-grid convergence under the stated assumptions. In a high-dimensional portfolio application, we establish an exact projection identity for mean-anchored joint KL ambiguity under a Gaussian reference. The resulting standardized worst-case ES coefficient applies to every portfolio, reducing robust portfolio selection to an exact second-order cone program after a single calibration.

</details>
