### [1] [A Generalized Langevin Model of Latent Liquidity and Concave Price Impact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37872)
*Andrey Itkin*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 将市场冲击建模为对提交订单流的响应，其中潜伏交易者在价格位移超过个体阈值时被激活提供反向流；订单流耗尽该池，其恢复由广义Langevin方程支配。在未耗尽池假设下，聚合个体交易响应可在不预设平方根冲击律的前提下自然导出中间平方根区间，并可解释冲击对波动率的正比关系及与执行时长无关的性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 论文旨在为市场冲击提供一个微观基础模型，解释经验中观察到的平方根冲击规律，而非直接假设该规律。其核心动机在于：将冲击归因于对潜伏交易者（latent traders）反向订单流的耗尽，并通过个体阈值激活机制，把宏观冲击曲线与微观交易者行为联系起来，从而统一描述小订单的线性区、大订单的线性区以及中间的平方根区。

Method: 方法上，模型将市场冲击视为对净提交订单流（提交流减去潜伏交易者反向流）的响应。潜伏交易者在其价格位移超过个体阈值时被激活。订单流会耗尽这一潜伏池，池的恢复由广义Langevin方程刻画，涉及多个时间尺度；其记忆核为有限指数之和，因此Markovian提升是精确的而非近似。在未耗尽池的显式假设下，对个体交易响应进行聚合，得到介于小订单线性区与大订单线性区之间的中间平方根区间。

Result: 主要结果包括：(1) 在不强加平方根冲击律的情况下，通过个体响应的聚合自然出现中间平方根区间；(2) 当阈值和响应随价格噪声缩放时，该区间内的冲击与波动率成正比；(3) 当阈值随执行时长增长时，冲击与执行时长无关；(4) 在恒定显示深度下，按对数价格约定，预期往返成本非负且与记忆核无关；(5) 数值实验显示，池耗尽会缩窄平方根区间，且产生相似单订单冲击的记忆谱在大量先前交易后可表现出不同响应。市场数据校准留待配套论文。

Conclusion: 结论：该模型通过潜伏交易者池的耗尽与恢复机制，为平方根冲击规律提供了微观解释，并统一了线性与平方根区的转换；记忆核的精确Markovian提升使多时间尺度恢复可被严格处理。往返成本非负性对记忆不敏感，说明该性质具有稳健性。模型还揭示了单订单冲击相似但历史交易不同时响应可能分化，强调了记忆谱在冲击预测中的作用。校准与实证验证将在后续论文中进行。

Abstract: We model market impact as the response to submitted order flow net of counterflow from latent traders, activated when price displacements from the level that would prevail without the order exceed individual thresholds. Order flow depletes this pool, and a generalized Langevin equation governs its recovery over several time scales. Its memory kernels are finite sums of exponentials, so its Markovian lift is exact rather than an approximation. For an undepleted pool, aggregation under explicit assumptions on individual trading responses yields an intermediate square-root regime between linear small- and large-order limits, without imposing a square-root impact law. Scaling thresholds and responses with price noise makes impact in this regime proportional to volatility, and thresholds that grow with the execution horizon make it independent of duration. With constant displayed depth, expected round-trip costs are nonnegative under the log-price convention, independently of the memory. Numerical experiments show that depletion narrows the square-root range and that memory spectra producing similar single-order impacts can respond differently after substantial prior trading. Calibration to market data is left to a companion paper.

### [2] [Not All LPs Are Equal: The Active-Passive Gap in Automated Market Maker Liquidity Provision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37963)
*Agathe Sadeghi,Dingyue Liu,Ciamac Moallemi,Xin Wan,Brian Zhu*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 该论文提出基于markout的框架，将Uniswap LP盈利能力分解为主动与被动成分，并发现集中流动性池存在系统性的主动-被动收益差。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统流动性提供分析通常在池层面进行，隐含假设LP同质化，无法揭示不同LP策略间盈利差异，尤其在高性能区块链上集中流动性AMM允许主动调整仓位时这一缺陷更明显。

Method: 提出两种互补方法：（1）LIFO减法法，匹配短生命周期的mint-burn头寸并按流动性份额归属swap级别markout；（2）无穷小LP基准法，直接从AMM价格路径估计完全被动、始终在区间的边际LP表现。应用于以太坊、Arbitrum和Base上的Uniswap v2、v3、v4池。

Result: 被动盈利能力可能与池层面总体盈利能力显著不同。Uniswap v2中两者几乎相同；集中流动性池存在系统性主动-被动差，被动LP表现逊于池层面指标，且以太坊上的差距大于L2，与主动流动性在块时间和排序条件有利时更有用一致。高费率池中被动LP表现更好。LIFO与无穷小估计在方向上基本一致。

Conclusion: AMM中的逆向选择在LP之间并非均匀分布，这对LP策略、费率层级设计和DEX市场质量衡量具有重要启示。

Abstract: Liquidity provision in automated market makers is typically analyzed at the pool level, implicitly assuming LP homogeneity. This aggregate view can hide how liquidity provision outcomes differ between LP strategies, particularly as concentrated liquidity AMM designs operating on high-performance blockchains allow liquidity to be actively repositioned around trades. We develop a markout-based framework to decompose Uniswap LP profitability into active and passive components using two complementary methods: a LIFO subtraction method that matches short-lived mint-burn positions and attributes swap-level markouts by liquidity share; and an infinitesimal LP benchmark that estimates the performance of a fully passive, always-in-range marginal LP directly from the AMM price path.
  We apply these methods to Uniswap v2, v3, and v4 pools on Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Base chains, and find passive profitability can materially differ from aggregate pool profitability.In Uniswap v2, with liquidity distributed evenly and active LP behavior nearly absent, the overall and passive markouts are almost the same. In contrast, concentrated-liquidity pools have a systematic active-passive gap: passive LPs tend to underperform aggregate pool-level measures. The gap is wider on Ethereum than on L2s, consistent with active liquidity provision being more useful when block times and ordering conditions allow LPs to react to incoming flow. In general, passive LPs perform better on higher fee pools. The LIFO and infinitesimal estimates are generally consistent directionally across most pools, providing evidence of the robustness of the decomposition. The results suggest that adverse selection in AMMs is not evenly distributed among LPs, with important implications for LP strategy, fee-tier design and measurement of DEX market quality.

### [3] [Ranking the wrong places: vulnerability assessment, flood losses and risk sharing in Italy and Europe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36349)
*Stefano Blando*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 欧盟用于指导防洪投资排序的复合脆弱性指数无法预测实际洪水损失分布，其误差源于权重设计：该指数主要衡量经济发展水平，而损失沿江河流域集聚。修复无需新数据，重新加权即可显著提升预测力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 政府需在灾前决定防护投资去向、灾后决定补偿额度，欧盟以此复合指数对次国家单元进行脆弱性排序。论文旨在检验该指数能否真实反映各省损失，从而判断其作为资源分配依据的有效性。

Method: 以2005—2024年意大利遭遇同一洪水的省份为样本，比较指数排名与实际损失（含/不含灾害、暴露度与收入控制变量）的相关性；分解误差来源；并在2010—2020年每个预测起点上预先注册重建该指数及八个竞争指数进行对比；考察意大利公开洪水风险图与重新加权后的指数分项表现；最后在1,142个欧洲单元中检验普适性并量化储备金池效应。

Result: 指数排名与损失相关性极低（ρ为-0.06至0.09）；误差主要存在于共享流域的省群之间（超过一半）。预测检验中该指数在两项任务上均垫底：前十省份仅捕捉16%后续损失（低于随机抽签），而洪水风险图达44%；与后续损失的秩相关为0.03，而其分项按历史损失重新加权后可达0.42。2013—2022年记录的123亿欧元损失中85%未获国家转移支付覆盖，按同一方式评分的预防资金分配不优于随机。因损失在国界内集聚，欧洲国家池需比同规模跨境池多39%的储备；在1,142个欧洲单元中指数以同样方式失效，修复效果较弱。

Conclusion: 欧盟复合脆弱性指数并不能预测洪水损失的空间分布，其缺陷在于权重偏向经济发展而非水文风险。仅通过历史损失重新加权现有分项即可大幅改善，无需新增数据；现行指数导致大量损失由地方自担，且跨境风险池可显著降低所需储备。

Abstract: Before a flood, governments decide where to invest in protection; after it, how much of the loss to compensate. The European Union informs the first decision with a composite index that ranks every subnational unit by vulnerability. This paper tests the ranking by comparing Italian provinces struck by the same flood between 2005 and 2024. The published index does not order provinces by the losses they suffer, with or without controls for hazard, exposure and income ($ρ$ between -0.06 and 0.09). The failure lies in what it weights. As published, the index mostly measures economic development, while losses follow water: they arrive in recurring groups of provinces that share a river basin, and more than half of the ranking error lies between those groups. In a pre-registered test that rebuilds it and eight rivals at every forecast origin from 2010 to 2020, the index comes last on both questions an allocator asks. On where damage will land, its top ten provinces capture 16 per cent of later damage, less than a random draw; Italy's public flood hazard map, blind to losses, captures 44. On who loses more from the same water, its rank correlation with later losses is 0.03; its own components, reweighted on past losses, reach 0.42. Neither repair needs new data. The misranked loss is paid locally. Of EUR 12.3 billion recorded over 2013-2022, 85 per cent was not covered by national transfers, and prevention funding, scored the same way, does no better than chance. Because correlated losses cluster within borders, national pools in Europe need 39 per cent more reserve than cross-border pools of the same size. The index fails the same way across 1,142 European units, where the repairs are weaker.

### [5] [Classification as Search Infrastructure: How Category Creation, Addition and Cleanup Shape Knowledge Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37343)
*Kerstin Hötte,Nicolò Barbieri,Su Jung Jee*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该文研究分类架构修订（类别创建、路径增加、类别清理）如何通过改变对象进入检索视野的路径来影响检索效率，并以2013年创建、2020年修订的Y02/Y04绿色技术分类为实证场景。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分类系统决定检索者能否找到相关对象，但既有研究较少关注分类架构修订本身如何通过改变"对象进入考虑范围的路径"来影响检索；作者希望厘清分类距离、路径选择与更新成本等机制，而非仅把分类视为静态标签体系。

Method: 理论层面提出机制：横切类别可缩短分类距离、降低翻译成本；在成熟系统中增加路径会提高路径选择与更新成本；类别清理可恢复对比。实证上利用CPC中Y02/Y04绿色技术代码2013年创建与2020年修订这一外生冲击，将受处理专利与同"技术-世代"单元内未变更的绿色专利匹配，估计审查员与申请人各自在首次引用时点和引用量上的效应（引用风险模型）。

Result: 类别创建使审查员首次引用风险提高约25%；在成熟系统中，增加路径使审查员引用风险下降13–15%；清理则使风险提高14%、引用量提高12%。效应在审查员身上普遍更强，因其检索任务与专利分类耦合更紧。时点与数量未必同步：创建与进入绿色领域改变时点但未检测到引用量变化，清理则同时提高速度与记录到的覆盖范围。

Conclusion: 分类基础设施会改变相关对象被发现的时点，即便整体记录到的关注度不变；更高的分类细化程度并不必然提升检索效率。分类架构设计应被视为影响检索行为的主动变量，而非中性背景。

Abstract: Classification systems shape how searchers find relevant objects. We study how revisions to classification architecture affect retrieval by changing the routes through which objects enter consideration. We theorize that creating a cross-cutting category can reduce classificatory distance and translation costs, whereas adding routes to an established system can increase route-selection and updating costs; category cleanup can restore contrast. We match treated patents to unchanged green patents within technology-cohort cells and estimate examiner- and applicant-specific effects on first-citation timing and citation volume. Category creation is associated with a 25% higher examiner first-citation hazard. In the mature system, additions are associated with 13-15% lower examiner hazards, while cleanup is associated with a 14% higher hazard and 12% higher citation volume. Effects are generally stronger for examiners, whose search task is more tightly coupled to patent classification. Timing and volume need not move together: creation and green-domain entry shift timing without detectable changes in volume, while cleanup increases both speed and recorded reach. Classification infrastructure may therefore change when relevant objects are found even when overall recorded attention is unchanged, and greater classificatory detail need not improve search efficiency.

### [6] [A tale of two allocations: Risk capital contributions versus risk contributions in the tail](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36177)
*Nawaf Mohammed,Edward Furman*

Main category: q-fin.RM

TL;DR: 本文比较了两种自然的风险成分贡献比例配置：条件尾部期望（CTE）下的比例配置与几何尾部期望（GTE）下的成分期望实现份额。作者证明两者一般不同，刻画了一致性条件、不一致时的排序，以及渐近分离，并给出在独立指数离散模型和共单调情形下的具体结论。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在聚合尾部风险资本分配中，存在两种自然的按比例分配风险成分贡献的直觉方式：CTE下的分配份额，以及GTE下的期望实现份额。二者在实践中均被使用，但此前缺乏对其异同、排序及极端尾部行为的系统刻画。作者希望明确二者何时一致、何时一方占优，以及其差异在极端尾部如何演变。

Method: 将两种比例分配统一表示为给定聚合风险下成分条件风险份额的加权平均：CTE按尾部情景的严重程度加权，GTE则均匀加权，其差为归一化尾部协方差。在允许原子、带符号风险及任意尾部区域的任意随机向量框架下，刻画一致性（条件风险份额在尾部恒为常数），并在温和单峰条件下用其单调性刻画占优关系。进一步考察独立指数离散模型（异构自然参数）与共单调情形，并分析极端尾部下比例CTE与GTE配置的极限关系，用期望损失（ES）与在险价值（VaR）之比控制。

Result: 两种比例分配一般不同，其差为归一化尾部协方差。当且仅当条件风险份额在尾部恒定时二者在整个尾部一致；在温和单峰条件下，占优由该条件风险份额的单调性刻画。在参数异质的独立指数离散模型中，精确一致仅对尺度泊松族可能；在共单调情形下，一致等价于分位函数成比例。极端尾部中，比例CTE与GTE的极限关系由ES/VaR之比主导：有界性保证共同极限，收敛到1迫使二者合并，发散则可使极限分离。

Conclusion: CTE与GTE两种比例尾部风险配置的差异本质上是尾部条件风险份额的协方差，其一致性、排序与渐近行为可由条件风险份额的常数性、单调性及ES/VaR之比刻画。结果覆盖带原子、带符号风险及任意尾部的广泛随机向量，并在指数离散族与共单调情形给出简洁判别准则，为实践中按比例分配尾部风险资本提供了理论依据。

Abstract: We compare two natural proportional notions of a risk component's contribution to the aggregate tail risk of a collection of risks: the fraction of aggregate tail risk capital allocated to the component under Conditional Tail Expectation (CTE), and the component's expected realized share of aggregate risk under Geometric Tail Expectation (GTE). The resulting proportional allocations generally differ. For arbitrary random vectors-allowing atoms, signed risks, and any tail domain--we establish when the allocations agree, determine their ordering when they do not, and characterize their asymptotic separation.
  Both proportional allocations are weighted averages of the conditional risk share of a component given the aggregate: the proportional CTE allocation weights tail scenarios by severity, whereas the proportional GTE allocation weights them uniformly. Their difference is therefore a normalized tail covariance. The proportional CTE and GTE allocations agree throughout a tail exactly when the conditional risk share is constant there; under a mild unimodality condition, dominance is characterized by its monotonicity. Among independent exponential dispersion models with heterogeneous natural parameters, exact agreement is possible only for the scaled Poisson family; under comonotonicity, it is equivalent to proportional quantile functions.
  In the extreme tail, the limiting relationship between the proportional CTE and GTE allocations is governed by the ratio of Expected Shortfall to Value-at-Risk: boundedness ensures common limits, convergence to one forces the allocations to merge, and divergence can cause their limits to separate.
