*Marcus Gawronsky*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 本文将四种表格基础模型（TFM）的冻结内部表示用作金融距离度量，嵌入空间套利定价模型（S-APT），在美国股票（2011–2025）上检验其相对直接文本距离的经济增益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: TFM 在中小规模表格基准上预测性能优异，但可解释性差，难以满足受监管金融应用对解释性的要求；内部表示已被广泛用于探测神经网络所学内容，但在金融经济结构度量上的价值尚未被系统研究。

Method: 以四种 TFM 的冻结表示构造企业间金融不相似度，作为 S-APT 中溢出效应的空间权重；与基于同一披露文本的直接距离（基线度量）对比，在 2011–2025 年美国股票样本上估计因子模型外溢收益，并逐步纳入更广的 SEC 申报数据。

Result: 当纳入广泛的 SEC 申报数据后，基于 TFM 表示的估计增益相对直接距离基线持续为正。

Conclusion: TFM 可从黑箱预测器重新定位为测度经济结构的工具，可直接应用于同业筛选、风险归因与网络化资产定价；基础模型的内部表示本身应作为经济对象加以研究，开辟"预训练模型知道企业什么"的新研究方向。

Abstract: Spatial arbitrage pricing theory (S-APT) extends factor models to allow returns to spill over between connected firms, beyond what common risk factors explain. In recent years, tabular foundation models (TFMs) have matched or outperformed tuned gradient-boosted and bagged ensembles on small and medium-sized tabular benchmarks. Although their predictive performance is strong, these models reveal little about what they have learned, which limits their use in regulated financial applications that require explanation. Internal representations are widely used to probe what neural networks learn and to measure similarity. In this paper, we use the frozen representations of four TFMs as a measure of financial dissimilarity in a spatial asset-pricing model of US equities from 2011 to 2025, and find that their estimated gains over a direct distance between the same disclosures become consistently positive once broad SEC filing data are included. This reframes tabular foundation models from black-box predictors into instruments for measuring economic structure, with immediate applications to peer selection, risk attribution and network-based asset pricing. More broadly, our findings suggest that the internal representations of foundation models, not only their predictions, deserve study as economic objects, opening a new line of research on what pretrained models know about firms.

### [2] [Semivariance and Semivolatility in Portfolio Selection: A Unified Framework for Downside-Risk Portfolio Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32638)

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文按风险度量对权重的正齐次度对下行风险组合模型进行分类，给出长仓下行风险的非退化条件，使风险平价、最分散化与Sortino比率问题良定义；该条件下两类比率问题可化为凸规划，并用真实数据比较十七种策略。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 以风险度量的正齐次度为分类依据；针对均值、零与市场指数收益三种阈值，提出长仓下行风险的非退化条件；证明两类比率问题的凸性归约，利用半波动率的可微性使风险预算配置达到预设预算；刻画均值下方半波动率的可加性条件；在道琼斯与纳斯达克100成分股上做滚动窗口回测，比较十七种长仓策略并用块自助法做族内推断。

Conclusion: 正齐次度是区分下行风险组合模型适用性的关键维度，非退化条件是长仓下行风险模型良定义的核心前提，半波动率兼具凸性与可微性，适合统一处理风险预算与比率优化。

Abstract: In this paper, we study downside risk in portfolio selection beyond minimum-risk models, where semivariance and its square root, semivolatility, yield the same portfolio. Downside-risk models are often presented as direct extensions of their variance-based counterparts, although different classes of portfolio problems require different properties of the risk measure. We classify these models by the degree of positive homogeneity of the risk measure in the weights: degree one makes risk contributions add up to the portfolio risk and keeps diversification and reward-to-risk ratios scale invariant. For three thresholds, the portfolio mean, zero and a market-index return, we state a non-degeneracy condition on long-only downside risk under which the risk-parity, most-diversified and Sortino-ratio problems are well posed. Under this condition, the two ratio problems reduce to convex programs, and the differentiability of semivolatility ensures that the risk-budgeting formulation attains the prescribed budgets. We also characterize when below-mean semivolatility is additive. Finally, we provide an out-of-sample analysis on two real-world datasets of total returns, the current constituents of the Dow Jones and Nasdaq-100 indexes. We compare seventeen long-only strategies, covering the minimum-risk, reward-to-risk, risk-parity and most-diversified families, in a rolling-window backtest, and test the differences within each family by block-bootstrap inference.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有随机占优研究多局限于静态建模框架下的整数阶占优准则：整数阶尺度过于粗糙，且现实决策多发生于动态环境，静态方法难以适用，因此需要更精细的占优刻画与动态决策框架。

Method: 构建基于分数阶随机占优准则的动态随机多属性群决策模型；定义分数阶随机占优下的优先度，该优先度包含整数阶随机占优优先度作为特例，并适用于效用函数非处处凹的决策者；模型允许在不同决策期内增删属性与方案，以适配一般动态决策过程；最后通过数值算例与对比分析验证有效性。

Result: 数值算例与对比分析表明所提方法具有有效性与稳健性，相较于传统方法具有更广的适用性；分数阶优先度能更精确、灵活地刻画方案间的占优关系。

Conclusion: 该研究丰富了动态随机多属性群决策的理论基础，有助于提升决策结果的可靠性与精确性，并将随机占优准则从静态整数阶推广至动态分数阶框架。

Abstract: The stochastic dominance criterion has been widely employed in stochastic multi-attribute decision making as a robust tool for quantitatively comparing decision-makers' preferences over alternatives. Existing research predominantly relies on integer-order stochastic dominance criteria within static modeling frameworks. Consequently, the proposed model captures the dominance relations between alternatives with greater accuracy and flexibility. Moreover, the model allows for the addition or removal of attributes and alternatives across decision periods, thereby exhibiting the applicability to any dynamic decision process. Numerical examples and comparative analyses show the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed approach, highlighting its broader applicability compared with conventional methods. This study enriches the theoretical foundation of dynamic stochastic multi-attribute group decision making and contributes to improving the reliability and precision of decision outcomes.

### [4] [Burning the Bridge: Agentification and Domestic Value Added](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31518)
*Eduardo Levy Yeyati*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 企业以外国AI智能体替代本地劳动，可在降低本土收入的同时提高每付费工时产出。文章在小国开放经济框架下，比较可贸易部门与本地服务业采用智能体的收益分配阈值，并以巴西、墨西哥数据给出出口增长要求。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有讨论多聚焦AI对生产率与就业的总量影响，缺乏对"生产率上升、国内收入下降"这一背离机制的结构化分析；尤其缺少区分可贸易部门与本地服务业在智能体替代下不同约束条件的理论刻画与量化门槛。

Method: 构建小国开放经济模型，出口部门与本地服务业均使用智能体；引入工资保留下限与供应商议价份额；以不变价格增加值、消费与外汇约束为口径，推导维持收入与消费所需的出口增长条件；用巴西国民账户校准阈值，并结合巴西、墨西哥历史账户做情形演示。

Result: 在巴西账户参照下，供应商议价份额约高于0.15时，即便外国成本可忽略，本地服务业进一步采用智能体也会压低不变价格增加值；可贸易部门的对应阈值约为0.66。更便宜的智能体放松消费所需出口增长，出口价格下降则提高该要求。

Conclusion: 智能体替代的生产率收益与国内收入存在实质性权衡，且该权衡在本地服务业远早于可贸易部门显现；国内能力随时间的退化会提高复归本地生产的成本，增强供应商续约时的议价能力，政策需同时关注议价份额、外汇获取能力与出口增长要求。

Abstract: Firms that replace local work with foreign AI agents can produce more per paid hour while generating less income at home. The corresponding threshold in tradables is about 0.66. We derive separate export-growth requirements for preserving income and consumption and illustrate them using historical accounts for Brazil and Mexico. Cheaper agents ease the consumption requirement; lower export prices raise it. Over time, declining domestic capabilities can also make returning to local production more expensive, allowing suppliers to charge more at renewal.

  <summary>Details</summary>

### [6] [Grokipedia stretches the US political spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32420)
*Philipp Koch,Alexander Posztos*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 大语言模型生成的百科条目（Grokipedia）并非简单偏向某一党派，而是与维基百科相比以约两倍斜率"拉伸"政治光谱：越保守的政客其传记显得作者越保守，越自由的政客则越被推向更左，导致九成共和党人条目显得比维基更保守，而民主党人仅约一半如此。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 语言模型已开始撰写被人类和其他AI系统查阅的参考性著作，Grokipedia自我定位为比众包编辑的维基百科偏见更小的替代品；因此需要实证检验其是否真的降低了偏见，以及偏见可能以何种形式出现。

Method: 选取1,614名现任及历任民主党与共和党政治人物（从Andrew Jackson到现任国会议员），比较其在Grokipedia与维基百科上于2026年2月与11月的传记条目；以"条目读起来像由多保守的作者撰写"为偏见度量，并结合1,022名拥有国会点名投票记录的政治人物的意识形态评分，进行线性回归分析；另以Elon Musk的America PAC竞选支出作为额外检验。

Result: 约十分之九的共和党人条目在Grokipedia上读起来比维基百科更像出自更保守的作者之手，而民主党人仅约一半如此；政客意识形态可解释这一不对称：感知作者保守度随政客意识形态线性上升，且Grokipedia上的斜率约为维基百科的两倍；在America PAC投入资金的选区，共和党人条目未见额外偏见。

Conclusion: Grokipedia的偏见不是党派偏向，而是对政治光谱的拉伸——把最自由政客的传记推得更左、最保守政客的推得更右；这说明模型生成的参考性著作可能以"极化放大"而非"立场站队"的方式偏离中立性。

We compare the biographies of 1,614 Democratic and Republican current and former politicians, from Andrew Jackson to members of the current Congress, on both encyclopedias in February and November 2026. For nine in ten Republicans, Grokipedia's biography reads as if written by a more conservative author than Wikipedia's. For Democrats, this holds only about half the time. We find the politicians' ideology explains this asymmetry. For 1,022 politicians with Congressional roll call voting records, how conservative the author seems rises linearly with the politician's ideology and about twice as steeply on Grokipedia as on Wikipedia. Republicans in races where Elon Musk's America PAC spent money show no extra bias. Rather than favoring a party, Grokipedia stretches the political spectrum, moving the biographies of the most liberal politicians further left and of the most conservative politicians further right.
