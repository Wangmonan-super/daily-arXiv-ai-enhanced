### [1] [The Market's Conditioning Representation: Equilibrium, Crowding, and Convention Multiplicity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18299)
*Alejandro Rodriguez Dominguez*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文提出"表征均衡"（representation equilibrium）框架，将资产定价模型中的信息集从外生给定转为内生选择。投资组合可选择表征，其诱导的风险暴露影响价格，价格反馈又通过认证机制改变可许可的表征，形成配置—价格—认证的固定点回路。文章在紧致性、预解正则性和连续认证条件下证明均衡存在，并揭示位置拥挤与表征拥挤的区分，以及由谱统计量决定的三类持仓路径动态。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统资产定价模型通常假设固定的信息集，但现实中市场参与者的信息结构、表征方式（representation）是内生的：资产组合的表征选择会通过风险暴露影响价格，而价格又反过来影响表征的价值与合法性。本文试图将这种配置—价格—认证的反馈回路纳入一般均衡框架，以解释偏离基本面定价、多重自证惯例（self-confirming conventions）等市场现象。

Method: 作者构建一个表征均衡框架：资本在不同表征间的配置决定总持仓与清算溢价；价格反馈改变表征价值，并通过"因果认证"（causal certification）决定哪些表征被许可。均衡是这一配置—价格—认证回路的不动点。文章区分了通过市场冲击产生的位置拥挤（position crowding）与由驱动空间重叠产生的表征拥挤（representation crowding），后者通过基不变的信息容量成本定价。在紧致性、预解正则性与连续认证假设下，证明任意切换成本下均衡存在；在稳定认证格内，信息拥堵产生凹种群博弈，小增益条件下局部唯一。通过对交叉冲击、协方差与已部署容量的组合谱统计量，划分三个持仓路径区域。

Result: 在确定的配置下，一个结合交叉冲击、协方差与已部署容量的谱统计量决定三类持仓路径动态：低于1/2时基本面解唯一；边界处仅剩无创新的偏差；高于1/2时失稳方向支撑连续统的自证惯例。单调冲击位于唯一性区域内，而不定冲击本身不足以产生多重性。中介对冲可产生不定响应但同时保持往返交易成本高昂。内生风险容量限制惯例振幅，最小二乘学习使主导惯例趋于中性而非吸引。经验部分不做阈值估计，而是记录与表征拥挤一致的样本外、驱动特定信号，并给出直接测量的条件。

Conclusion: 本文建立了内生信息架构的定价理论，表明市场表征的选择不是外生给定而是内生于均衡。通过谱临界值（1/2）刻画了从唯一性到惯例多样性的转变，为理解定价异常、自证预期及中介行为提供了统一框架。经验证据与表征拥挤的理论预测一致，未来可在此基础上发展直接测量方法。

Abstract: Asset-pricing models typically condition on a fixed information set. This paper endogenises the market's conditioning architecture by allowing portfolios to choose representations whose induced exposures affect prices. Capital allocated across representations determines aggregate positions and the clearing premium, while price feedback changes representation value and, through causal certification, admissible representations. A representation equilibrium is the fixed point of this configuration--price--certification loop. The framework separates position crowding through market impact from representation crowding generated by driver-space overlap and priced through a basis-invariant information-capacity cost. Under compactness, resolvent regularity and continuous certification, equilibrium exists at every switching cost; within a stable certification cell, informational congestion yields a concave population game and local uniqueness under a small-gain condition. At a settled configuration, a spectral statistic combining cross-impact, covariance and deployed capacity determines three position-path regimes: below one half the fundamental solution is unique; at the boundary only innovation-free deviations remain; above it, destabilising directions support a continuum of self-confirming conventions. Monotone impact lies inside the uniqueness region, while indefinite impact alone is not sufficient for multiplicity. Intermediary hedging can generate an indefinite response while round trips remain costly. Endogenous risk capacity bounds convention amplitudes, and least-squares learning makes the dominant convention neutral rather than attracting. The threshold is not estimated in data; instead, the empirical exercise documents an out-of-sample, driver-specific signature consistent with representation crowding and states the conditions required for direct measurement.

### [2] [Accounting for intra-household joint travel in agent-based transport simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18657)
*Javaudin Lucas,Araldo Andrea,Coulombel Nicolas*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出一个三步方法论，将家庭内部联合出行（joint home-based tours）整合进基于代理人的交通模拟模型中：随机森林分类器识别联合出行、Multinomial Logit模型估计联合出行专属的模式选择、惩罚逻辑回归用于驾驶员/乘客分配。在巴黎地区的应用中，该方法成功在合成人口中复现了联合出行的份额和模式分布。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 家庭内部联合出行（家庭成员共同出发、共享活动、共同返回）占日常出行的显著比例，但在交通模拟中被系统性忽略。将联合出行与单独出行混在一个模式选择框架中会导致偏好参数估计偏差，从而影响政策评估的可靠性。

Method: 提出三步骤方法：(1) 随机森林（Random Forest）分类器识别联合出行；(2) 多项Logit（Multinomial Logit）模型估计联合出行专属的模式选择；(3) 惩罚逻辑回归（Penalized Logistic Regression）进行驾驶员/乘客分配。应用于巴黎地区，使用家庭出行调查（household travel survey）数据在合成人口中建模。

Result: 在巴黎地区应用中，该方法成功在合成人口中复现了观察到的联合出行份额和模式分布，验证了框架的有效性。

Conclusion: 该框架能够更可靠地评估对联合出行与单独出行影响不同的政策（如HOV车道或家庭公交票价折扣），弥补了现有交通模拟模型对联合出行系统性忽略的缺陷。

Abstract: Intra-household joint home-based tours - trips in which household members depart together, engage in shared activities, and return together - represent a significant share of daily travel, yet are systematically ignored in transport simulations. Conflating joint and solo tours within a single mode choice framework introduces bias in preference parameter estimates. This paper proposes a three-step methodology to integrate joint tours in agent-based transport models: a Random Forest classifier to identify joint tours, a Multinomial Logit model estimating mode choice specific to joint tours, and a Penalized Logistic Regression for driver/passenger assignment. Applied to the Paris region using household travel survey data, the methodology successfully replicates observed joint tour shares and mode distributions in a synthetic population. The proposed framework enables more reliable evaluation of policies whose impacts differ between joint and solo travel, such as HOV lanes or family transit fare discounts.

### [3] [Multi-Level Market Making with Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18195)
*Patrick Cheridito,Moritz Weiss*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文提出一个用于限价订单簿做市的强化学习框架，通过动态提交跨多个价格水平、不同规模的市场单和限价单来最大化交易收益并控制库存大小。算法使用多元logistic-normal分布建模订单分配，采用deep-set编码器将变长订单集聚合为固定维潜在表示，并引入基于势能的奖励塑形以加速学习且不改变最优策略。在三种模拟市场环境中验证了性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 做市商需要在动态的限价订单簿环境中同时管理订单提交策略和库存风险，传统方法难以应对高维动作空间和变长订单集表示问题，亟需一个能够自适应学习最优做市策略的框架。

Method: 1) 采用强化学习框架，动作空间覆盖多个价格水平上的市价单和限价单及不同订单规模；2) 使用多元logistic-normal分布对订单分配进行参数化建模；3) 引入deep-set编码器将变长订单集特征聚合为固定维度的潜在表示；4) 采用基于势能的奖励塑形（potential-based reward shaping）加速收敛同时保证不改变最优策略。

Result: 在三种模拟市场环境（噪声交易者、战术交易者、战略交易者）中展示了方法的性能，表明算法能在不同市场微观结构条件下有效学习做市策略。

Conclusion: 该框架通过深度强化学习结合有效的状态表示和奖励塑形技术，为限价订单簿中的做市问题提供了一个可扩展且性能良好的解决方案，能够适应不同类型的市场参与者和交易模式。

Abstract: We introduce a reinforcement learning framework for market making in a limit order book. Our algorithm aims to maximize trading revenue by dynamically submitting market and limit orders of varying sizes across multiple price levels while controlling inventory size. We use multivariate logistic-normal distributions to model order allocations and employ a deep-set encoder to aggregate features from variable-length order sets into a fixed-dimensional latent representation. Additionally, we incorporate potential-based reward shaping to accelerate learning without altering the optimal policy. We illustrate the performance of the method in three simulated market environments consisting of noise traders who submit random trades, tactical traders who respond to instantaneous volume imbalance, and strategic traders who trade in the direction of an exponentially weighted volume imbalance signal.

</details>
