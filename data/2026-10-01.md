### [1] [Say, Echo, Do: Strategic Narratives and Revealed Positioning in Financial Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38545)
*Ali Atiah Alzahrani*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文研究金融市场中"言行不一"现象：构建机构表态(Say)、媒体复述(Echo)与真实持仓(Do)三重视角，提出线性二次模型刻画"唱空买入"的最优条件，并给出无分布假设的Say-Do协方差判据，配合三项测量工具在仿真市场中验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 金融文本的机器学习信号通常把机构表态和媒体复述当作价值证据，但塑造叙事的一方可能反向交易，因此需要判断"何时该跟随言辞、何时该反向淡化"。

Method: 建立知情机构在轻信人群前先发言后交易的部分均衡线性二次模型，推导"唱空买入"最优的条件 φ²<2λk<φ；用无分布假设的恒等式将可观测Say-Do协方差与言辞预测方向挂钩；测量层面：(i) 结合到达时间与嵌入相似度的精确因子化后验识别回声文章；(ii) 收益对齐的对比学习目标，其界在嵌入距离平方为结果距离平方的递增仿射函数时取等，并给出最紧的损失证书；(iii) 路径签名统计量判定谁先行动。

Result: 在29个已知真值的受控仿真市场中，回声情绪对收益的预测系数均显著为负；滚动Say-Do相关以AUC=0.90标记虚假警报事件；收益对齐嵌入按"后果"而非"主题"组织标题；并报告了工具的失效场景。

Conclusion: 言辞与真实仓位偏离时，言辞具有负向预测价值，应当反向淡化；所提判据与测量工具在受控环境中有效，但存在明确适用范围与失效情形。

Abstract: Machine-learning signals built from financial text treat what institutions say, and what the media repeat, as evidence about value. But whoever shapes a narrative may be trading against it. We study markets with three observable voices: institutional statements (Say), media repetition (Echo) and revealed positioning (Do). We ask when words should be followed and when they should be faded. In a linear-quadratic model of an informed institution that speaks and trades before a partly credulous crowd, talking an asset down while buying it is optimal exactly when $\varphi^2<2λk<\varphi$. A distribution-free identity then shows that when the observable Say-Do covariance is negative, words carry negative predictive content and should be faded. For measurement, we derive (i) an exact factorised posterior over which articles are echoes, combining arrival times with embedding similarity; (ii) a return-aligned contrastive objective that attains its bound exactly when squared embedding distances are an increasing affine function of squared outcome distances, with the tightest loss-based certificate of which neighbour rankings survive imperfect training; and (iii) a path-signature statistic for who moved first. In a controlled market with known ground truth, echo sentiment predicts returns with a significantly negative sign in all 29 simulated markets, the rolling Say-Do correlation flags false-alarm events with an AUC of 0.90, and return-aligned embeddings organise headlines by consequence rather than topic. We also report where the tools fail.

### [2] [Food Insecurity Among Military Veterans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39932)
*Senan Hogan-Hennessy,Seungmin Lee,Christopher B. Barrett,John Hoddinott,Matthew P. Rabbitt*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 使用PSID新数据与越战抽签征兵法识别因果效应，发现兵役对退役军人家庭粮食不安全无不利影响，其粮食不安全水平反而低于非退役军人家庭，且更少参与联邦营养援助项目。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 退役军人退役后常面临多重困境，但"粮食不安全"是否是其中之一尚不明确。已有研究对兵役与粮食不安全之间的因果关系缺乏可信识别，本文旨在检验兵役是否对粮食不安全产生长期影响。

Method: 基于PSID（收入动态追踪调查）新数据，比较退役军人主导家庭与非退役军人主导家庭的粮食不安全状况及联邦营养援助项目参与情况；利用越战时期抽签征兵（lottery draft）作为工具变量，识别兵役的因果效应。

Result: 退役军人主导家庭的粮食不安全程度低于非退役军人主导家庭，且联邦营养援助项目参与率更低；使用越战抽签征兵进行因果识别后，未发现兵役对粮食不安全产生不利影响的证据。

Conclusion: 兵役并未加重粮食不安全，二者关系可能受选择性因素驱动。政策应重点帮扶现役军人以及不太可能成为传统家庭户主的退役军人，以更有效应对退役军人群体中的粮食不安全问题。

Abstract: Veterans face multiple hardships after they leave the military. Is food insecurity one of these hardships? This paper examines the long-term effects of military service on food insecurity using new data from the PSID. Military veteran-headed households experienced lower food insecurity than non-veteran-headed households, and participated less in federal nutrition assistance programs. We use the Vietnam-era lottery draft to identify causal effects, and find no evidence that military service adversely affects food insecurity. Our results suggest policies that assist currently enlisted service members, and veterans unlikely to head a traditional household, may most effectively tackle food insecurity among military veterans.

### [3] [Spouse-Protected Tontines: Household Decumulation via Neural-Network Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39569)
*Duy-Minh Dang,Yukan Perumal*

Main category: q-fin.PM

TL;DR: 本文构建"配偶保护型"tontine：首次死亡不触发池内转移，同一账户延续至合同终止，若养老金领取者先去世则进入仅配偶领取的延续阶段。推导精算公平条件与有限池死亡率信用分配规则（事后预算精确平衡），在大池同质近似下建立多维去累积最优控制问题，目标为期望实际家庭支付与终端短缺CVaR的权衡，并用全局时间神经网络求解。基于澳大利亚数据的实验表明配偶延续阶段是显著且持续的家庭事件，book层面分散化可消除代表性合同的大部分上尾超成本而不改变期望成本。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统tontine在首次死亡时发生池内转移，导致遗属（配偶）面临支付中断与保障缺失。本文动机在于设计一种对配偶友好的tontine结构，使首次死亡仅改变家庭状态而不产生池转移，从而为配偶提供延续收入，同时保持tontine的死亡率信用共享优势，并量化该设计带来的配偶延续成本及其在合同层面与book层面的风险特征。

Method: 1) 设计spouse-protected tontine：首次死亡不转移池资金，账户保留于家庭合同直至终止，若领取者先逝则进入仅配偶延续阶段；2) 推导合同级精算公平条件与有限池死亡率信用分配规则，保证事后预算精确平衡；3) 在同质大池近似下，建立代表性家庭的多维去累积问题，控制变量为领取者存活期间的提取与再平衡；4) 目标函数权衡期望累积实际家庭支付与终端短缺（相对家庭储备目标）的CVaR，且不以求生存至终点为条件；5) 采用基于全局时间神经网络参数化可行控制策略的数值解法；6) 通过期望成本与风险加载的支付尺度负荷，在代表性合同与book两个层面量化政策引致的配偶延续成本，并刻画大book极限下平均每合同延续成本等于以共同市场信息为条件的期望代表性合同成本。

Result: 基于澳大利亚校准的数值实验显示：仅配偶阶段是一阶且持续的家庭事件；在book层面，分散化在大book极限下消除了代表性合同配偶延续的大部分超额上尾成本，但期望每合同延续成本不变；跨国死亡率比较表明仅配偶阶段的普遍性与持续性并非澳大利亚特有。

Conclusion: spouse-protected tontine通过延迟池转移并将账户绑定于家庭合同，实现了对配偶延续收入的保护，同时维持精算公平与事后预算平衡。大池分散化能显著降低配偶延续的风�险加载成本而不牺牲期望成本，且该设计的适用性具有跨国的普遍意义。

Abstract: We develop a spouse-protected tontine in which a first death changes the household state but generates no pool transfer. The same account remains attached to the household contract until extinction and funds a spouse-only continuation phase if the retiree dies first. We derive contract-level actuarial-fairness conditions and a finite-pool mortality-credit allocation rule with exact ex post budget balance. Under a homogeneous large-pool approximation, we formulate a multidimensional decumulation problem for a representative household, with withdrawal and rebalancing controls while the retiree is alive. The objective balances expected cumulative real household payments against the Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) of terminal shortfalls relative to household reserve targets, without conditioning on survival to the horizon. We develop a numerical solution method for this problem based on a global-in-time neural-network parameterization of admissible control policies. We quantify policy-induced spouse-continuation costs using expected-cost and risk-loaded payment-scale loads at both representative-contract and book levels. We characterize the large-book limit of average per-contract continuation cost as the expected representative-contract cost conditional on common market information.
  Numerical experiments calibrated to Australia show that the spouse-only phase is a first-order and persistent household event. In these experiments, book-level diversification removes most of the representative-contract excess upper-tail cost of spouse continuation in the large-book limit, without changing expected per-contract continuation cost. A cross-country mortality comparison indicates that the prevalence and persistence of the spouse-only phase are not specific to Australia.

</details>
