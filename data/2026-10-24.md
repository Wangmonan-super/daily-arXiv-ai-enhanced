### [1] [Multiple Stopping and Exercise Boundaries for American Put Options](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30637)
*Katsunori Ano*

Main category: q-fin.PR

TL;DR: 研究具有至多 m 次行权权、相邻行权需间隔耐火期 δ>0 且未用权可失效的有限期限美式看跌期权；证明最优行权由单一且按剩余权数有序的行权边界刻画，并给出边界的光滑拟合、自由边界/QVI 形式、提前行权溢价表示与递推 Volterra 型边界方程，最后推广到随机终止时间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典美式看跌期权仅允许一次行权，而实际合约（如带行权限制与冷却期的结构化产品）允许多次行权但要求相邻行权间隔一定时间。此类"有限次数+耐火期"的行权约束破坏了标准美式期权的单一边界结构，需要重新刻画最优行权策略并给出可计算的行权边界方程。

Method: 在风险中性 Black–Scholes 框架下，将问题按剩余行权次数 k=1,…,m 做状态分层。对每层证明最优行权区域为单一边界（单一 hitting time 最优），并给出边界关于剩余权数的单调排序；利用边界在相邻到期时点（deadline）之间所形成开条带内的局部 Lipschitz 连续性，在远离 deadline 处导出光滑拟合（smooth fit）、拟变分不等式与自由边界表述、提前行权溢价（early-exercise premium）分解，以及递推 Volterra 型积分方程；并通过验证定理确认边界的解即为最优策略。进一步将递推结构推广至独立随机终止时间。

Result: (1) 每个剩余行权次数对应单一行权边界，且边界按剩余次数有序；(2) 首次触及边界时间的最优性得到证明；(3) 边界在内部开 deadline 条带上局部 Lipschitz；(4) 在远离 deadline 处成立 smooth fit、QVI 与自由边界等价、提前行权溢价表示及递推 Volterra 边界方程；(5) 验证定理成立；(6) 递推框架可容纳独立随机终止时间（外生终止）。

Conclusion: 带耐火期的有限次数美式看跌期权具有结构化、单边界且可递推计算的最优行权刻画：m 次行权可分解为 m 个相互耦合并按剩余次数有序的自由边界问题，其边界由 Volterra 型递推方程确定，并可通过验证定理严格证明最优性；该框架可自然扩展至含外生随机终止的合约。

Abstract: This article studies a finite-horizon American put option with up to $m$ exercise rights under the risk-neutral Black--Scholes model, where consecutive exercises must be separated by a prescribed refractory period $δ>0$ and unused rights may expire. For each number of remaining rights, we show that optimal exercise is characterized by a single exercise boundary and that these boundaries are ordered by the number of remaining rights. We prove optimality of the first boundary-hitting time and establish local Lipschitz continuity of the exercise boundaries within each open deadline strip. Away from the deadlines, we derive smooth fit, the associated quasi-variational inequality and free-boundary formulation, an early-exercise-premium representation, recursive Volterra-type boundary equations, and a verification theorem. We also extend the recursive formulation to an independent random termination time, thereby allowing for exogenous termination of the option contract.

TL;DR: 利用SHARE六轮十一国数据构建涵盖功能健康、经济不安全与社会排斥的多维剥夺指数，在双向固定效应模型中将其与国家-轮次层面的周期调整基础财政余额（CAPB）相联系，考察财政整顿是否使老年女性剥夺程度甚于男性及其渠道。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Abstract: This paper studies whether fiscal consolidation is associated with deeper multidimensional deprivation among older women than among older men, and through which channel. This regional pattern holds under individual clustering but does not survive clustering at the country level. Further, the economic insecurity channel the literature would predict runs in the opposite direction, with men's measured insecurity rising more steeply than women's; this is the only dimension whose interaction survives country-level inference. Social exclusion exhibits a larger gender effect, concentrated in the Eastern and Southern Europe, but it is significant only under individual clustering. The gendered cost of austerity among older Europeans is therefore neither uniform across welfare regimes nor routed through income, though the evidence for its social channel is inconclusive.

### [3] [The Delegation Divide: An Open Index of AI-Agent Delegability across Occupations and Countries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30752)
*Cristian Espinal Maya,Santiago Jiménez Londoño*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文构建"智能体可委派性指数"(ADI)：用两个语言模型按冻结、预注册的委派层级与障碍量规，对全部18,796条O*NET任务陈述打分，映射到433个ISCO-08单位组并按146国ILOSTAT就业加权。结果：2026年全球35.2%任务时间可部分委派、仅2.5%可完全委派；可委派份额随收入上升（低收入22.5%→高收入48.7%），构成"委派鸿沟"。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有度量主要刻画对生成式AI的"暴露度"，且多为美国样本，缺乏全球可比、可区分委派层级与障碍的指标，无法回答"多少工作可真正交给AI智能体、在哪些国家"。作者据此提出以智能体委派为对象、覆盖146国的统一指数。

Method: 1）量规：预注册、冻结的委派等级与障碍（物理、认知等）评分体系；2）标注：两个LLM对O*NET 30.2全部18,796条任务陈述打分；3）聚合：任务映射至433个ISCO-08单位组，以ILOSTAT 146国就业结构加权；4）效度：与Eloundou et al.(r=0.93)、Felten et al.(r=0.89)的职业层面暴露度做相关；5）稳健性：模型间kappa=0.77，300任务盲评人工kappa=0.85，带检索的智能体小组kappa=0.87；6）机制检验：按人均GDP的对数回归（斜率7.23 pp/log点）、委派与101国实际AI使用率的相关（r=0.86）及中等收入国家差距检验（p=0.27）。

Result: ①2026年全球就业任务时间中35.2%至少可部分委派，仅2.5%可完全委派；②可委派份额随收入单调上升，低收入22.5%、高收入48.7%，斜率7.23个百分点/每对数单位人均GDP，差距主要为构成效应，物理障碍主导低收入国家主体岗位；③ADI与既有暴露度指标高度相关（0.93/0.89，高于预注册0.80阈值），属增量而非重新排序；④完全委派稀少，且集中于人类约11.8分钟（峰值11.8分钟）的任务；⑤预注册的"采用缺口"假设未获支持：101国委派性与实际AI使用相关r=0.86，中等收入国家缺口并不更大（p=0.27）。

Conclusion: ADI将"暴露度"推进为"可委派性"，在全球、职业与任务三层提供可复现的度量，并揭示与收入相关的"委派鸿沟"及其以物理障碍为主的构成性来源；同时否证了"欠采用"假设，说明观测到的AI使用与可委派性基本同步。作者开源指数、图谱、提示词与MCP服务器，供后续复现与政策分析。

Abstract: 35.2% of task time in world employment is at least partly delegable to AI agents in 2026, and only 2.5% can be handed over in full. Existing measures score exposure to generative AI, mostly for the United States. We build the Agent Delegability Index (ADI): two language models rate all 18,796 O*NET 30.2 task statements on a frozen, preregistered rubric of delegation levels and barriers; tasks are mapped to 433 ISCO-08 unit groups and weighted by ILOSTAT employment in 146 countries. Four findings. First, delegable employment rises with income, from 22.5% in low-income to 48.7% in high-income countries, a slope of 7.23 percentage points per log point of GDP per capita. This delegation divide is compositional: the physical barrier dominates the jobs poorer countries employ most. Second, at the occupation level ADI tracks exposure closely (r = 0.93 with Eloundou et al. and 0.89 with Felten et al.), above the preregistered bound of 0.80; ADI adds delegation levels and barriers on top of exposure, for the world, not a new ranking of occupations. Third, full delegation is rare and peaks for tasks that take a human about twelve minutes (11.8 minutes). Fourth, the preregistered adoption gap is not supported: across 101 countries, delegability and observed AI usage correlate at r = 0.86, and the gap between them is not wider in middle-income countries (p = 0.27). Model agreement is kappa = 0.77; a blind human rating of 300 tasks agrees with the model consensus at kappa = 0.85, and an agentic panel with web search at kappa = 0.87. We release the index, the atlas, the prompts and an MCP server at https://github.com/Cespial/delegation-divide.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Incumbents remain protected by complementary assets including distribution, product bundling, switching costs, proprietary data, serving capacity, and contractual trust. The analysis suggests that investors and policy makers should assess risks looking not only at model capability, but also at the ownership and durability of complementary assets across the AI value chain.

### [5] [Directionally Correct Views in Portfolio Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31074)

### [6] [Intraday Forecasting Ability and Liquidity Provision of Institutional Traders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31141)

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 利用交易所高频数据并将买卖执行匹配为持仓头寸，作者发现机构交易员具有持久的预测能力，且预测能力与流动性提供强度均显著提升日内自营盈亏。

  <summary>Details</summary>

We discover persistent forecasting abilities among traders. Our findings indicate a direct correlation between improved forecasting ability and profitability, where a one-standard-deviation increase in the forecasting ability proxy is associated with 1.02 -- 1.19 currency units higher P&L per matched position. Additionally, we measure liquidity-provision intensity within a matched position by its number of passive executions: zero, one, or two. In these regressions, P&L is 1.28 -- 1.31 currency units higher for one passive execution than for none, and a further 3.79 -- 3.82 higher for two than for one, underscoring the significant financial benefits of liquidity provision.
