### [1] [Axient: Canonical Protocol-Graph Composition for Leveraged Event Markets: Single State Authority, Atomic Composition, Durable Sagas, and Exactly-Once Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02834)
*Maksym Nechepurenko*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文提出"规范协议图"，通过为每个金融域设定唯一存储权威并让组合组件协调状态转移，消除模块化杠杆事件市场中"合约各自正确却缺乏统一金融执行路径"的问题，并给出形式化安全证明与可复现证据链。

Motivation: 模块化杠杆事件市场协议中，风险审批、头寸、债务、结算证据、信用池、次级后备、储备、清算、治理等合约可能各自正确，但缺乏一条权威的金融执行路径，导致余额、债务、头寸、证据收据、队列、储备、暂停状态或终局场景状态被重复创建，破坏一致性与损失守恒。因此需要一种统一权威与可组合转移的架构，并配套形式化保证和可复现证据。

Conclusion: 该工作将规范协议图与可复现证据链相连接，同时明确区分已观测的工程结果与未来外部运行假设之间的边界。

Abstract: A modular leveraged event-market protocol can contain individually correct contracts for risk approval, positions, debt, settlement evidence, credit pools, junior backstops, reserves, liquidation, and governance while still lacking one authoritative financial execution path. This paper develops a canonical protocol graph in which every financial domain has one storage authority and composition components coordinate transitions without recreating balances, debt, positions, evidence receipts, queues, reserves, pause state, or terminal scenario state. The architecture formalizes atomic position origination, a settlement-confirmed debt saga, trader residual release, an ordered reserve-backstop-Senior loss waterfall, loss-participating withdrawal queues, capability-conditioned execution, protocol-wide pause and timelocked recovery, deterministic deployment manifests, canonical state projection, and exactly-once reconstruction after a chain-confirmed checkpoint. The implementation is evaluated through twelve parent Financial Interaction Assertions (FIA-001-FIA-012), including mandatory FIA-011A/B capability subcases. The registered local cohort contains twelve uniquely correlated scenario records and 84 retained ACTUAL_EVIDENCE layer positions, two clean materializations with one byte-identical file index, a deterministic strict archive accepted by a separate verifier, and a selected ordered replay bound to that archive. The result connects the formal protocol graph to a reproducible evidence chain while preserving explicit boundaries between the observed engineering result and future external-operation hypotheses.

### [2] [Axient: Manifest-Bound Evidence for On-Chain Financial Protocols: Seven-Layer Derivation, Correlation, Tamper Rejection, and Reproducible Claim Promotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02838)
*Maksym Nechepurenko*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文面向混合链上金融协议，提出"清单绑定证据架构"（manifest-bound evidence architecture）：以密封执行清单绑定系统身份、角色、模式与发布身份，保留七层证据并用公共关联身份链接，配合联合式主张提升规则，从金融主张到可重放证据包形成可审查的证据链。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有对混合链上金融协议的评估证据"单独有用但整体不足"：单元测试、交易回执、截图、服务响应或若干相互匹配的哈希，常被当作完整工作流的证明，即使这些层次共享同一个生成来源，或遗漏了真正关键的金融权限。因此需要一种能防止"复制哈希冒充独立派生"、并可审查地连接金融主张与证据包的架构。

Method: 构建清单绑定证据架构：1）密封执行清单绑定声明的系统身份、角色、模式与发布身份；2）保留七层证据，各层带注册派生关系，并通过公共关联身份相互链接；3）采用联合式（conjunctive）主张提升规则，除哈希相等外还要求溯源、谓词特定根条件、阴性对照、可复现打包、历史重放与独立复核。形式化证据图：证明复制哈希无法建立独立派生，说明抗碰撞承诺可使指定篡改可检测，定义"恰好一次"金融效果证据，并区分原始历史身份与后续可复现性证明。以注册式本地案例研究进行验证。

Result: 案例研究在一个被接受的队列中记录12条父级"金融交互断言"（FIA）：84个保留的 ACTUAL_EVIDENCE 位置、两次干净物化（其中一个字节完全相同的文件索引）、一个被独立验证器接受的确定性严格归档，以及绑定到该归档的选定有序重放。FIA-011为联合式断言，同时保留"准入前已过期"与"持仓期间簿记纪元变更"两个子案例。

Conclusion: 该架构展示了从金融断言到重放绑定包的可审查监管链（chain of custody），同时保持"观测证据、解释与未来假设"三者的区分，为混合链上金融协议提供整体充分且可复核的评估证据。

Abstract: Hybrid on-chain financial protocols are frequently evaluated with evidence that is individually useful but collectively insufficient: a unit test, transaction receipt, screenshot, service response, or several matching hashes may be presented as proof of a complete workflow even when the layers share one generated source or omit the financial authority that matters. This paper develops a manifest-bound evidence architecture for such systems. A sealed execution manifest binds declared system identities, roles, schemas, and release identity. Seven evidence layers are retained with registered derivations and linked by a common correlation identity. A conjunctive claim-promotion rule requires provenance, predicate-specific root conditions, negative controls, reproducible packaging, historical replay, and independent review in addition to hash equality. We formalize the evidence graph, prove that copied hashes cannot establish independent derivation, show how collision-resistant commitments make specified tampering detectable, define exactly-once financial-effect evidence, and distinguish original historical identities from later reproducibility attestations. The registered local case study records twelve parent Financial Interaction Assertions in one accepted cohort: 84 retained ACTUAL_EVIDENCE positions, two clean materializations with one byte-identical file index, a deterministic strict archive accepted by a separate verifier, and a selected ordered replay bound to that archive. FIA-011 is conjunctive and retains both stale-before-admission and book-epoch-change-mid-position subcases. The result demonstrates a reviewable chain of custody from financial assertion to replay-bound package while preserving the difference between observed evidence, interpretation, and future hypotheses.

### [3] [Mixture-of-Experts for Cryptocurrency Order Execution: Training Stability, Tail Risk, and Failure Modes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03369)
*Alexander Ardaiz,Varun Budati,Ali Habibnia*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 该论文通过多种子重复评估，检验专家混合（MoE）架构在订单执行深度强化学习中是否带来真实增益，结果发现专家划分并未提升表现，而是掩盖了训练设定（探索退火等）的失效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 深度强化学习订单执行策略在不同训练随机种子下差异显著，单次训练观察到的架构改进可能只是有利的随机实现，而非架构本身可复现的性质。因此需要重复种子评估来区分真实架构增益与训练设定造成的假象。

Method: 基于 Binance 5 分钟均值聚合 BTC/USDT 限价订单簿数据，对比 vanilla Double DQN（DDQL）、K-means 划分子专家 MoE（K∈{2,4,8}）以及与 K=4、K=8 专家预算参数量匹配的稠密网络。指标为实现差额（implementation shortfall），对照 TWAP 与即时清仓。随后在设备匹配基线上逐一分解训练设定：退火探索、对齐奖励及三者组合，并以 McNemar 检验评估崩塌消除的显著性。

Result: 没有任何学习配置显著优于 DDQL 的平均实现差额；在报告设定下，所有方法均劣于 TWAP（0.39 bps）与即时清仓（0.21 bps）。100 次 vanilla DDQL 中有 11 次收敛到等待被强制清算的策略，而 MoE K≥4 各臂均无此崩塌。仅采用退火探索即将崩塌从 12/100 降至 0/100（McNemar p=4.9×10⁻⁴），与专家划分的消除效果相当；退火探索加对齐奖励使崩塌回升至 19/30，三项设定同时改动则升至 48/100。K=8 在六种设定下均无崩塌。跨种子离散度在 K=8 最低，但呈非单调且经族系校正后不稳健；策略内尾部风险随 K 单调恶化。失败模式的归因在 30 与 100 种子之间发生反转。

Conclusion: 在该环境中，专家划分并非抑制策略崩塌的必要条件，其作用更像是在掩盖训练设定的失效，而非提供内在性能优势。结果强调订单执行 RL 研究必须进行多种子重复评估，否则架构归因可能不可复现甚至方向相反。

Abstract: Deep reinforcement-learning policies for order execution can vary substantially across training seeds, so apparent architectural gains may reflect favourable training realisations rather than reproducible properties of the architecture. We evaluate vanilla Double Deep Q-Learning (DDQL), K-means-partitioned mixtures of DDQL experts at $K \in \{2, 4, 8\}$, and dense networks parameter-matched to the $K{=}4$ and $K{=}8$ expert budgets on 5-minute mean-aggregated BTC/USDT limit order book data from Binance. No learned configuration significantly improves mean implementation shortfall over DDQL. Under the reported specification, all have higher mean shortfall than TWAP (0.39 bps) and immediate liquidation (0.21 bps) in an environment whose frictionless replay and terminal-urgency penalty make early liquidation nearly costless; 11/100 vanilla-DDQL runs, versus none in either MoE $K{\geq}4$ arm, converge to a policy that waits until forced liquidation. We then decompose this specification on a device-matched baseline. Annealed exploration alone eliminates observed collapses (12/100 to 0/100; exact McNemar $p{=}4.9{\times}10^{-4}$), matching the elimination under expert partitioning. Combining annealed exploration with the aligned reward restores collapse in 19/30 runs; with all three specification changes, it rises to 48/100. In this environment, expert partitioning is unnecessary to suppress collapse and appears to mask a training-specification failure rather than confer an intrinsic performance benefit. No MoE $K{=}8$ run collapses under any of the six specifications tested. Across-seed dispersion is lowest at $K{=}8$ but non-monotone and not robust to family-wise adjustment, while within-policy tail risk worsens monotonically with $K$. The apparent attribution of the failure mode reverses between 30 and 100 seeds, illustrating the importance of repeated-seed evaluation.

### [4] [When a Correct Reward Is Not Enough: Diagnosing and Guiding PPO in an Analytically Solved Broker-Trader Game](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03598)
*Siu Tung Wong,Carlo Campajola*

Main category: q-fin.TR

TL;DR: 本文将PPO智能体置于一个已有解析解的连续时间经纪商—交易者博弈中，用连续时间收益构造有限步奖励，系统诊断RL能否利用金融数学中的解析结果，并发现PPO在无信息流时逼近参考动作、在随机信息流下表现不佳，而解析策略更适合作为基准与热启动策略。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 金融最优控制中复杂动态常使解析策略难以获得，但金融数学文献已积累大量有闭式解的模型；作者希望检验强化学习能否利用这些解析解来评估、引导和加速学习。

Method: 采用PPO替代经纪商，在解析可解的连续时间经纪商—交易者博弈中选择交易速度，并与知情交易者和随机不知情订单流交互；由连续时间收益推导有限步奖励，通过网格细化和精确一步恒等式验证离散实现；比较PPO-FFNN与PPO-LSTM，并配合监督学习、蒙特卡洛评论家诊断、势函数奖励塑形，以及在部分信息下与因果确定性等价控制器对照；最后冻结解析策略并训练PPO在成本变化后调整。

Result: 无不知情流时，经验证集选择的PPO-FFNN接近参考动作；有随机不知情流时，PPO-FFNN与PPO-LSTM均不准确，尽管监督学习表明其actor能表示该动作，蒙特卡洛诊断显示critic无法可靠排序邻近动作，势函数奖励塑形也无稳定改进；部分信息下因果确定性等价控制器接近参考，而PPO误差更大、收益更低；成本减半后，以解析策略为初始策略训练PPO可重复改进，缩小到变更成本参考的2.22%差距。

Conclusion: 解析解既是诊断RL失效原因的基准，也可作为适应环境变化的有用初始策略；RL在此类金融控制问题中尚不能可靠替代或自主发现解析策略。

Abstract: Reinforcement learning (RL) is increasingly used for financial optimal-control problems when complex dynamics make analytical strategies difficult to obtain. There are financial mathematics literactures which provides many solved models whose equations and controls could evaluate and guide learning; we ask whether RL can exploit these results.
  We place a proximal policy optimisation (PPO) agent in an analytically solved continuous-time broker--trader game. PPO replaces the broker and chooses its trading speed while interacting with an informed trader and stochastic uninformed order flow. We derive a finite-step reward from the broker's continuous-time payoff and verify its discrete implementation through grid refinement and an exact one-step identity. With zero uninformed flow, a validation-selected PPO--FFNN approaches the reference action. With stochastic uninformed flow, the tested PPO--FFNN and PPO--LSTM remain inaccurate, although supervised learning confirms that their actors can represent the action. Monte Carlo diagnostics show that their critics do not reliably rank nearby actions; potential-based reward shaping also gives no reliable improvement. Under partial information, a causal certainty-equivalent controller based on the broker's observable history remains close to the reference, while PPO has larger errors and lower payoffs.
  Finally, we freeze the analytical policy and train PPO to adjust it after the execution cost changes. Halving the cost yields a repeatable improvement that closes \(2.22\%\) of the gap to the changed-cost reference. The analytical solution therefore provides both a benchmark for diagnosing RL and a useful starting policy for adaptation.

  <summary>Details</summary>
