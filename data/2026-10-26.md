### [1] [When AI Gets Cheaper, Do Prices Follow? Information Delays and Algorithmic Pricing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31693)
*Krish Tadigotla*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 该文用双寡头模拟市场（logit需求 + 表格Q学习）检验AI推理成本下降是否传导为价格下降，以及竞争者价格信息时滞与调价频率限制如何影响消费者剩余，结论是成本节约未必完全传导，且学习型定价下消费者剩余普遍低于静态Nash基准。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AI模型运行成本下降被期待带来更低价格，但若企业价格信息观测存在时滞、调价频率受限，成本红利的消费者传导程度可能被削弱；作者希望量化"信息时滞×调价频率"对消费者剩余的交互作用，并考察成本下降机制与企业异质性的调节作用。

<details>

Abstract: We study a model in which artificial intelligence lowers the cost of writing faster than the cost of reading. As writing costs fall, the band of verified papers shrinks: outsiders are no longer read, and established authors are no longer checked. In the ``costless writing limit,'' the share of verified papers vanishes, and a paper's impact depends on its author's reputation alone. Result: 最优终端财富由两部分组成：在监管阈值处的常数额富区域与Merton型投资区域的组合。与单一阈值VaR约束不同，该表述可同时保护多个阈值；两阈值情形下可识别"同时保护两阈值"与"保护集中于较大阈值"的条件。数值结果显示：当合规概率要求高、较小财富阈值仍较高或初始资本有限时，监管约束显著重塑最优收益结构并造成较大福利损失。异质投资者池中的福利损失强烈依赖风险厌恶构成与Pareto权重；高风厌投资者占主导时损失尤为显著。资本贡献较小的投资者可能承担不成比例的大额福利损失份额，而较大出资者承担的份额可能低于其资本比例。

Conclusion: 监管不确定性不仅降低总体福利，还会在投资者之间造成显著的成本分配差异；多阈值VaR约束提供了对多个监管门槛的保护，但其代价及分配效应取决于合规概率要求、初始资本、投资者风险偏好构成与风险分担权重。

Abstract: We study optimal collective investment subject to a Value-at-Risk (VaR)-type constraint that accounts for uncertainty about future regulatory requirements. The constraint incorporates several possible wealth thresholds and imposes a minimum weighted average probability of meeting them. We characterize the optimal terminal wealth, which combines regions of constant wealth at regulatory thresholds with Merton-type investment regions. Unlike a classical VaR constraint with only one threshold, our formulation generates protection against several thresholds. For two possible thresholds, we identify when both are protected and when protection concentrates on the larger one. Numerical results show that the regulatory constraint can substantially reshape the optimal payoff and generate large welfare losses relative to unconstrained investment when the required compliance probability is high, even the smaller wealth threshold is high, or initial capital is limited. In heterogeneous investor pools, welfare losses depend strongly on the composition of the risk aversions and the Pareto weights governing risk sharing. Losses are particularly pronounced when highly risk-averse investors dominate the pool. Moreover, investors with smaller capital contributions can bear disproportionately large shares of the total welfare loss, while larger contributors may bear less than their capital-proportional share. Thus, regulatory uncertainty not only reduces aggregate welfare but can also create substantial disparities in how its costs are distributed among investors.
