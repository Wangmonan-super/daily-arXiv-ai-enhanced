### [1] [Disaffection at Work: Employee Responses to Job-Related Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27538)
*Beatrice Braut,Mariele Macaluso,Vincenzo Mollisi*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本文通过随机调查实验（意大利和法国代表性员工样本），研究不同职场叙事如何影响员工的"安静离职"行为。结果发现，强调社会正义与集体权利的道德框架会增加员工的疏离感但减少被动脱离；强调工作组织与雇佣实践的框架则无系统性影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 安静离职（quiet quitting）是一种在劳动力供给的集约边际上运作的劳动者不满形式，而非通过离开工作岗位表达。作者希望探究不同的职场叙事（narratives）如何塑造劳动者的行为反应，从而理解非货币因素对工作投入度的影响机制。

Method: 在意大利和法国的代表性员工样本上实施随机调查实验（randomized survey experiment）。参与者被随机分配到两种基于实证的道德叙事框架之一：一种强调社会正义与集体权利（justice-oriented），另一种强调工作组织与雇佣实践（organization-centered），随后测量其行为反应。

Result: 道德叙事会在不同行为边际上重新分配劳动者的行为：正义导向的叙事增加了疏离感（detachment），同时减少了被动性脱离（passive disengagement）；而以组织为中心的框架没有产生系统性效应。

Conclusion: 职场叙事（尤其是涉及社会正义与集体权利的道德框架）能够显著影响员工在安静离职这种"边际内退出"行为上的选择，表明道德认知因素在塑造劳动供给行为中具有重要作用，且不同叙事框架的效果具有不对称性。

Abstract: Quiet quitting reflects a form of worker disaffection that operates along the intensive margin of labor supply rather than through job exit. We study how alternative workplace narratives shape workers' behavioral responses using a randomized survey experiment on a representative sample of employees in Italy and France. Respondents are exposed to empirically grounded moral framings of work emphasizing either social justice and collective rights or work organization and employment practices. We find that moral framings reallocate behavior across margins: justice-oriented narratives increase detachment while reducing passive disengagement, whereas organization-centered framing generates no systematic effects.

### [2] [Do Customer Disclosures Affect Suppliers' Internal Capital Allocation Decisions?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27598)
*Sangwook Nam*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 本研究考察了客户披露是否影响供应商企业跨业务部门的资本配置。利用SFAS 131作为客户层面披露冲击，发现供应商在面临更充分的客户披露时，会经历更激烈的产品市场竞争，并将资本重新配置到成长机会信号相对较弱的部门，以维护客户关系（竞争威胁渠道），这些投资虽然降低了ROA，但有助于供应商保住市场份额并扩大客户基础。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究客户披露如何通过信息渠道（帮助供应商将资本与增长机会对齐）与竞争威胁渠道（削弱现有供应商的私有信息优势，诱发防御性投资）这两条竞争性渠道来影响供应商的内部资本配置决策。

Method: 以SFAS 131的采纳作为客户层面披露的外生冲击；使用一种新颖的方法将供应商部门与客户部门进行关联；检验供应商资本配置对客户披露的反应及其对市场份额、客户基础、ROA等结果变量的影响。

Result: 供应商在客户披露扩大的环境下会重新配置资本至成长信号较弱的部门；这种偏离预测配置的行为有助于供应商保持市场份额并扩大客户基础；调整由投资产能驱动，而非纠正先前投资不足；对于客户更大及行业更集中的部门，投资调整更强；这些部门后续ROA更低。

Conclusion: 披露可以塑造经济关联企业内部的资本配置方式，且竞争威胁渠道在解释供应商投资行为方面起主导作用——供应商以接受更低盈利为代价来捍卫客户关系。

Abstract: This study examines whether customer disclosures affect how supplier firms allocate capital across business segments. Customer disclosures can shape supplier investment decisions through two competing channels. They can improve suppliers' information about downstream demand, helping suppliers align capital with growth opportunities ("information channel"), or erode incumbent suppliers' private information advantage, inducing costly investments to defend customer relationships ("competitive-threat channel"). I use the adoption of SFAS 131 as a customer-level disclosure shock. Suppliers exposed to expanded customer disclosures experience increased product-market competition and reallocate capital toward segments with relatively weak growth-opportunity signals. Suppliers that deviate from allocations predicted by growth signals are more likely to preserve market share and expand their customer base in subsequent years. Using a novel approach to link supplier segments to customer segments, I show that this reallocation is driven by investing in capacity in affected segments, rather than by a correction of prior under-investment. Consistent with the competitive-threat channel, the investment adjustment is stronger for segments linked to larger customers and for segments operating in more concentrated industries. Segments making these investments subsequently experience lower ROA, consistent with suppliers accepting lower profitability to defend customer relationships. Overall, the findings show that disclosures can shape how economically linked firms allocate capital internally.

### [3] [The Race for Elite Destinations: Education Competition and Low Fertility in Korea](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27980)
*Dongwoo Kim*

Main category: econ.GN

TL;DR: 韩国生育率全球最低，教育竞争激烈，家庭将9%的终身收入投入教育（多为私人补习但回报近乎为零）。本文提出竞争性职业导致"分配外部性"：所有家庭增加教育投入时，录取门槛水涨船高，孩子变得昂贵。在据此校准的量化模型中，用保持容量的抽签替代分数分配可使每对夫妇的生育率提高0.24个孩子；生育补贴仅能实现该增幅的八分之一，教育税几乎无效。跨代际的生育率下降主要由偏好转变而非更激烈的竞争驱动。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 韩国面临全球最低生育率与极端教育内卷并存的现象，家庭投入大量资源于回报近乎为零的私人补习，需解释这一悖论及其与低生育率之间的因果机制。

Method: 提出"分配外部性"理论（教育投入竞争导致录取门槛上升），构建校准到该机制的量化模型，对比分数分配与容量保持抽签两种分配制度下生育率的差异，并模拟生育补贴和教育税的政策效果。

Result: 用抽签替代分数分配可使生育率提高0.24个孩子/夫妇；生育补贴仅带来该增量八分之一的改善；教育税几乎无效；跨代际生育率下降的主因是偏好转变而非教育竞争加剧。

Conclusion: 教育竞争的分配外部性是低生育率的重要驱动因素，改变选拔机制（如抽签分配）比传统激励政策（补贴、税收）更有效；长期生育率下降应归因于偏好变迁。

Abstract: South Korea has the world's lowest fertility and an intense education race. Families devote nine percent of lifetime income to education, mostly to private tutoring with near-zero measured returns. I show that competition for coveted careers generates an assignment externality: when all families spend more, the admission bar rises and children become costly. In a quantitative model calibrated to this mechanism, replacing score-based assignment with a capacity-preserving lottery raises completed fertility by 0.24 children per couple. Pronatal transfers deliver one eighth of that increase. Education taxes deliver almost none. Preference shifts, not a fiercer race, explain the decline across cohorts.

</details>
